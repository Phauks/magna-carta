**Legal Citations.** A new tool formats legal citations in the Uniform System of Citation (from the
public-domain Indigo Book), with Nevada and California formats. Paste a link to a statute,
regulation or opinion, or a rough citation such as "347 us 483", and the details are read for you;
anything you type overrides them. It gives the full form, the short form and the "Id." form, each
ready to copy.
