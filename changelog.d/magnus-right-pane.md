**The Magnus right pane: Context, Permissions and Artifacts.** A pane beside the open conversation
has three tabs. Context lists the files attached to the conversation. Permissions says what Magnus
may read, per conversation: the attached files of This Matter, and a switch, off by default, for the
member's own other conversations on the same matter (audited by name when turned on or off).
Artifacts lists the documents Magnus wrote in the conversation, each with its versions: Magnus writes
a draft as a document, revising it adds a version, and a member can step between versions, see what
changed, search a version, copy or download it, and put an earlier version back as the newest. The
disclosure moves to version 3, which members accept before their next question.

## Public

Magnus can now write drafts as documents you keep in the conversation, with every version, and can
read your own earlier conversations on the same matter when you turn that on.
