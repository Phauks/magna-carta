**Segmented controls are fully rounded.** The track and the chosen tile of `SegmentedControl` (`packages/design-system/src/lib/components/segmented-control/segmented-control.css`) both take the full radius, replacing a square tile inside a rounded track. Every two-to-four-choice toggle changes with it, including the Magnus sort and pane sections.

## Public

Toggles with two to four choices, such as the Date, Name and Matter # sort in Magnus, are now fully rounded.
