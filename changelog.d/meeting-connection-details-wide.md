**Meeting Connection Details is wide and short instead of narrow and tall.** The popover takes the record-pane width (capped to the window) and at most 70% of the window's height, in two columns that scroll on their own: this browser's numbers and Copy Diagnostics on the left, the people and the wire events on the right.

## Public

**Connection Details in a meeting is easier to read.** It now opens as a wider panel in two columns (your own connection on the left, everyone else and recent events on the right) instead of a narrow strip running off the screen.
