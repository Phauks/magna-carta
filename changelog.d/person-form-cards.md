**Each part of a person's record is a card you can fold away.** On New Person and in a record's window, every section sits in its own card with its name and how many entries it holds at the top; press the header to fold it, and choose it in the list on the left to open it again. Beside New Person, the Directory check now says plainly whether anyone similar is already recorded.
