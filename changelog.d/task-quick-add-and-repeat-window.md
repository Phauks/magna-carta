**Add a task straight from the Tasks list, and set a repeat in its own window.** Every group in the
Tasks list now ends in an Add a task line: type what to do and press Enter, and the task lands in
that group's list, status, person, matter or day, with the line left open for the next one. Making a
task repeat now opens a window of its own, and once a task repeats it shows the rule as a sentence
with a Change Repeat button, so the series cannot be changed by a stray click.
