**Translate screen and Translate line.** A `/translate` tool (rail cluster Language) puts pasted text
beside its Google Cloud Translation, paragraph by paragraph, and `translate-line.svelte` drops a
Translate button under a text message, a saved note and an email in the File Workbench. Both show
"Powered by Google Translate" in place of a notice. The `tools:translation` role permission is the
only gate; nothing of the text is stored. The firm switch `translation.enabled` that the Google engine
shipped behind is removed, with its settings route and contract types: the owner ruled that
translation is its own service, not an AI feature.

## Public

**Translate.** A new Translate screen, under Tools, puts pasted text beside its translation, paragraph
by paragraph, in any language Google Translate offers. A Translate button under a text message, a
saved note and an email in the File Workbench shows the translation right there. Every translation
says it is powered by Google Translate. Magna Carta keeps no copy of the text, and every role can
translate unless an administrator takes it away.
