Four more panels open in `ActionDialog`, and two of them are the first callers of the seams the shell just gained.

Move to Another Ledger hands the shell `commitSoon` with the dependency the move waits on, so the shell wraps its own commit in the Soon affordance and the panel no longer composes one around a commit of its own. New Person / New Organization lays its fields out with `LabelledField` and `FieldRow`, which mint the ids and wire the labels; its "Checking the firm's record" hint moves from the bar into the body. Fold One Record Into The Other is a `destructive` kind — the alertdialog role, no incidental dismiss, the commit in the colour of an act that takes something away — and states why its commit is refused until the acknowledgement is ticked, where it used to be silently disabled. Answer This Hit puts its rationale and the waiver's pickers in labelled fields and its "recorded as a new row" line in the body.

Four allowances leave the registry; fifteen remain, eleven of them named exclusions. Two wording changes: "Record the answer" is "Record the Answer", and the commit icons (the arrows, the merge glyph) are gone, as they went from billing.
