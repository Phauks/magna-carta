Problem reports can now be read and answered on the server. Firm admins list and read the reports
their members send and answer each one with a status (Received, Investigating, Fixed, Closed) and an
optional note, which is sealed under the firm key. The sender reads their own reports, hears when a
report is received and when it is answered, and can attach one screenshot to a report. The screens
for this follow once the layouts are picked. The Error ID lookup now also shows background-work
records instead of refusing them.
