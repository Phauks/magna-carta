**`FieldLabel` and `Label` share one voice.** `FieldLabel` drops the extra-small tracked capitals for the form label's type (small, medium weight, `text-muted`, sentence case), and `Label` moves from `text-default` to `text-muted`, so the label over a read-only fact and the label over a field read alike.

## Public

**Labels read the same everywhere.** The small labels over a record's details used to be tiny capital letters, while the labels in forms were ordinary words. They now match, so a detail looks the same on the record and in the window where you edit it.
