**`SegmentedControl`, for two to four choices.** A new design-system part (`@magna-carta/design-system/segmented-control`) on Bits RadioGroup: every option in one track, the chosen one raised with the control edge. The Billing field in the time, cost and fee windows uses it in place of a Select or Combobox, on a row of its own. The date field's label now shares the muted label voice.

## Public

**Billing choices are all on screen.** When you log time or record a cost or a fee, the billing choices sit side by side and you pick one with a single click, instead of opening a list.
