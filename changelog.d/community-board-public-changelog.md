**The public changelog lives on the community board.** `community.magnacarta.esq/changelog` shows
the customer changelog (`docs/public-changelog.md`, built into the bundle) to anyone, signed in or
not, as a place in the board's top bar, and the same page answers at `changelog.magnacarta.esq`, a second
custom domain on the board's worker and the one address of it that search engines may list. The top bar's Changelog used to point at
`magnacarta.esq/changelog`, which was never deployed. The application's own Changelog screen
(`/updates`, every merged pull request) is unchanged.
