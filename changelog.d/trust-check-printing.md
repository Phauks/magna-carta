**Trust checks print from the app.** A trust payment recorded as paid by check, with no number typed in, waits on the account's new Checks screen. Printing numbers each check from the account's next number and makes a PDF in the browser, with every field placed where the firm's own check paper wants it. Check stock is set up per account over a scan of one blank check: fields start at the standard spots for the chosen page layout and can be dragged into place, nothing may be placed in the magnetic-ink strip at the bottom, and a test page prints the boxes for holding against a real check. After printing, each check is confirmed printed or marked spoiled; a spoiled or voided check keeps its number and its payment returns to the print list. A printed check's number is what the bank statement is matched on during reconciliation. Design: `docs/specs/2026-11-14-trust-check-printing-design.md`.

## Public

**Print trust checks.** A trust payment made by check now waits on the account's Checks screen to be printed. Set up your check paper once over a scan of a blank check, drag each field to where your paper wants it, and print a test page to hold against a real check. Checks are numbered from your next check number, and a check that jams is voided with its number kept and printed again on the next.
