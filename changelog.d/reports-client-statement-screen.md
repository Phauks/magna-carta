**The Client Statement of Account runs on the report screen.** The registry declares it with a
client scope (`client: true`), and the run screen draws a Client field from the directory's picker,
with nothing to add from it, beside the period: the owner's pick A of 2026-11-19. The client rides
the address as the scope's subject, and a link opened cold reads the client's name back from the
directory.

## Public

**You can now run a Client Statement of Account.** Choose Reports, then Client Statement of
Account, and pick the client in the Client field beside the period. The statement shows what the
client owed at the start, each bill and payment with the balance after it, what they owed at the
end, and the bills still unpaid.
