**`pnpm run lint:modal-headers` — the scan that reads a panel's two lines.** The sibling of `lint:screen-headers`, built the same way: pure rules in `modal-headers.mjs`, the filesystem in `index.mjs`, fixtures beside both.

Every rule it asserts was already written down — in the shells' own prop documentation, and in the owner's rulings of 2026-09-08 — and none of it held. A rule recorded in a ledger binds the people who read the ledger; a rule with a gate binds the diff.

- **`heading-act`** — an acting panel's heading names the act in the imperative. A panel that makes a record says "New &lt;Noun&gt;"; a reading panel and a record window name the thing they show, and are asked nothing. The verb vocabulary is a closed set, so a new act is one reviewed line rather than a noun quietly becoming a heading.
- **`heading-question`** — no heading ends in a question mark. The panel is what makes it a question: a destructive kind already takes the `alertdialog` role and traps the decision.
- **`heading-case`** — interior articles and short prepositions stay lower case.
- **`subject-not-prose`** — no fact, and no subtitle snippet, is sentence-shaped.
- **`reference-shown`** — a panel handed a record's number as a prop names it in its heading or its subtitle. The registry of what counts as a record number is read out of the schema's own `*_number` columns, so a record kind that grows a number is asked this question the day its column lands; the eleven columns that are numbers of something else — a telephone, a vehicle, a parcel of land, a row in an import — are recorded with the reason each is not a record's own name. The scan exits non-zero if it can no longer parse the schema, because a rule that finds nothing passes vacuously.
- **`allowance`** — every recorded allowance still describes a real violation, on `lint:one-ui`'s discipline. The list is empty today, which is the honest state: the nine violations the first run found were fixed rather than recorded.
