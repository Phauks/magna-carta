**Address suggestions are easier to scan, saved addresses open in Google Maps, and ZIP+4 is kept.** Each offered address now shows a pin, the street on its first line with the part you typed in bold, and the town, state and country beneath it in grey. Every saved address on a Directory record has a map button that opens it in Google Maps in a new window. A ZIP+4 typed as nine digits gains its hyphen as you type (`89501-2245`), and choosing a Google suggestion fills in the full ZIP+4 when Google has the last four digits.

## Public

When you type an address, each suggestion now shows the street with the part you typed in bold and the town and state underneath, so the right one is easier to spot. Saved addresses have a map button that opens them in Google Maps, and a nine-digit ZIP+4 gets its hyphen as you type and is kept in full.
