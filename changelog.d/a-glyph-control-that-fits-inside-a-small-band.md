`icon-sm` — the Button's square at the small control height.

The icon size is a square at the medium height, which is right for a toolbar and four points too tall for a band that already holds one line at the small scale: a table's counts strip, a card's own header. A glyph control joining such a band had to either grow it or be styled off the recipe. This is the size that lets it join without doing either.
