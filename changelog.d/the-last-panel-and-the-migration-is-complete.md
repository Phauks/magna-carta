Add a Document opens in `ActionDialog`, and with it every panel in the migration's scope is on one of the two shells.

Its two paths — from a file, from a template — share the shell's one commit, whose verb follows the open path ("Add Document", "Build Document") and refuses with the reason until that path has what it needs. A document that landed, whether created, rejected by the scan, or still being checked, leaves the panel `done` with the outcome in place of the form; a request that never reached the server, or a problem the server stated, is a refusal with the reason in the notice band and the form still in hand.

Apply Trust Funds, excluded while the Soon affordance lived in the app, is a plain form with `commitSoon` now that the shell carries the affordance; its dependency reads on the commit's hover and to a screen reader rather than as a line beneath it.

Twenty-seven panels migrated across six pull requests. Ten `hand-composed-modal` allowances remain in `tooling/lint-one-ui/registry.json`, every one a named exclusion: the command palette (a launcher), the four directory panes (panes, not modals), the settings ledger (a Sheet), the three full-screen surfaces (a separate design), and the fee-arrangement window (`RecordWindow`'s shape).
