**The gate's test battery runs only when asked for.** The node shards, the Miniflare shards and the story suite are the bulk of the Actions bill and the whole of a pull request's wall-clock, and the node shards outgrew the 1-vCPU runner's fifteen-minute cap twice in one day. By the owner's ruling a pull request now runs them only under the `run-tests` label (attaching it re-runs the gate with the battery); a manual dispatch asks with its `test_battery` input; a queued merge always asks. The checks stay on every pull request. The branch's own full `pnpm test` is the standing merge bar, stated in the pull request. The node battery is also six shards rather than four. `docs/conventions/build-performance.md` carries the ruling and the trade.
