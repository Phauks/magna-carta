**Magnus uses the shared SegmentedControl.** The rail's conversation order (Date, Name, Matter #) and the pane's sections (Context, Permissions, Artifacts) were two hand-rolled toggles with their own markup; both are now `SegmentedControl` from `@magna-carta/design-system/segmented-control`, so they share one look, one tab stop and arrow-key movement.

## Public

**One switch style on the Magnus page.** The conversation order in the list and the Context, Permissions and Artifacts switch beside the conversation now use the same switch as the rest of the app, with the chosen option raised and outlined.
