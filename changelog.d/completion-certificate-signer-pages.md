**No signer's record on the Certificate of Completion runs across two pages.** The signer details are laid out two signers to a page and the audit log is filled a page at a time by the height its rows are expected to take (`SIGNERS_PER_PAGE` and `auditPages` in `packages/signing/src/certificate-sheet.ts`), and every page of a section that runs past one carries its heading and its place, as `Signer Details (1 of 3)`. The two Contact lines are labelled `Document` and `Certificate of Completion`. The document hash prints as two lines of 32 characters, each in eight groups of four set on a grid, so the groups of one line stand under those of the other. Every page is headed with a letterhead (a bar in the accent colour, the lockup, the certificate's name over its reference) in place of the lockup and title that opened only the first page, and the footer is the page number alone, centred under a rule. The PDF composer (`crates/pdf-compose-core`) gains the two things this needs, each off unless a document asks: a `letterhead` drawn in the top margin of every page, and `grid` on a field, which sets the value's words in columns of one width. The audit log gives the name and the address columns more of the width, so an IPv4 address no longer wraps.
