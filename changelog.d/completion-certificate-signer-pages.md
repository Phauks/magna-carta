**No signer's record on the Certificate of Completion runs across two pages.** The signer details are laid out two signers to a page and the audit log is filled a page at a time by the height its rows are expected to take (`SIGNERS_PER_PAGE` and `auditPages` in `packages/signing/src/certificate-sheet.ts`), and every page of a section that runs past one carries its heading and its place, as `Signer Details (1 of 3)`. The two Contact lines are labelled `Document` and `Certificate of Completion`. The document hash prints as two lines of 32 characters, each in eight groups of four set on a grid, so the groups of one line stand under those of the other. Every page is headed with a letterhead (a bar in the accent colour, the lockup, the certificate's name over its reference) in place of the lockup and title that opened only the first page, and the footer is the page number alone, centred under a rule. The PDF composer (`crates/pdf-compose-core`) gains the two things this needs, each off unless a document asks: a `letterhead` drawn in the top margin of every page, and `grid` on a field, which sets the value's words in columns of one width. The audit log gives the name and the address columns more of the width, so an IPv4 address no longer wraps.

**The certificate is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible and says where each signer signed from.** The sheet names its typeface (`typeface` on the composed document), and the composer bundles Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (SIL Open Font License 1.1) beside Liberation Sans. Atkinson draws Latin only, so the sign Worker reads the composer's `missingCharacters` and sets the sheet again in Liberation Sans when a name is in Greek or Cyrillic. The seal route records the city, region and country the network gives for the connecting address as `SignerAttribution.location` (server-observed, like the address itself, and absent where the runtime gives none); the certificate prints it under the address as `Location (by IP Address)`. Page one gains a `Documents` section (name, pages, hash) between the summary and the signers; the summary drops its `Time Zone` line, since every time names its zone, and its `Reference` line, since the letterhead of every page carries the reference, now with a small label beside it. The signers table and the audit log are banded (`banded` on a composed table: a shaded heading row and every second row, no rule under each row), a rule parts two signers who share a page, and the audit log's consent step reads `Agreed to E-Sign` so the event column does not wrap.

**The certificate's sections are cards, its margins are one measure, and it can be laid out for A4.** Summary, Documents, Signers, Contact, each signer's record and each page of the audit log are drawn as cards: a rounded frame with the section's name inside it over a rule. The summary is set in two columns. Every edge of the sheet is half an inch from the paper's edge on all four sides (the sides were an inch, the bar 32 points and the page number 27). `certificateSheet` takes `paper: "letter" | "a4"` and fills the audit log's pages for the paper's height and width; the seal path issues Letter until a firm can choose. The lockup in the letterhead is the mark and wordmark on the paper itself, with no dark tile behind the mark. The composer gains `cardStart` and `cardEnd` blocks, `twoUp` on a fields block, and, under a letterhead, a footer measured from the content (`Letterhead::FOOTER_HEIGHT`) so a caller can place it from the paper's edge.

**Each signer's record carries their signature, and the cards are quieter.** A caller of the seal route may send a signer's signature as a picture, and the certificate prints it at the foot of that signer's record at one height. A picture that cannot be read is left off and the certificate is still issued. Where a one-time code was sent is printed as `Code Sent To` when the caller gives `attribution.codeDestination`; the code itself is never printed. The first card is named `Request Details`, its counts read `Total Signatures` and `Total Initials`, and a signer's `Signature` line reads `Signature Type`. A card's name now sits in a bar in the accent colour with gently rounded top corners, its frame is a pale hairline, column headings are gray text with no shade, and every row is ruled off from the next with a pale rule that is never doubled against the frame.

**Initials print beside the signature, and the certificate names a support address.** The seal route accepts `signatureImages` and `initialsImages` on a signer, each a list of pictures with the pages it was placed on (`SealRequest.signatureImages` and `initialsImages` to the sign Worker), and the signer's record closes with them two to a line, each under a small label. A signer who used more than one signature has every one printed, numbered and with its pages, as `Signature 1 of 2 (Pages 1-3)`; a signer with more pictures than fit beside a second record has a page to themselves. The composer's image block gains `label`, a small bold line above the picture, and `beside`, which sets a picture on the line of the image block before it. The sign Worker's `CERTIFICATE_SUPPORT_EMAIL` is set in its `wrangler.jsonc`, so the Contact card gives an address for questions about the certificate itself.
