**A record's picture opens to look at, its Contact card edits all at once, and relationships are grouped.** In the Directory record window, pressing the picture now opens it large over the record (a `Dialog` in `entity-photo.svelte`) with Edit Photo and Download, instead of a Change, View or Remove menu, and View Full Size no longer opens a new tab. Edit Photo opens the cropping window on the current picture, where it can be re-centred, replaced or removed. The cropper takes JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, BMP and SVG up to 20 MB (`PHOTO_FORMATS` in `photo-crop.ts`), names them on a `Dropzone` and in the picker's `accept`, and marks a refused file with the Dropzone's new `invalid` state. Also: the Preferred star on a contact line is a toggle, and jumping to a record's last section scrolls only the content pane, where `scrollIntoView` used to lift the whole window out of its frame. The record's Contact card (`contact-card-editor.svelte`) is a plain list behind one Edit that opens every line at once, with Preferred and Do Not Use as two-way switches and one Save turned into single writes by `contact-edit-plan.ts`. The relationship list is grouped under headings (`EDGE_TYPE_GROUPS`), A to Z within each, and seven family edge types are added (`spouse_of`, `parent_of`, `child_of`, `sibling_of`, `grandparent_of`, `grandchild_of`, `family_member_of`), classed `family` for conflicts traversal; no migration, as `edge_type` is an open catalog.

## Public

**Looking at a client's photo no longer starts editing it, and contact details are easier to change.** Press a photo in a Directory record to see it large on the same screen; press Edit Photo to change or remove it, all in one window. Photos can now be JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, BMP or SVG, and the window lists what it takes. The Preferred star on an email, number or address can now be turned off again, and jumping to the last section of a record no longer leaves you unable to scroll back up. To change a record's email addresses and numbers, press Edit on its Contact section to change every line at once and save it in one go. The Relationship list is now in alphabetical order under headings, with family ties such as Parent, Child and Spouse in a Family group of their own.
