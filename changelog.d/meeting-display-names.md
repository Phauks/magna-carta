**Change your name in a meeting.** Press the pencil on your own row under People to go by another name for this meeting, such as the room you are in. Everybody else sees the name you joined with beside it, the meeting's record keeps your account's name, and Use My Account Name puts it back in one press. The room holds the chosen name through a reconnect.

## Public

You can change the name you go by in a meeting from your own row under People. Everybody still sees the name you joined with beside it, and one press puts your account name back.
