**The Certificate of Completion reads as a finished document.** The sheet opens with the product's lockup and a title in the application's dark gold, with rules in the same colour; the summary, the signers and the audit log each start a page of their own. The reference is now a number a person can read out, `MC-` and the first twelve characters of the document hash in three groups (`certificateReference` in `packages/signing/src/certificate.ts`, carried on the record as `reference`), where it was the request's internal identifier; that identifier stays in the record and the plain text as `Record` and is no longer printed on the sheet, and neither is a signer's internal identifier. The sender's name and address are separate rows, labels are in title case, the footer reads `Certificate MC-...` beside the page number, and the sheet ends its first page with who to ask about the document: `SigningEnvelope.contact` (a name with an address, a telephone number or both) or, without one, the sender. Two lines are gone: the note that times are as the server observed them, and the paragraph explaining the document hash. To draw this, the PDF composer (`crates/pdf-compose-core`) learned one thing: a document may name an `accent` colour, which its title, first two heading levels and rules are drawn in; a document that names none is written in black and gray exactly as before. The lockup is a picture (`packages/signing/src/brand-lockup.ts`) rendered once from the design system's `BrandMark`, because a page cannot load the blackletter web font.
