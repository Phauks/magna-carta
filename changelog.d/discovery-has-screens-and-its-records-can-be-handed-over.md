**The surface over slice 5·1, which was built end to end and completely invisible.** Three addresses, one per reader: `matters/[id]/discovery` (the section, entry only), `/discovery` (the register, scoped by the per-review-set access list rather than by matter access), and `/discovery/review-sets/[id]` (the workspace, outside the matter chrome — the matter header mounts a consolidated search on every section by construction, and that search must never reach a corpus another party produced).

Two registries carry the growth, both validated at module load: `criteria.ts` declares every narrowing (five served, four rendered visible and disabled with what each waits on and which read it will compose into), and `deliverables.ts` declares every record the corpus produces (two written, three declared). Under both, **a selection is a criteria set and never a list of identifiers**, which is what makes bulk coding over search results compose the day keyword narrowing lights up rather than being built then. A panel registry was deliberately not written — nothing renders it yet, and a registry no screen reads is scaffolding.

Two backend additions. `readVisibleReviewSets` is the matter-scoped builder without its matter clause, registered in the visibility fitness pass in the same commit, because the wall is the access list and a register scoped by matter access would hide from a contract reviewer exactly the work they were given. And `GET /api/v1/loads/{loadId}/artifacts/{artifact}` presigns the manifest or the exception report in either form, audited as an export — the two records the ingest lane exists to produce were previously reachable only as R2 keys on a row.

Recorded rather than assumed: the last-member revoke refusal that the feature ledger and the 5·2 design both list as an open owner question **is built**. The residual hole is on a different verb — the access predicate matches `member_user_id` with no active check while the platform's member reads treat a deactivated member as absent, so deactivating the one member on a review set's list makes the corpus permanently unreachable with no route refusing anything. The guard counts rows, not people who can sign in. Until that is closed, the surface does not offer the access list at all.

## Public

**Discovery has screens.** A matter's new **Discovery** section lists the review sets on that case and starts one; the **Discovery** row in the sidebar lists every review set you can open across every case; and opening one gives you its documents and its loads.

What you can do end to end: create a review set, name the Custodians of Record whose material it holds, declare a load — saying what is coming before it arrives, which is what lets the manifest state a finding rather than a claim — put the matter's own files into it, and watch every document reach one of exactly two endings. Nothing is copied: each document points at the version you picked, so your storage does not double and a later version of that file does not silently change what is under review.

**The two records a load produces can now be handed over.** The manifest says what arrived; the exception report names every document that could not be read, with the reason for each. Both download from the load, in a printable form for attaching to a letter and a machine-readable one. Taking a copy is recorded in your firm's audit log — which load, which record, which form, and never a word of the contents.

The documents screen narrows from one control: by Custodian of Record, by load, by whether a document was read, by the named reason it was not, and by family. Whatever you narrow to is in the address bar, so a narrowed list is a link you can send to somebody else on the review set. Searching by keyword, narrowing by date, and narrowing by coding are shown in the same control and unavailable, each saying what it is waiting for.

A review set you have not been put on does not appear at all, on any of these screens. That is deliberate: material another party produced is often under a protective order that not everybody on a case is cleared for, so working the matter is not enough, and a review set that showed itself while refusing you would already have said something.
