**House style for in-app copy, and `lint:ux-copy`.** `docs/conventions/ux-copy.md` sets the rules and glossary the wording batches followed (result first, errors say what happened then what to do, no machinery words, contractions in messages, one word per idea). `pnpm run lint:ux-copy` holds the mechanical part: em dashes in prose, "Nothing answered", "is where" sentences, "the platform" and "corpus", and the old Soon wording. Existing copy (mostly the areas still being reworked by other threads) is a per-file baseline that may only shrink.
