**A conflicts check names who ran it and who it searched.** The report's opening statement names the acting member from the roster (`readMemberDisplayName`) instead of quoting their handle, and reads "as a preliminary check" rather than "trigger preliminary". Each run summary and each row of the firm's log carries `subjectParties`, named from one set-valued Directory read; the check page lists them under Parties and the log gains a Parties column. A check filed on no matter reads No Matter.

## Public

**A conflicts check says who ran it and who it searched.** A check's report names the person who ran it, the check page lists the parties searched by name, and the conflicts log shows them in a Parties column. A check not filed on a matter now reads No Matter.
