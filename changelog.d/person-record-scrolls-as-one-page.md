**A person is entered and read on one scrolling page.** The New Person screen and a directory record's window now stack every section on one page that scrolls, and the list down the left side jumps between them and highlights the section you are in, so a name, a phone number and an address are entered or read without switching sections, and Tab walks every field. Pronouns and dates share one About section. New Person's right side reads the entry back as it will read (the email address itself, not "1 email"), and its button says Save. A record shows a person's pronouns and dates under their name, keeps its right-hand column for the roles it holds on the matter it was opened from, and shows the first few lines of a long list with Show All.
