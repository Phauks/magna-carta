**Meetings encryption on screen, behind the deployment switch.** The room screen draws the encryption state the client already exposed (`encryption-status.ts`): a shield mark in the header (`screen/encryption-mark.svelte`: End-to-end encrypted, Encryption paused, Encryption starting, Encryption failed), a pause banner with no dismiss (`screen/encryption-pause-notice.svelte`, the sentence from `pauseSentence`), and a fourth side-panel tab, Security (`screen/meeting-security-panel.svelte`: the state, the security code in its digit groups with Copy, and the key group's members named from the roster, guests marked). The side panel widens to `w-sm` on a wide screen in an encrypted meeting and its tab strip scrolls sideways below that. Start on a calendar entry gains an End-to-end encrypted switch, on by default, drawn only where the session says the deployment honours it: `GET /api/v1/me` now carries `meetings: { endToEndEncryption }` (schema `MeetingsDeployment` in `@magna-carta/meetings/deployment-contract`, read through `meetingsDeployment` in `apps/api-worker/src/meetings/encryption-switch.ts`). A running encrypted meeting's Join carries the mark. A host's browser now proposes the recording pause whenever the room's recording frame, the welcome or a new epoch disagrees with the group's recording bit (`#followRecordingPause` in the meeting client); captions and dial-in are not reachable on the client yet. The states real data cannot reach are reviewed at `/developer-console/meeting-encryption`. Not built: the `meeting_encryption_pause` rows and the attendance panel's Encryption tab. `MEETINGS_END_TO_END_ENCRYPTION` stays off.
