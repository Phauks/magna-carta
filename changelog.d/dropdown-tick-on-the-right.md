**Dropdown lists put the tick at the end of the row, and both kinds of choice field share one chevron.** `Select.Item` (also the Combobox and EntityPicker row) renders its text first and a trailing indicator box, so options line up with the field's own text instead of sitting behind an empty tick gutter; the chosen row is set in the medium weight with an accent tick. Group headings are semibold with more room above. `Combobox.Input` swaps its up-and-down chevrons for the Select trigger's single ChevronDown.

## Public

**Dropdowns are easier to scan.** Options now line up with the text in the field above them, the one you have chosen is marked with a tick at the end of its row, and group headings stand out from the options under them. Every dropdown shows the same arrow, whether or not you can type in it.
