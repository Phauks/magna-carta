**Changing one day of a repeating event in Outlook changes that day in Magna Carta.** A member who
moves or retitles a single day of a series in their Outlook calendar now changes that day alone in
the firm's calendar, as This Event Only does, and the rest of the series stays as it was. The same
limits apply as to any edit made in Outlook: the member needs permission to edit the calendar, and
only what the firm lets leave is carried. A change that cannot be carried is put back on that day
as it was sent. Deleting a single day in Outlook is not picked up and calls nothing off. Dormant
until a Microsoft application is registered.
