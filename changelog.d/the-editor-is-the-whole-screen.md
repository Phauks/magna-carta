**While a document is open, the editor is the whole screen.**

The edit screen drew the app's rail, the top bar, and a header naming the document over the editor, and the editor had what was left. The owner's direction is that the editor is the only thing on the screen: the page declares that it declines the shell, the app layout draws no rail and no top bar for it, and the page draws no header. A failure, a withheld file and a format with no editor keep the shell, because each of those is a message that needs its way back around it. What a member does on the platform from inside the document — save and close, the way back to the matter — will be the editor's own controls, added to its toolbar through its message protocol; until then, the way back is the browser's.

## Public

The document editor now fills the screen. Use the browser's Back to return to the matter for now.
