**Caller lookup names the town and the time there.** The free answer of `communications.callerLookup` now carries, beside the area code's region, `place` (the town the number's exchange was assigned to, from libphonenumber's exchange-level geocoding rows) and `timeZone` (the IANA zone of the number's prefix, from libphonenumber's time zone map; null where the prefix spans several). Both tables are generated by `packages/caller-lookup/scripts/refresh-places.mjs`. The record screen shows "702 · Henderson, Nevada number" and "3:19 PM PST there".

## Public

**More detail about where a number is from.** The line under a typed phone number now names the town the number was issued in, not only the state or province, and shows the current time there, so you can tell at a glance whether it is a sensible hour to call back.
