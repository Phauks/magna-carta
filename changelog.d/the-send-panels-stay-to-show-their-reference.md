The three send panels open in `ActionDialog`, and each stays on `done` to show what it came to.

Report an Issue and Report a Problem file through the firm's pipeline and then stay open with the reference the report was recorded under, the bar ending at Close; their Copy control moves out of the bar and sits beside the diagnostics it copies, since the bar holds one commit. Request a Change hands the shell `commitSoon` for the filing that is not wired yet, seats Copy beside the request it copies, and asks the shell not to restore focus to its row trigger on close — `restoreFocus={false}`, a new closed seam for the one trigger shape (revealed on focus) that a restored focus keeps latched open. The issue report and the change request keep their drafts across an ordinary close by design, so both tell the shell there is nothing to lose. All three lay their fields out with `LabelledField`.

Three allowances leave the registry; twelve remain, eleven of them named exclusions. Two wording changes: "Request a change" is "Request a Change", and the copy controls lost their bar seat, not their icons.
