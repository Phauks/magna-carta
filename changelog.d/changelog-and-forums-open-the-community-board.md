**Changelog and Forums now open the community board.** Websites > Changelog, the command palette's Changelog entry and the About window's Changelog link open the public changelog at `https://community.magnacarta.esq/changelog` in a new tab, and the in-app Changelog screen at `/updates` (the every-merged-pull-request ledger view) is removed, so there is one changelog. Websites > Forums, which was a disabled "Soon" entry, now opens the community board at `https://community.magnacarta.esq`, where its Sign In button brings a member back through the app's one-minute sign-in link. The ecosystem-site registry loses its `route` stand-in, which only the Changelog used. The merged-pull-request ledger stays in the bundle for the System Status page.
