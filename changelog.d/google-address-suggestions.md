**Address suggestions can come from Google.** With a Google Places API key set on the app worker, the street box of every address form offers Google's addresses for any country, with "Google Maps" shown under the list as Google's terms require; choosing one fills the town, region and postal code. Google takes precedence over the platform's own index and Smarty while the key is set, so the owner can try its data before deciding whether the self-hosted index is needed. Nothing changes until the key is set; the steps are in the deploy runbook, section 19a.
