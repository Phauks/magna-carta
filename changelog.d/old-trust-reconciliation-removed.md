**The Trust screen's old statement-and-match reconciliation is gone.** Its Statements and Reconciliations sections, the Record Statement and Import Statement windows and the `/trust/reconciliations` screen are removed, with their client calls and helpers. The Reconciliation screen replaces them, and the account's record now has **Open Reconciliation**. The stored statements, matches and closed proofs stay where they are and still feed the trust compliance report; the API that serves them is unchanged.

## Public

**One place to reconcile.** The older reconciliation on the Trust screen has been retired. Use **Reconciliation** in the sidebar, or **Open Reconciliation** on an account.
