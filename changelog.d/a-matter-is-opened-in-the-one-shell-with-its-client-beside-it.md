Opening a matter happens in `ActionDialog`, with the client and the clearance in the context pane beside the entry.

The window used to build the record window's frame by hand — a header band, a separator, a body that scrolled alone, a two-pane grid — because there was no frame to reach for. It was the evidence that one was needed, and it is the first caller of the one that was built: 647 lines to 622, the hand-built bands and grid gone.

It gains what every panel on the shell has. Escape, the corner dismiss and a press outside ask before throwing away a half-entered matter. The reason the commit is refused is rendered beside it rather than only reachable by hover. And a matter that opened with somebody left unstaffed now refuses its commit for the rest of the panel's life, naming who was missed — pressing it again used to open a second matter against the same draft.

The proposal that this window move onto `RecordWindow` with editing raised is withdrawn: it is not a record being read, it is an act with context beside it, and the file's own head comment says so.
