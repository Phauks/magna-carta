**The calendar's fourth round: the event-type catalog and related links.** Settings › Practice › Event Types is the catalog on the Rooms and Resources pattern: a platform type is recategorized or retired (its name and code are the shared vocabulary), a firm type is added, renamed or retired; retiring asks first and is one way, as the contract is. Two reads join the API, `GET /api/v1/events/{eventId}/links` and `/firm-events/{firmEventId}/links`, over the package's existing `readLinksTouchingEvent`, recorded as bounded by construction; the entry window's Links tab lists the settings related by hand with the other end's title (read on that end's own path, under its own visibility), and gains Relate (a setting on the same matter, or a firm event within six months, with a note) and Remove. The plan's Timeline view on the Calendar section was not built: the matter's Activity section, which landed meanwhile, is that read's one screen.
