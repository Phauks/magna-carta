**The Meetings page lists every member's room, and its History says where each meeting was filed and what became of its recording.** A new Firm Rooms card shows each colleague's standing room with Copy Link at any time and Join while it is open (`GET /api/v1/meetings/rooms`). My Room's settings (lobby, audio call, Get a New Link) move into a "..." menu beside the card's title. Recently Ended is renamed History and gains Filed To (the matter's title, now carried on every meeting summary as `matterTitle`) and Recording columns.

## Public

The Meetings page now lists everybody's room, so you can copy a colleague's link ahead of time or join when they are in. Your own room's settings are tucked into a "..." menu, and the History list shows which matter each meeting was filed on and whether it was recorded.
