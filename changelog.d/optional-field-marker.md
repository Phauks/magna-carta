**One "Optional" mark.** `Label`, `LabelledField` and `DatePicker` take `optional`, which writes "Optional" after the label in a lighter weight and ink (`mc-label-optional`). The five fields that spelled "(optional)" into their label or a hint line now use it.

## Public

**Optional fields are marked the same way everywhere.** A field you can leave empty now shows a light "Optional" after its name, instead of "(optional)" written different ways in different windows.
