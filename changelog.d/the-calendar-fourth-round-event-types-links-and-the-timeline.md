**The calendar's fourth round: the event-type catalog, related links, and the matter timeline as a view.** Settings › Practice › Event Types is the catalog on the Rooms and Resources pattern: a platform type is recategorized or retired (its name and code are the shared vocabulary), a firm type is added, renamed or retired; retiring is one way, as the contract is. Two reads join the API, `GET /api/v1/events/{eventId}/links` and `/firm-events/{firmEventId}/links`, over the package's existing `readLinksTouchingEvent`, recorded as bounded by construction; the entry window's Links tab lists the settings related by hand with the other end's title (read on that end's own path, under its own visibility), and gains Relate (a setting on the same matter, or a firm event within six months, with a note) and Remove. The matter's Calendar section gains a Timeline view: the federated read, newest first, a page at a time, each row through the calendar's one vocabulary (`timeline-presentation.ts`) with the day it fell on and a link to its record.

## Public

**Three more things on the calendar.** Under Settings › Practice › Event Types, keep the words a setting is classified under: recategorize or retire the ones that ship, add your own. On any event, press **Relate** to link it to another setting that belongs with it, and the Links tab lists them by title. And a matter's Calendar section gains **Timeline**: everything the firm has recorded about the case, newest first, from documents and mail to trust movements and the dates on this calendar, each row opening the record it came from.
