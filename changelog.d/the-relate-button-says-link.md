**The entry window's Relate button says Link, and names what it links.** The owner: "Not sure what the relate button means."

One act had two names. The tab is called Links, the model calls them links (`entry-links.ts`, `createEventLink`, `RelatedLink`), and the button alone said Relate — so the word a reader met first was the one the rest of the product never used, and a bare verb with no object told them nothing about what it would act on.

It reads **Link Settings** on a matter's setting and **Link Events** on a firm event, matching the action bar's own verb-and-noun grammar (Continue Setting, Book a Room, Cancel Event). The dialog is "Link Two Settings" / "Link Two Events", its commit reads Link, its refusal says which choice is missing, and the tab's own heading and empty line read Linked rather than Related.

Nothing about the records or the contract changed: a link is the same link, written the same way.
