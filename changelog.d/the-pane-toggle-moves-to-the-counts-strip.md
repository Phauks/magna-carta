The control that shows and hides a side pane now ends the counts strip, on every surface that has one.

It used to sit above the table. On Time, Fees, Costs and Billing that meant a row whose only occupant, most of the time, was one glyph — a control's height plus the stack gap, taken off a table already fighting for it, spent on something that is not an act on the records at all. Those rows now render only when something has to be in them: the way out of a filter, and Costs' Review where the firm runs review. An unfiltered ledger draws no row and its table starts at the top of the screen.

The file manager's two toggles came down as well, though it never had the complaint — theirs ended a toolbar the screen draws anyway. They moved because the answer has to be in one place: a reader should not have to remember that it is at the top of one screen and the bottom of four others. The folder tree keeps its toggle for now.

All six render through one component rather than six copies of a button, which is what let four ledgers draw it four different ways the last time. Every glyph control in the file manager's strip stepped down to the small square with them, so one band wears one chrome and the strip is four points shorter than it was.
