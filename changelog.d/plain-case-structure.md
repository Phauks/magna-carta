**Structure stops shouting.** `mc-table-head`, `mc-table-filter-heading`, `mc-table-grid-scope` and `mc-sidebar-group-label` drop uppercase and caps tracking for the label voice (small, medium, muted). The matters, rooms and directory tables' title links underline on hover only. `ScrollEdgeHint` draws in `border-strong` (the chrome's muted ink in the sidebar) instead of the accent gold. The `Badge` status tones become a dot in the status hue before a plain word, and the `caps` option no longer changes the case.

## Public

**Calmer lists and sidebar.** Table headings and the sidebar's section names are now written normally instead of in spaced-out capitals, record titles in lists are no longer underlined until you point at them, and the line that shows a list scrolls further is grey instead of gold. Statuses such as Active or On Hold show as a coloured dot beside the word instead of a coloured pill.
