**The address index leaves out two more share-alike sources.** A re-read of OpenAddresses' source records found the Austin-area regional layer (`us/tx/capcog`, CC BY-SA) and the Portland Metro layer (`us/or/portland_metro`, a share-alike database licence) recorded as share-alike but missing from the licence register, so the next build excludes them; the Texas and Oregon statewide layers still cover both areas. The runbook now says how to re-read the register, and the cost note prices a build at the three hours its instance stays awake.
