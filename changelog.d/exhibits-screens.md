**Exhibits screens: sets from a folder, labels that follow the order, and Mark as Filed.** A matter's new Exhibits section (under Record) lists its exhibit sets on the left and opens the chosen set as a table on the right. **Make Exhibits** on a folder in Files starts a set from that folder, and the set keeps up with the folder until it is filed. Exhibits can also be added by hand from the matter's files. Rows move by dragging their handle or from the row menu. A strip names every label the move changes, and nothing is saved until Save Order. The numbering window sets the style, the word, the start, an optional prefix and what follows Z. Mark as Filed records how and when the set went out and freezes it. A filed exhibit can be withdrawn and keeps its label.

## Public

Matters have a new Exhibits section. Choose Make Exhibits on a folder in Files and its documents become Exhibit A, B, C, in order. Drag exhibits to reorder them, and the labels follow. Before anything is saved you see which labels change. Mark a set as filed when it goes out and its labels are kept exactly as filed.
