**Scheduled billing (slice 3·7) designed.** Recurring and batch bills, payment reminders, payment
plans, and evergreen below-floor replenishment — design and implementation plan only, no application
code. Automations are tiered by consequence: drafting and recording are free, finalizing and notifying
sit behind a firm setting and manual authorization, and moving money is unreachable because no channel
to a client and no cron actor exist yet.
