**The api worker gets a design for a shape that grows by domain.** A spec and a proposed decision reorganize the Worker's 99 flat route files into about twenty domain modules, each a sub-app built by one constructor and mounted by a short manifest, with a route's request and response shapes moving to the domain package and the tree split into an HTTP side and a background side held apart by a fitness test. Four repo-wide checks are specified to stop the shape regrowing, and `wrangler check startup` joins the gate as a recorded trend. Lazy per-domain loading was checked and deferred: Hono's router refuses additions after its first match, and domains do not map to path prefixes. The standing ruling that the background orchestration stays in this Worker is kept. Step 1 of the migration is built in the same change: the `Bindings` type, the per-env auth instance and the per-env logger leave `app.ts` for modules of their own (so no consumer imports the app module), `createDomainApp()` becomes the one router constructor, the four fitness tests hold today's tree with every current offender on a ledger that only shrinks, and `pnpm gate` gains a `startup` dimension that records the worker's local startup span as a trend. Step 2 follows in the same change: the 107 route files move into 21 domain directories, each a sub-app mounted by a manifest `app.ts` iterates; the hand-maintained list of every operation becomes one committed list per domain, emitted through the contract pipeline and held per module by the conformance suite; the area note's per-surface rules move beside their modules; and the emitted contract is proved identical after a canonical key sort, so the snapshot and the client carry a reordering only. Step 3 follows: the tree splits into `src/http/` and `src/background/`, every consumer and sweep under its subsystem's name on the background side, and the boundary test becomes directory-based with no allow-list left. Step 4 begins with the calendar: its 42 request and response shapes and 11 response envelopes move to `packages/calendar/src/contract.ts` as plain zod named with `.meta({ id })`, the emitted contract stays byte-identical, and the two helpers a package-side shape needs move beside it (`identifierField` to `@magna-carta/identifiers/field`, the list envelopes to `@magna-carta/data-access/pagination-contract`). The directory follows: its 60 request and response shapes move to `packages/directory/src/contract.ts`, field descriptions included, and the contract stays byte-identical.
