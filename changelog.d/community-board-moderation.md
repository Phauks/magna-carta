**The community board's moderation queue and team triage.** The Magna Carta team has a Moderation
page on the board listing every held first contribution and every reported post or comment, with
its text in full and the reports counted by reason, never by who made them; Publish puts it up and
Remove blanks it in place. On a request, the team sets its status beside the post (a decline needs
a reason and a note, a duplicate names the original), and a question's author or the team can mark
the reply that answered it.
