**The settings surface consolidates: one page shape, one choice field, one table, one version report.** The owner's first Settings round (`docs/conventions/settings-rulings.md`, R1). The hand-rolled scroll region twenty pages carried by copy is one component (`settings-scroll-region.svelte`); the standard page (`settings-page.svelte`) is rewritten around the server-backed draft every firm page actually holds, and Billing, Calendar, Reports and File Safety render it; a plate's closing line is a component of its own. `SettingsRow`'s `select` kind renders the product's combobox, and every hand-placed `Select` under Settings (the notification matrices, the catalog dialogs, Matter Plans, Intake Procedures, the numbering editor, the Text page's Soon seams) is that same field, with sentinel values where "none" was an empty string. The five settings tables are `DataTable.Grid` over column models in `catalog-columns.ts`, so they sort, filter from their headers, offer their column roster and count themselves. The version chip's popover body is `version-report.svelte`, rendered by the chip and by the Versions page alike; the page's histogram workaround is gone. The Beta Access switch is bound to the session's channel through a getter/setter so a cancelled consent leaves it off, and its plate reads "Beta Branch". The scan depth options read "Core", "Extended", "Full", with the engine's reading and the chosen depth's meaning beneath them on File Safety. The matter numbering editor is a table of parts inside the settings anatomy, arrows leading the row.

## Public

**Settings look and behave like one surface.** Every settings page now scrolls, spaces and lists the same way. Wherever a setting is a choice from a list you can type to narrow it, on every page. The tables under Users, Event Types, Rooms and Resources and both Notifications pages sort by their headers, filter from a right-click on a column, let you choose which columns you see, and count what they show. The Versions page shows exactly what the version chip at the top of the screen shows, with more room: every part of the platform, whether the page and the server agree, and whether your firm's records can be reached. Under Beta Access the switch stays off if you cancel joining, and the heading reads Beta Branch. Under File Safety the scan depth reads Core, Extended or Full, with the engine every upload is scanned by and what the chosen depth means shown beneath it. Under Matter Numbering the parts of a scheme are a table: move a part with the arrows at the start of its row, remove it at the end, and choose a common shape from the Matter Numbering Scheme field.
