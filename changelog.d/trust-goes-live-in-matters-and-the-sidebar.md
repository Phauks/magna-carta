**Trust, first production round.** The matter's Trust section stops standing empty: `apps/web/src/lib/trust/matter-trust.svelte.ts` reads the firm's trust accounts, chooses one (the only open account, else the remembered one, else the first open one alphabetically), reads the matter's ledger against it — a 404 is "no ledger yet", a fact the surface states rather than a failure — and the three writes the section was designed around land as dialogs: a deposit, a payment out proposed under the firm's authorization posture (released at once or left pending with the eligible approvers named), and a transfer between two of the client's matters. Pending requests on the matter show in the notices band with approve, reject and withdraw. The sidebar's Trust row lights up over `apps/web/src/routes/(app)/trust`: the accounts register, the act that opens one, and per account the approval queue with its verbs, the statements and reconciliations as recorded, the bank-charge cushion, and the firm's authorization posture with its change. `POST /api/v1/trust/accounts` is the one route the slice lacked — every test had seeded an account through the domain package — and it derives the jurisdiction from the office rather than taking one. The trust-account listing now has one home, `$lib/trust/trust-api.ts`, which the report screen re-exports. The reconciliation workbench, statement entry and import, funding the cushion, and the unclaimed-funds file remain backend-only and say so on the screen.

## Public

**Trust is now in the product.** Open a matter's **Trust** section to see its trust ledger: every deposit, every payment out and every transfer between the client's matters, with the balance, what is under hold and what is available. **New Deposit** records money received; **Pay Out** proposes a payment, which is made at once when your firm's policy lets you release it alone and otherwise waits for a colleague who can; **Transfer** moves money between two of the client's matters. A payment waiting on approval shows at the top of the ledger with the verbs the right person needs. In the sidebar, **Trust** opens the firm's trust accounts: open one against an office, and for each account see the payments waiting on approval and decide them, the statements and reconciliations on record, the firm's bank-charge cushion, and the firm's policy on who may release client money.
