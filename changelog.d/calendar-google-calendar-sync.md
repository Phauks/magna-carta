**Google Calendar can be connected for calendar sync, beside Outlook.** A member connects their own Google account on Connected Accounts, with calendar permission only, and the same rules, copies, change notices and own-calendar display that Outlook has then work against Google Calendar. A member connects Outlook or Google, one at a time. It is switched off until the firm's workspace is registered with Google; the steps are in `docs/deploy-queue.md`.
