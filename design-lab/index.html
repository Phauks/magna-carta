One screen, three registers

The Matters list rendered in three craft directions. Same law in each — dark ink chrome, calm paper canvas, gold spent like money — different finishing voice.

  1. A

    Engraved Ledger

    The approved mockup pushed to its finished form: small-caps label voice, ink-tinted hairlines, ledger figures closed with a double rule, the gilt-draw selection.

  2. B

    Quiet Luxury — Law Report

    The editorial register: serif display at an off-grid weight, marginalia metadata, typeset page margins, controls held back until wanted.

  3. C

    Counsel's Terminal

    Home — the day
