The sidebar's Documents row stops being a Soon stub. Behind it is the firm's template library on
the platform's one table, over the template routes that have been on `main` since slice 2·6·1: a
template is registered from a Word document or a fillable PDF (presign, upload, register), and any
template is added to several matters at once — one assembly job per matter, each polled and each
reported with its filed document. Archive is offered to a member who holds `templates:manage`. The
matter's own Files page keeps its single-matter build path untouched; this is the firm-side twin of
it. The field inspector and browser authoring (2·6·4) stay unbuilt.
