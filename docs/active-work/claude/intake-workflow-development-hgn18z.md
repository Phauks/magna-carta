The second intake slice, from the owner's direction of 2026-09-05: the sidebar's Intake destination ships as the desk — every consultation in flight, firm-wide, with the client, the responsible member, where it stands against the procedure binding its kind, and whether the consultation is on the calendar — plus starting an intake from it (the New Matter window in intake purpose), resuming one on the matter's Intake section, and scheduling the consultation once every required step is answered. `@magna-carta/intake` gains `desk.ts` and `GET /api/v1/intake/consultations`; the step-kind vocabulary gains `client-forms` and `payment` through a per-firm table rebuild (migration `20260905T195543`, snapshot `0095`), so a stream adding its own per-firm migration will need to re-sequence the journal, and a stream regenerating the OpenAPI snapshot or the API client will collide on those files.

The design system's `EntityPicker` gains a top-level New control and a per-kind add row (`onCreate` now takes the kind), and `apps/web/src/lib/directory/directory-entity-picker.svelte` wraps it with the New Person and New Organization windows for every surface that picks a person: the matter window, the cost payee, the conflicts subject and consenting party, the directory's edges and organizations, and the calendar's participants. A stream touching any of those pickers should take the wrapper rather than the bare component.
