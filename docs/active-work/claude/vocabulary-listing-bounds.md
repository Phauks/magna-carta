The eighteen entries still named on the shrink-only baseline. Ten are firm-configured catalogs or
per-record reads bounded by something checkable, and want the claim written rather than a fix. Seven
carry a silent cap — three of them no cap at all — and gain a `capped` flag beside their rows. One is
left named with the reason it needs a design call: custody reminders merges two streams.

Tracing changed the split from what the note first said. `matters.listAssignments`,
`emails.listCaptureAddresses` and `narrativeTemplates.recentNarratives` looked like catalogs and are
not: staffing windows, retired capture addresses and distinct narratives all accumulate with a
matter's life rather than with a person configuring something, and the first two had no `LIMIT` at
any layer. `emails.listAutoFileRules` and `emails.autoFileDecisions` had caps the caller could not
see. A claim that cannot be checked at a named place is what this baseline exists to refuse, so those
five moved from "write the claim" to "give it the signal".
