The owner asked what forms are, and whether templates are simply documents. The word carried two opposite jobs on shipped screens — the sidebar said Forms meant document automation, the intake procedure said forms were what the client fills in. The study at `docs/specs/2026-09-07-documents-forms-templates-taxonomy-study.md` set out three taxonomies; the owner ruled **B** on 2026-09-07: **Forms means what a client fills in.**

Executing that ruling: `destinations.ts` changes what the Forms destination is for (id, label and route unchanged, still announced-not-shipped); the field-input scaffold moves from `/forms/assemble` to `/developer-console/document-assembly`; the ruling is recorded in `design-corrections.md` and as an ADR fragment; the feature ledger gains `document-automation.supplied-values` for the gap the move surfaced.

**Anyone editing `destinations.ts` should coordinate** — this stream changes the Forms entry's comment and description. It touches no other destination and no ordering. Nothing else in `apps/web` moves except the one route directory and the developer-console fixture list.

The owner then answered the two flags that gate the build (2026-09-07): the **public intake form ships before the portal's client forms**, with the form definition and its builder as the one shared component; and it is built **widget-first**, which produces the Turnstile browser component the joining screens have been blocked on and inverts that dependency. Recorded in the study's section 10, `design-corrections.md`, `roadmap.d/intake.md` and the deploy queue. **J3** (attachments on an anonymous form) is the one flag still open, and it scopes the slice rather than blocking it. The fail-open ADR stays Proposed by the owner's choice.

Then the slice itself, on the owner's go-ahead: `@magna-carta/intake-forms`, four per-firm tables, two permissions, the `intakeForms.*` routes including the two anonymous ones, and four screens — `/forms`, `/forms/{id}`, `/forms/{id}/submissions`, and `/enquiry/{firmId}/{token}` outside the app shell. The human-verification widget lands with it, which is the browser half the joining screens have been blocked on.

**Cross-cutting edits worth knowing about:** `packages/access-control/src/registry.ts` (two new keys), `packages/data-access/src/pii-registry.ts` (the four tables classified — a submission holds a stranger's name, address and account of their problem), `packages/identifiers/` and `packages/database/` (one per-firm migration), and `tooling/lint-back-navigation/` (taught the builder form of a back target, with its own tests). `destinations.ts` flips Forms to shipped at maturity stage alpha.

**Acceptance closes the loop (2026-09-08),** on the owner's framing that forms are vectors for clients to input information which the firm then verifies. It is the matter plan's propose-and-confirm: a pure read that maps the answers onto a record, then a write of only what a member confirmed — the entity, the intake and the client party link in one batch. It extracted `composeCreateIntakeMatter` from `packages/matters/src/matter-service.ts` and `composeCreateOrganization` from `packages/directory/`, and exported `renderDisplayName`, so **anyone editing those three should coordinate**; the composers are additive and the existing entry points call them.

Two unrelated repairs rode along, both surfaced by this stream's own gate. The triage operation predated acceptance and could write `accepted` from a bare state string, or hit the row's CHECK on an already-accepted submission; both are now refused where a member can read the answer. And generating `packages/communications/tsconfig.test.json` pulled that package's tests into `tsc -b` for the first time, exposing eight pre-existing type errors in one helper's signature — fixed in place rather than left failing the gate.
