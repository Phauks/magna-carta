# Downloads

**Downloads** is your own record of everything you have downloaded here — reports you exported,
files you took off a matter, bundles and converted documents the platform put together for you, and
discovery productions. Anything that left the platform for your computer is on the list.

**Exporting a report adds it here; it does not save a file.** Pick the format you want on the Export
menu and the report appears in your Downloads, ready. That way you can run several reports across a
morning and take them all at once, instead of a file landing in your downloads folder each time.

There are two ways in. The **download button in the top bar**, beside the timer, marks itself when
something new arrives; clicking it shows what you have taken recently and links to the full list. The
**download icon at the far end of the row at the foot of the sidebar**, beside Uploads, opens that
full list directly.

## Reading the list

Every row says what it was, what made it, when you took it, and its **standing** — which is the
answer to "can I have this again":

| Standing      | What it means                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Available     | It is here. Use **Download**.                                                                                                                                 |
| Preparing     | You asked for it and it is still being put together. It becomes Available when it is ready.                                                                   |
| On the matter | A file that lives on a matter. Still yours to download, through the matter, for as long as that matter is.                                                    |
| Record only   | It happened, and there is nothing to come back to — either it was sent straight to you and never kept, or the copy the platform held has since been released. |

The list is a table like every other register here. **Right-click any column heading** to sort it,
filter it, hide it, or pin it, and to turn on the columns that start hidden — the size, the format a
report was made for, and the day a copy stops being available. What you arrange stays arranged the
next time you come back.

**Search** narrows the whole record, not just what is on screen, so a report you exported months ago
is one word away.

## Taking something again

Use **Download** on any row.

An exported report can be taken in **any** format, not only the one you made it for — the report
itself is kept rather than the file, so the same figures are available as Excel, Word, PDF, CSV, or
plain text. The figures are exactly as they were when you ran it: an export you took on Monday still
reads Monday's numbers on Thursday, which is what makes it worth keeping.

A file the platform assembled for you, such as a bundle of documents, downloads directly through your
browser, so your browser's own download manager handles pausing, resuming, and the transfer speed.

A file that lives on a **matter** is fetched from that matter, not from this list. That matters for a
reason worth knowing: your access is checked again at the moment you ask, so this list never becomes
a way around a matter you are no longer on.

## Taking several at once

Select the ones you want and choose **Download Selected**. Exported reports arrive together as a
single zip file, each in the format it was made for.

A file the platform assembled for you, or one that lives on a matter, cannot travel inside that zip —
it downloads directly through your browser instead. If your selection includes one, it opens on its
own alongside the zip. If anything in the selection could not be produced, you are told how many,
rather than quietly handed a smaller zip.

## If you would rather have the file straight away

**Settings · Downloads** has a switch: _save a file as soon as I export_. Turn it on and exporting
does both — the file downloads immediately, and the item still appears in your Downloads so you can
take it again later. The setting follows you to any computer you sign in on.

Where a file lands is your browser's own choice, not the platform's. Every browser has an "ask where
to save each file" setting; turn that on and it will ask you each time.

## How long things stay

**The record is kept; the copy is not.** A row stays on your list, so you can always answer "did I
download that, and when". The **copy** the platform was holding for you is released after a week,
and the row's standing changes to Record only. Nothing disappears from the list on its own.

Two things never had a copy of their own to release. A file on a matter lives on that matter and is
always fetched from there. A download that was sent straight to you as it was made was never kept
anywhere, so it is Record only from the moment it appears.

If something has passed its date, taking it again will tell you so plainly. Run the report or the
download once more from the screen it came from, and the new copy appears here.

## Clearing the list

**Remove** takes a row off your list straight away. For an exported report this also deletes the copy
the platform was holding. For an assembled file it removes your row and leaves the file to expire on
its own schedule, since a colleague may have asked for the same one. For a file on a matter it
removes nothing but the row — the file is the matter's, and was never this list's to delete.

## What this list is, and what it is not

Your list is **yours**. Colleagues do not see what you have downloaded, and you do not see theirs.

It is a record of what you took, not a place to keep things. If you need a document permanently,
file it to the matter it belongs to, or save it where your firm keeps its records.
