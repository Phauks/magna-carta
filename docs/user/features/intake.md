# The Intake Desk

**Intake**, in the sidebar, is where a new client's matter begins. It lists every intake and
consultation your firm has in flight, starts a new intake, completes one into a consultation once
your intake procedure is satisfied, and puts the consultation on the calendar.

A matter passes through four statuses. **Intake** is the walk from the first call to a
consultation: the matter exists from the moment someone asks the firm to act, so the caller is in
the conflicts record, but it carries no number yet. **Consultation** is a matter the firm has not
taken on, with its own consultation number. **Active** is an open matter with a matter number.
**Closed** is a matter whose work has ended.

## Starting an Intake

1. Open **Intake** and press **New Intake**.
2. Choose the **Client** from the directory. If the caller is not in the directory yet, press
   **New** beside the field and choose **Person** or **Organization**; the record is made there and
   then, with the same duplicate check the Directory uses, and lands in the field. You can also type
   the name and pick **Add "…" as a person** from the list.
3. Give the matter a title and a practice area, and name the responsible attorney. The conflict
   check on the right runs against the client before the matter exists, if you want an early
   answer.
4. Press **Start Intake**. The matter is recorded in intake, with no number yet, and the desk
   shows it.

## Reading the Desk

Each row is one intake or consultation: its number, its status, the client, the title, the
practice area, who carries it, how far it is, when the consultation is, and when it started.

- **Number** is the consultation number once the intake is complete. An intake reads
  **Unnumbered**, because nothing has been drawn for it yet.
- **Status** is **Intake** or **Consultation**. Open and closed matters have left the desk.
- **Intake** says how far an intake is against your firm's intake procedure for that kind of
  matter: "3 of 5 answered, 1 required outstanding", or "ready to complete" once nothing required
  is left. A consultation reads **Intake complete**. A kind of matter no procedure binds says so.
- **Consultation** shows the next scheduled consultation, or **Not scheduled**.
- **Stale** marks a matter that has sat past the firm's stale-consultation age without moving.

Search the desk by title, number or client; narrow it further from any column's header. Open a
row by activating it.

## Resuming an Intake

Opening a row takes you to the matter's **Intake** section, where every step of the procedure
stands with who answered it and what they found. Record the steps only a person can do, run the
conflicts check from **Conflicts**, produce the engagement letter from **Files**, and record the
retainer, the client's forms and the payment as they happen. Several members can work the same
intake, and anyone who can read the desk can pick it up where it was left.

Files the client brings in belong on the matter's **Files** section, which is the same file
manager the matter keeps for the rest of its life; nothing moves when it becomes a consultation or
opens.

## Completing the Intake

When every required step is answered, **Complete Intake** on the row makes the matter a
consultation and draws its consultation number, which is what you quote back to the caller from
then on. Until then the control stays but is refused, and says how many required steps are
outstanding. The same step is on the matter's own status seal, as **Complete Intake**, with the
outstanding steps listed under **Requirements To Complete Intake**.

Completing an intake is recorded under your name. Steps can no longer be recorded on it afterwards;
its Intake section keeps the record as it stood.

## Scheduling the Consultation

On a consultation, **Schedule** on the row opens the calendar's event window with the title and the
people already filled in: the client and the responsible attorney. Set the date, the time and
where, and record it. The row then shows the setting; move or cancel it from the matter's
**Calendar**. On an intake, **Schedule** is refused and says the intake comes first.

## Opening the Matter

Once the consultation has happened and the firm agrees to act, open the matter from its status
seal, as described in
[`intake-procedure.md`](intake-procedure.md#opening-the-matter). The matter leaves the desk when it
opens, and when an intake or a consultation is closed without opening.

## Tips and Edge Cases

- **The desk is the whole firm's.** A receptionist, a paralegal and an attorney all see the same
  list; the per-attorney Consultations view under Matters still shows only your own consultations,
  and intakes appear only here.
- **A consultation can be recorded outright** from **New Matter** on the Matters screen, skipping
  intake, the way a matter can be opened outright for an existing client. It takes its consultation
  number at once and no procedure gates it.
- **New beside a person field is everywhere.** The same button stands beside every field where a
  person or an organization is chosen: a cost's payee, a conflicts check's subjects, an event's
  participants. It needs the permission to add to the directory, and says so when your account
  does not hold it.
- **Two new kinds of step** are available to your intake procedures: **Client Forms**, for the
  forms the client fills in online, and **Payment**, for money sent or received before the firm
  acts. Both are recorded by a member today.
