# The Intake Desk

**Intake**, in the sidebar, is where a new client's matter begins. It lists every intake your firm
has in flight, starts a new one, and puts the consultation on the calendar once the intake is
complete. An intake is a consultation: a matter that has not yet been opened.

## Starting an Intake

1. Open **Intake** and press **New Intake**.
2. Choose the **Client** from the directory. If the caller is not in the directory yet, press
   **New** beside the field and choose **Person** or **Organization**; the record is made there and
   then, with the same duplicate check the Directory uses, and lands in the field. You can also type
   the name and pick **Add "…" as a person** from the list.
3. Give the matter a title and a practice area, and name the responsible attorney. The conflict
   check on the right runs against the client before the consultation exists, if you want an early
   answer.
4. Press **Start Intake**. The consultation is recorded with a number of its own, and the desk
   shows it.

## Reading the Desk

Each row is one intake: its number, the client, the title, the practice area, who carries it, how
far it is, when the consultation is, and when it started.

- **Intake** says how far it is against your firm's intake procedure for that kind of matter:
  "3 of 5 answered, 1 required outstanding", or "ready to open" once nothing required is left. A
  kind of matter no procedure binds says so.
- **Consultation** shows the next scheduled consultation, or **Not scheduled**.
- **Stale** marks an intake that has sat past the firm's stale-consultation age without moving.

Search the desk by title, number or client; narrow it further from any column's header. Open an
intake by activating its row.

## Resuming an Intake

Opening a row takes you to the consultation's **Intake** section, where every step of the
procedure stands with who answered it and what they found. Record the steps only a person can do,
run the conflicts check from **Conflicts**, produce the engagement letter from **Files**, and
record the retainer, the client's forms and the payment as they happen. Several members can work
the same intake, and anyone who can read the desk can pick it up where it was left.

Files the client brings in belong on the consultation's **Files** section, which is the same file
manager the matter will keep once it opens; nothing moves when it does.

## Scheduling the Consultation

When every required step is answered, **Schedule** on the row opens the calendar's event window on
the consultation with the title and the people already filled in: the client and the responsible
attorney. Set the date, the time and where, and record it. The row then shows the setting; move or
cancel it from the consultation's **Calendar**.

Until the intake is complete, **Schedule** stays but is refused, and says how many required steps
are outstanding.

## Opening the Matter

Once the consultation has happened and the firm agrees to act, open the matter from the
consultation's status seal, as described in
[`intake-procedure.md`](intake-procedure.md#opening-the-matter). The intake leaves the desk when
the matter opens, and when a consultation is closed without opening.

## Tips and Edge Cases

- **The desk is the whole firm's.** A receptionist, a paralegal and an attorney all see the same
  list; the per-attorney Consultations view still shows only your own.
- **New beside a person field is everywhere.** The same button stands beside every field where a
  person or an organization is chosen: a cost's payee, a conflicts check's subjects, an event's
  participants. It needs the permission to add to the directory, and says so when your account
  does not hold it.
- **Two new kinds of step** are available to your intake procedures: **Client Forms**, for the
  forms the client fills in online, and **Payment**, for money sent or received before the firm
  acts. Both are recorded by a member today.
