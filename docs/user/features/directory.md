# The Directory

The Directory is the firm's one book of the people and organizations it deals with: clients,
opposing parties, counsel, courts, experts, vendors. Every matter's client, every party on a matter,
every payee on a cost and every subject of a conflicts check is a record here, so a person recorded
once serves every file they appear on.

## Finding a record

Open **Directory** from the sidebar. The list shows everyone the firm holds, people and organizations
together, in name order, with the count beside it. Use the filter to see one kind, and the search to
find a record by any name it has ever held: a maiden name, a former trading name or a typo still
finds the right record, and the results say which name matched.

Press **New** to record a person or an organization. Before it is created, the Directory checks for
records that may already be the same person and shows them, so the same client does not end up in
the book twice under two spellings.

## A record

A record answers the questions the firm keeps an answer to:

- **Names**: every name the record has held, with the current one marked. Recording a marriage or a
  rename keeps the old name findable.
- **Contact**: email addresses, telephone and fax numbers, and postal addresses, each marked as
  permitted or not and one of them as the preferred way to reach the person. A number that turned
  out to be disconnected, or mail that came back, is recorded as failed rather than deleted, so the
  next person does not ring it again.
- **Pronouns** and **Dates** on a person: what the person stated, and their dates of birth and death.
- **Identifiers**: sealed numbers such as a social security number or a driver's licence. The screen
  shows that a value exists; revealing it needs a separate permission and is written to the firm's
  audit log.
- **Duplicates**: records the Directory suspects are the same, with a way to fold one into the other.
  Folding keeps everything from both records and never deletes either.
- **Relationships**: how this record connects to others. On an organization, **People** lists its
  officers, directors, partners, employees, members, shareholders and points of contact; on a person,
  **Organizations** lists the organizations they belong to. Recording one is choosing the other
  record from the Directory and the kind of relationship, with an optional role and note.
- **Matters**: every matter this record is a party to, and in what role.
- **Categories**: the firm's own labels, such as attorney, judge, court, expert or vendor. Assign one
  from the list, or type a new one and it is created and assigned in one step.

## Who can change the Directory

Reading needs only access to the firm. Adding a record, changing names, contact points,
relationships and categories needs the Directory edit permission. Revealing a sealed identifier needs
its own permission and is always logged.
