# What's new — the product changelog

_Stub — the full guide is authored when the changelog page ships. The content and its contract are built; there is no page to read it on yet._

## What it does

The product changelog is where you find out what changed in Magna Carta, written for you rather than for the people who build it. Entries are grouped **by date** — the product is improved continuously rather than in numbered releases, so a date is the honest way to say when something arrived.

Each entry is filed under one of six headings, so you can tell at a glance what kind of change it was:

- **Added** — something you can now do that you could not before.
- **Changed** — something that already existed and now behaves differently.
- **Deprecated** — something still working that is on its way out.
- **Removed** — something that is gone.
- **Fixed** — something that was not working properly and now is.
- **Security** — a change made to protect your data.

## What you will find there, and what you will not

The changelog covers **changes you can see or use.** Design work, internal tooling, and research are deliberately kept out of it: an entry describing a feature that has been planned but not built would tell you a capability exists when it does not, and that is worse than saying nothing.

That filter is the reason entries can be short. A month of work on the platform's own machinery may produce no changelog entry at all, and that is the changelog working as intended rather than a gap in it.

## Common tasks (once the page ships)

- **See what changed recently.** Open the changelog and read from the top; the newest date is first.
- **Find when something arrived.** Entries stay on the page permanently, so a feature's arrival date remains findable long after the fact.
- **Filter by kind of change.** Narrow to Fixed or Security when you are checking whether a specific problem has been addressed.

## Related features

- **Service status** — for whether something is working *right now*, rather than what changed. A current outage or degradation appears on the status page, not here.
- **Community board** — for asking for a change, or asking how something works. Not yet available.

## Questions this guide does not answer

- **Something is broken and I want to know if you know.** That is the service status page, not the changelog.
- **I want to request a feature.** The community board is designed but not yet built. Until it is, contact support.
