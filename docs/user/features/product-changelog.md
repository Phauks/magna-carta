# What's new — the product changelog

## What it does

The Changelog lists **every change merged into Magna Carta**, newest first. Each change is grouped under the day it landed, and each one shows:

- **Its title**, saying what changed.
- **Its badges.** The first two are the change's number and its code reference; each opens that change on GitHub, which only people with access to the project's code can see. Then comes the kind of change, a **Members see this** badge when the change is one you can see or use, and the parts of Magna Carta it touched, such as Matters or Billing.
- **Its changelog entry**, the note its author wrote about it. A change merged without a note says so.

The kinds of change are:

- **Added** — something you can now do that you could not before.
- **Changed** — something that already existed and now behaves differently.
- **Deprecated** — something still working that is on its way out.
- **Removed** — something that is gone.
- **Fixed** — something that was not working properly and now is.
- **Security** — a change made to protect your data.
- **Unrecorded** — a change merged without a note.

Changes that touched no part of Magna Carta you can see, such as work on the tools that build it, are tagged **Behind the scenes**. A change that touched many parts at once is tagged **Across the app**.

## Opening it

Select your avatar at the top right, point to **Websites**, and choose **Changelog**. You can also press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), type "changelog", and choose **Website: Changelog**. The page opens inside Magna Carta.

The changelog comes with the version of Magna Carta you are using, so it never describes something your version does not have yet, and it opens even when the service is having trouble.

## Common tasks

- **See what changed recently.** The newest day is open at the top; the line under the title says when the latest change landed. Select an older day to open it.
- **Find when a part of Magna Carta changed.** Open **Area** and tick the part you are interested in, or select an area badge on any change. Tick more than one to see changes to any of them.
- **See only what you would notice.** Open **All pull requests** and choose **Only what members see**.
- **Filter by kind of change.** Open **Kind** and tick Added, Fixed, or another kind.
- **Search.** Use the search at the top right; it looks through titles, numbers and notes.
- **Read titles only.** Select **Collapse entries** to fold every note away, or the arrow beside one title to fold just that one. **Expand entries** opens them all again.
- **Clear the filters.** Select **Clear**, which appears whenever a filter is on.

## Related features

- **Service status** — for whether something is working _right now_, rather than what changed. A current outage or degradation appears on the status page, not here.
- **Community board** — for asking for a change, or asking how something works. Not yet available.

## Questions this guide does not answer

- **Something is broken and I want to know if you know.** That is the service status page, not the changelog.
- **I want to request a feature.** The community board is designed but not yet built. Until it is, contact support.
