# Banking

## What it does

Banking shows each of the firm's bank accounts, operating and trust, with the bank's balance next to
the balance in the books, and the bank's lines from the last 90 days. Each line is marked:

- **In Books**: the books have an entry for the same amount within a week of it.
- **Not in Books**: no entry matches it yet.
- **Pending**: the bank has not posted it.

Banking only reads. Nothing the bank reports is entered into the books unless someone adds it. A
deposit that equals several entries added together stays Not in Books on purpose, so a deposit posted
to the wrong place cannot hide behind a total that happens to agree.

## Who can use it

Owners, administrators, attorneys and billing staff can see Banking. Only owners and administrators
can connect or disconnect a bank or choose which book account a bank account is.

## Connecting a bank

1. Open **Banking** and press **Connect Bank**.
2. Pick your bank in the window that opens and sign in there. Magna Carta never sees your bank
   password.
3. Choose which of your checking and savings accounts to share.
4. For each account, choose the bank account in the books it is, or choose not to use it.

## When the bank asks you to sign in again

Banks ask for a new sign-in from time to time. The connection then shows **Sign In Again**. Press it
and sign in; nothing is lost.

## Disconnecting

Open the connection's menu and choose **Disconnect**. The bank stops sharing with Magna Carta. Lines
already shown stay, for the record.

## If Connect Bank is not available

Your firm's Banking has not been set up yet. Ask whoever runs Magna Carta for your firm.
