# In/Out Log

_The front desk's record of things left for somebody to pick up: copies, papers, a file handed back.
Each one gets a number, lists who it is for, and stays on **Waiting for Pickup** until somebody picks
it up or it is moved into Custodian._

## What it does

When something is left at the desk, press **Drop Off** and say who it is from, what it is, and who it
is **To**. You can list several people; anyone listed can pick it up. The matter, where it is kept and
notes are optional. The number (for example `IO-0042`) is on screen before you save, so you can write
it on the envelope.

When somebody collects it, press **Pick Up** on its row. Choose one of the people it was to, or
**Someone else** and type their name, and tick **ID checked** if you checked their identification.
Magna Carta records who handed it over.

## The two tabs

- **Waiting for Pickup** lists what is still at the desk, oldest first.
- **Log** lists everything, newest first, with what happened to each one.

Search finds an entry by its number, what it is, who it is from or to, or the matter.

## Moving something to Custodian

If something turns out to need safekeeping, open the row's **...** menu and choose **Move to
Custodian**. The New Packet window opens with the description and the Directory people already filled
in; choose who it is held for and where it goes, and save. The entry then shows the custody number it
became. You need permission to use Custodian for this.

## Deleting a mistake

**Delete** in the row's menu removes an entry recorded by mistake. It is only offered while the entry is
waiting. Its number is not used again.

## Who can use it

Everyone can read the log. Owners, attorneys, paralegals and billing staff can drop off, pick up, move
and delete. An entry on a matter is only shown to people who can see that matter.
