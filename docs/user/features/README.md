# Feature guides (end users)

One plain-language guide per user-visible feature. Naming: `<feature>.md` (e.g. `time-tracking.md`, `client-portal.md`, `matters.md`).

Each guide follows the per-feature pattern in [`../README.md`](../README.md): what it does, how to do the common tasks, tips/edge cases, related features — no internal architecture.

- [`signing-in.md`](signing-in.md) — Signing in: the email, password, and authenticator-code steps, staying signed in, trusting a device, and what to do when a sign-in is refused.
- [`opening-and-finding-matters.md`](opening-and-finding-matters.md) — Opening and finding matters: the firm's docket, the scope and search controls, what the matter-number and Created columns mean, and opening a matter or recording a consultation.
- [`matter-documents.md`](matter-documents.md) — A matter's documents: the Files page — adding a document from a file or from a template, saving a new version, the version history, and opening the browser editor.
- [`joining-magna-carta.md`](joining-magna-carta.md) — Joining Magna Carta: registering, the three-way choice between being a client, working at a firm, or starting one, and redeeming an invitation with a code, surname and date of birth.
- [`asset-register.md`](asset-register.md) — Asset register: what an estate or a client owns, kept on the matter, with a dated value history, interests, and linked documents (backend built, screens pending).
- [`billing-fee-arrangements.md`](billing-fee-arrangements.md) — Fee arrangements: recording how a matter is billed (hourly, flat fee, contingency, retainer), billing each kind, retainer top-ups, and how multi-matter bills are presented (backend built, screens pending).
- [`correcting-a-bill.md`](correcting-a-bill.md) — Correcting a bill: credit notes, write-offs, money the firm owes a client back, undoing a misplaced payment, and what to do when a settlement collapses after the contingency fee was billed (backend built, screens pending).
- [`matter-notes.md`](matter-notes.md) — Matter notes: writing down what happened on a case, correcting a note without losing what it said before, withdrawing one that should never have been written, and who can read them.
- [`searching-a-matter.md`](searching-a-matter.md) — Searching a matter: one search at the top right of every matter screen, starting on the section you are on and widening to the whole matter, what answers today and what a group says when it cannot.
- [`case-calendar-and-deadlines.md`](case-calendar-and-deadlines.md) — Case calendar and deadlines: the firm calendar and the matter's Calendar section, recording hearings and filings, recording the dates the firm must act by, opening an entry, and the matter timeline that gathers everything already recorded about a case.
- [`firm-events-and-rooms.md`](firm-events-and-rooms.md) — Firm events and rooms: meetings, closures, training, and holidays that belong to the firm rather than to a case, recorded from the firm calendar, plus bookable rooms, vehicles, and equipment with visible double-booking (rooms and bookings: backend built, screens pending).
- [`continuing-a-hearing.md`](continuing-a-hearing.md) — Continuing a hearing: when the court vacates a setting and makes a new one, the record keeps both, linked in order — and no deadline ever moves by itself.
- [`document-templates-and-assembly.md`](document-templates-and-assembly.md) — Document templates and assembly: authoring a template in Word, registering it, and producing a document onto a matter (backend built, screens pending).
- [`recording-that-a-bill-went-out.md`](recording-that-a-bill-went-out.md) — Recording that a bill went out: how and when each client got their bill, why every attempt is its own permanent record, and where it shows up before you take fees out of trust (backend built, screens pending).
- [`directory.md`](directory.md) — The Directory: finding a person or organization by any name they have held, what a record holds (names, contact, pronouns, dates, sealed identifiers, duplicates, relationships, matters, categories), and who can change it.
- [`matter-parties.md`](matter-parties.md) — A matter's parties: who is on a matter and in what role, adding one from the Directory with a role and a capacity, and removing one without touching the Directory record.
- [`pronouns.md`](pronouns.md) — Pronouns on a Directory record: recording the pronouns a person stated, and how assembled documents refer to someone whose record states none (backend built, screens pending).
- [`filing-email-to-a-matter.md`](filing-email-to-a-matter.md) — Filing email to a matter, including capture-address management and auto-file rules (backend built, screens pending).
- [`notification-emails.md`](notification-emails.md) — Notification emails: the daily or weekly summary of what is waiting for you, why it carries counts and never a matter, client, document or amount, and what you and your firm each control (built, and unreachable until the firm's outbound mail is set up).
- [`review-sets-and-loading.md`](review-sets-and-loading.md) — Review sets: gathering the documents in a case, saying whose records they are, and loading the firm's own files and filed email (backend built, screens pending).
- [`reading-the-documents-in-a-review-set.md`](reading-the-documents-in-a-review-set.md) — Reading a review set: what each document says, how it was read, and the named reason for every one that could not be (backend built, screens pending).
- [`discovery-exception-report.md`](discovery-exception-report.md) — The load manifest and the exception report: the two records a load produces, what every named reason means, and which of them a firm can act on (backend built, screens pending).
- [`coding-documents-in-review.md`](coding-documents-in-review.md) — Coding documents in review: deciding what each document is, coding families and in bulk with exact undo, seeing where the review has got to, and who can see a review set at all (backend built, screens pending).
- [`trust-compliance-report.md`](trust-compliance-report.md) — The trust compliance report: the document a firm hands an examiner about one trust account — client ledgers, the journal, what the firm attested to at each reconciliation, and what did not tie out (backend built, screens pending).
- [`safe-custody-register.md`](safe-custody-register.md) — Safe custody register: physical originals the firm holds, where they are, and whose they are (backend built, screens pending).
- [`conflicts-reports.md`](conflicts-reports.md) — Conflicts reports: what a conflicts check searched, what it could not search, what is still unanswered, and why the platform reports a search rather than a verdict (backend built, screens pending).
- [`automatic-conflicts-rechecks.md`](automatic-conflicts-rechecks.md) — Automatic conflicts re-checks: the six changes that make a matter get looked at again, what deliberately does not, and why a matter says a re-check is owed before one has run (backend built, screens pending).
- [`shipping.md`](shipping.md) — Shipping & postage (stub; screens pending and no account can be connected yet).
- [`whois-lookup.md`](whois-lookup.md) — WHOIS Lookup: who registered a domain or IP address, and when.
- [`safe-website-viewer.md`](safe-website-viewer.md) — Safe Website Viewer: what a suspicious link leads to, seen from an isolated remote browser (screen shipped; captures wait on the rendering service being enabled).
- [`metadata-inspector-scrubber.md`](metadata-inspector-scrubber.md) — Metadata Inspector & Scrubber (stub; backend built, screens pending).
- [`file-hash-integrity.md`](file-hash-integrity.md) — File Hash & Integrity (stub; backend built, screens pending).
- [`file-workbench.md`](file-workbench.md) — File Workbench: the Tools screen a file that belongs to no matter is dropped on, where its real type, its fingerprints and every other file capability are read in one place — including for a file the scanner flagged.
- [`file-unlock-password-recovery.md`](file-unlock-password-recovery.md) — File Unlock & Password Recovery (stub; backend built, screens pending).
- [`product-changelog.md`](product-changelog.md) — What's new: how the changelog is organized and what it covers (stub; the content is built, the page is not).
- [`when-something-goes-wrong.md`](when-something-goes-wrong.md) — When something goes wrong: what a failure tells you, the Error ID and what to do with it, what Copy details puts on your clipboard and what it never includes, reporting a problem from anywhere, and the two messages that mean the trouble is ours rather than yours.
- [`seeing-what-version-you-are-on.md`](seeing-what-version-you-are-on.md) — Seeing what version you are on: the version chip at the top right, the overlay listing every part of the platform with its build, and the notice when the web app and the API differ.
- [`product-roadmap.md`](product-roadmap.md) — What is coming: the horizon bands, why there are no dates, and what the roadmap deliberately leaves off (stub; the content is built, the page is not).
- [`asking-a-question-of-a-matters-documents.md`](asking-a-question-of-a-matters-documents.md) — Asking a question of a matter's documents: it returns passages rather than answers, what it can and cannot see, the freshness sentence on every result, and what an empty result does and does not mean (off for every firm; backend built, retrieval and screens pending).
