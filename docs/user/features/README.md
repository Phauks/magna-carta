# Feature guides (end users)

One plain-language guide per user-visible feature. Naming: `<feature>.md` (e.g. `time-tracking.md`, `client-portal.md`, `matters.md`).

Each guide follows the per-feature pattern in [`../README.md`](../README.md): what it does, how to do the common tasks, tips/edge cases, related features — no internal architecture.

- [`signing-in.md`](signing-in.md) — Signing in and out: the email, password, and authenticator-code steps, staying signed in, trusting a device, what to do when a sign-in is refused, and ending the session you are on.
- [`your-account.md`](your-account.md) — Your account: setting your name in its parts, adding or removing your photo, changing your password (and signing your other devices out in the same step), and which email address your account uses — with why that one cannot be changed here.
- [`signed-in-devices.md`](signed-in-devices.md) — Signed-in devices: every computer and phone signed in as you, what each row says, signing one out or everything except the one you are on, and why a signed-out device takes a few minutes to be turned away.
- [`opening-and-finding-matters.md`](opening-and-finding-matters.md) — Opening and finding matters: the firm's docket, the scope and search controls, what the matter-number and Created columns mean, and opening a matter or recording a consultation.
- [`matter-documents.md`](matter-documents.md) — A matter's documents: the Files page — adding a document from a file or from a template, saving a new version, the version history, opening a document in its editor or reader (Word documents in the browser editor, text documents in the text editor, PDFs in the reader), and looking inside an archive - zip, tar, 7z or rar - and taking files out of a zip, a tar or a 7z.
- [`joining-magna-carta.md`](joining-magna-carta.md) — Joining Magna Carta: registering, the three-way choice between being a client, working at a firm, or starting one, and redeeming an invitation with a code, surname and date of birth.
- [`asset-register.md`](asset-register.md) — Asset register: what an estate or a client owns, kept on the matter, with a dated value history, interests, and linked documents (backend built, screens pending).
- [`billing-fee-arrangements.md`](billing-fee-arrangements.md) — Fee arrangements: recording how a matter is billed (hourly, flat fee, contingency, retainer), billing each kind, retainer top-ups, and how multi-matter bills are presented (backend built, screens pending).
- [`correcting-a-bill.md`](correcting-a-bill.md) — Correcting a bill: credit notes, write-offs, money the firm owes a client back, undoing a misplaced payment, and what to do when a settlement collapses after the contingency fee was billed (backend built, screens pending).
- [`matter-money-sections.md`](matter-money-sections.md) — A matter's money sections: Time, Fees, Costs, Billing and Trust on one shared surface, stepping between them from the top row, clearing filters, row density, the framed alternative, and what the Trust section shows.
- [`the-firms-bills.md`](the-firms-bills.md) — The firm's bills: every bill for every client with the receivables total over it, choosing which bills by state, what the document's own figures mean across the firm, opening a bill as a link, and why the acts against one live on its matter.
- [`the-firms-trust-accounts.md`](the-firms-trust-accounts.md) — The firm's trust accounts: opening one against an office, where each account stands, the payments waiting on approval and deciding them, recording or importing a bank statement and reconciling it, the bank-charge cushion, the unclaimed-funds file, and the firm's policy on who may release client money.
- [`the-firms-fees.md`](the-firms-fees.md) — The firm's fees: every flat charge recorded across every matter for the days you choose, why a fee is never worked out from time, the named spans and a custom range, billable against absorbed, what the two figures mean, going to a row's matter, finding the bill a fee reached, and why deleting a draft is not voiding a fee.
- [`the-firms-costs.md`](the-firms-costs.md) — The firm's costs: what was advanced across every matter for the days you choose, the named spans and a custom range, billable against absorbed, what the three figures split, going to a row's matter, and finding the bill a cost reached.
- [`the-firms-time.md`](the-firms-time.md) — The firm's time: every hour recorded across every matter for the days you choose, the six named spans and a custom range, reading a colleague's time or the whole firm's, narrowing by column, what the hours-and-money figures cover, and opening an entry from the period.
- [`matter-activity.md`](matter-activity.md) — Matter activity: a case's whole story in date order across every part of the matter, each row named by the section that holds it, filtered by column, opened into its section, and a note written from it.
- [`matter-notes.md`](matter-notes.md) — Matter notes: writing down what happened on a case, saying when it happened, editing and deleting one, attaching the matter's documents to it, and who can read them.
- [`searching-a-matter.md`](searching-a-matter.md) — Searching a matter: one search at the top right of every matter screen, starting on the section you are on and widening to the whole matter, what answers today and what a group says when it cannot.
- [`searching-across-matters.md`](searching-across-matters.md) — Searching across matters: one question asked of every matter you can see, reached from the top bar, what is searched, the list of places, what is not searched here and why, and what a very common word does.
- [`searching-a-screen.md`](searching-a-screen.md) — Searching the screen you are on: the one search control in the top right of every Workspace screen, how it opens and clears, what each screen's search reaches, and why the calendar's search covers only the dates on screen and says so.
- [`intake.md`](intake.md) — The intake desk: starting an intake from the sidebar, recording a caller into the directory from the client field, reading how far every intake is, resuming one, and scheduling the consultation once the intake is complete.
- [`intake-procedure.md`](intake-procedure.md) — The intake procedure: writing what a kind of matter must clear before it opens, what each kind of step is answered by, recording and waiving steps on a consultation, and how the matter opens once every required step is answered.
- [`tasks.md`](tasks.md) — Tasks: work with somebody's name on it, on a matter or on none — due dates and priorities, finishing and reopening, the difference between cancelling and deleting, waiting on another task, the firm-wide Tasks list, the matter plans a firm writes for itself, the time logged against a task, and the groundwork for connecting tasks to another tool.
- [`asking-magnus.md`](asking-magnus.md) — Asking Magnus: the assistant in the sidebar and the one on a matter, what it can see (what you type, and the files you attach) and do (only answer), sorting and renaming conversations, keeping one on a matter, the disclosure you accept once, what happens to a transcript, and why it is off until the firm turns it on.
- [`case-calendar-and-deadlines.md`](case-calendar-and-deadlines.md) — Case calendar and deadlines: the firm calendar and the matter's Calendar section, recording hearings and filings, recording the dates the firm must act by, opening an entry, and the matter timeline that gathers everything already recorded about a case.
- [`firm-events-and-rooms.md`](firm-events-and-rooms.md) — Firm events and rooms: meetings, closures, training, and holidays that belong to the firm rather than to a case, recorded from the firm calendar, plus bookable rooms, vehicles, and equipment with visible double-booking, booked from an event and read on the room grid.
- [`choosing-what-syncs-to-your-calendar.md`](choosing-what-syncs-to-your-calendar.md) — Choosing what syncs to your calendar: sending each event type to one of your Outlook calendars or not at all, what your firm's limit means, and how an administrator sets that limit.
- [`connecting-your-google-calendar.md`](connecting-your-google-calendar.md) — Connecting your Google Calendar: connecting your own Google account from Settings, what the permission window allows, why you connect Outlook or Google and not both, what to do when the sign-in does not complete, and disconnecting.
- [`connecting-your-outlook-calendar.md`](connecting-your-outlook-calendar.md) — Connecting your Outlook calendar: connecting your own Microsoft 365 account from Settings, what the permission window allows, what to do when the sign-in does not complete, and disconnecting.
- [`continuing-a-hearing.md`](continuing-a-hearing.md) — Continuing a hearing: when the court vacates a setting and makes a new one, the record keeps both, linked in order — and no deadline ever moves by itself.
- [`document-templates-and-assembly.md`](document-templates-and-assembly.md) — Document templates and assembly: authoring a template in Word, registering it from the Documents screen, reading a template's standing in the library, producing a document onto a matter from its Files page, and adding one template to several matters at once.
- [`recording-that-a-bill-went-out.md`](recording-that-a-bill-went-out.md) — Recording that a bill went out: how and when each client got their bill, why every attempt is its own permanent record, and where it shows up before you take fees out of trust (backend built, screens pending).
- [`directory.md`](directory.md) — The Directory: finding a person or organization by any name they have held, what a record holds (names, contact, pronouns, dates, sealed identifiers, duplicates, relationships, matters, categories), and who can change it.
- [`matter-parties.md`](matter-parties.md) — A matter's parties: who is on a matter and in what role, adding one from the Directory with a role and a capacity, and removing one without touching the Directory record.
- [`pronouns.md`](pronouns.md) — Pronouns on a Directory record: recording the pronouns a person stated, and how assembled documents refer to someone whose record states none (backend built, screens pending).
- [`filing-email-to-a-matter.md`](filing-email-to-a-matter.md) — Filing email to a matter, including capture-address management and auto-file rules (backend built, screens pending).
- [`your-notifications.md`](your-notifications.md) — Your notifications: the bell and the list behind it, what the twelve categories cover, working through the list, why items can leave it without anybody editing them, and choosing per category what reaches you in the app and by email.
- [`notification-emails.md`](notification-emails.md) — Notification emails: the daily or weekly summary of what is waiting for you, why it carries counts and never a matter, client, document or amount, and what you and your firm each control (built, and unreachable until the firm's outbound mail is set up).
- [`review-sets-and-loading.md`](review-sets-and-loading.md) — Review sets: gathering the documents in a case, saying whose records they are, and loading the firm's own files and filed email.
- [`reading-the-documents-in-a-review-set.md`](reading-the-documents-in-a-review-set.md) — Reading a review set: the document list, narrowing it, walking the documents you narrowed to, what each one says, the family it arrived in, the other copies of it the firm holds, and the named reason for every one that could not be read.
- [`discovery-exception-report.md`](discovery-exception-report.md) — The load manifest and the exception report: the two records a load produces, where to download each, what every named reason means, and which of them a firm can act on.
- [`coding-documents-in-review.md`](coding-documents-in-review.md) — Coding documents in review: deciding what each document is, coding families and in bulk with exact undo, seeing where the review has got to, and who can see a review set at all (backend built, screens pending).
- [`reports.md`](reports.md) — Reports: the catalogue of every report the firm can run, setting a report's account, period, office and knowledge day, reading the totals and tables on screen, exporting it, and sending somebody the same report by its address.
- [`trust-compliance-report.md`](trust-compliance-report.md) — The trust compliance report: the document a firm hands an examiner about one trust account — client ledgers, the journal, what the firm attested to at each reconciliation, and what did not tie out.
- [`calls-and-texts.md`](calls-and-texts.md) — Calls and texts: recording what was said on the phone and by text, the queue of messages nobody has filed yet, and filing one onto a matter (including an intake or a consultation, which carry no matter number).
