# Reconciling a trust account

_New. Open it from **Reconciliation** in the sidebar, under Money._

## What it does

Reconciliation is where you tick off a trust account's bank statement, one period at a time. You
enter the dates and the balances from the statement, tick each deposit and withdrawal that cleared
the bank, and the screen shows the difference between what cleared and what the bank says. When
the difference is what you expect, you finish the reconciliation and print the report.

Each account has one reconciliation in progress at a time. Several people can work on it at once:
every tick is saved the moment you make it, and the screen shows other people's ticks as they
happen, without a Refresh button.

## The list

**Reconciliation** opens on the list of the account's reconciliations, newest first. If the firm
has more than one trust account, pick the account at the top. Each row shows the dates, the start
balance, what was deposited and withdrawn, the end balance, the **Change** (end less start), the
**Difference**, its status, and how many files and notes it has. Use the header menu on any column
to sort it, filter it or hide it; **Finished By** and **IOLTA Interest** are hidden until you turn
them on.

The buttons above the list narrow it to **In Progress**, **Finished** or **Needs Review**.

## Starting a reconciliation

Press **New Reconciliation**.

- The first time an account is reconciled, type the **Start Date** and **Start Balance** from the
  statement.
- After that, the start date and start balance carry over from the end of the last finished
  reconciliation, and are shown locked.

Enter the **End Date** and, if you have it, the **End Balance** from the statement. Any dates
work: a month, half a month or a quarter. You can attach the statement and any other files now,
or later with **Attach Files**. Press **Start Reconciliation**.

Every attached file is checked for malware first. A file that fails the check is not kept, and you
see a message saying so: ask the bank for a fresh copy of the statement. If the check can't run at
that moment, nothing is kept and you can try again shortly.

## Ticking off the statement

The figures across the top read: start balance, plus what is ticked as deposited, less what is
ticked as withdrawn, gives the **Cleared Balance**. The **Difference** is the end balance less the
cleared balance, and is shaded while it is not zero.

- Tick each line that appears on the statement. Untick it to take it back.
- Narrow the list by type (checks, deposits, wires and so on) or to **Uncleared**, **Needs
  Review** or **New** lines. **Tick All Shown** and **Untick All Shown** are in the menu beside the
  filters.
- Change the end date or end balance at the top at any time.
- Double-click a line, or press Enter on it, to see its details, with links to the matter's trust
  ledger and to the account's checks.

The account is never locked. If someone records a payment dated inside the period after you have
started, it appears in the list marked **New** so you can see what arrived late.

## Notes and Needs Review

The **Note** column opens a line's notes. Write a note and press **Add Note**; notes are kept for
good and are never undone. Turn on **Needs Review** to flag a line for someone to look at, and
press **Mark Resolved** when it is settled. Lines that need review are counted at the top, and you
are reminded of them before you finish.

## IOLTA interest

Interest the bank credits to an IOLTA account belongs to no client, so it sits in its own box at
the bottom, not in a matter ledger. **Add Interest** records interest **Credited by Bank** or
**Swept Out** to the bar foundation; tick the box to record the matching sweep on the same day.
Interest lines are ticked like any other line and count in the deposited and withdrawn figures.

## Undo, History, Save and Restore Points

- **Undo** and **Redo** step back and forward through your own changes.
- **History** lists every change anyone made, newest first. **Undo** on any line there reverses
  that one change.
- **Save** keeps a named copy of where the ticks and balances stand. **Restore Points** lists
  them; picking one puts the reconciliation back to that point. The changes a restore makes are in
  History, where each can be undone.

## Finishing and the report

**Print Draft Report** prints the report at any time. Until the reconciliation is finished, every
page is marked DRAFT. The report lists the figures, the uncleared and cleared items, IOLTA
interest, the lines that need review, every note and the attached files.

**Finish Reconciliation** asks you to confirm the difference, how many lines are still uncleared
and how many need review. You can finish with a difference; the reconciliation is then marked
**Finished With a Difference** rather than **Reconciled**. Once it is finished the ticks and
balances can no longer change, but notes can still be added, and **Print Report** prints the final
report. The next reconciliation starts where this one ended.

## Who can use it

Anyone who can manage the firm's trust accounts can start, tick and finish a reconciliation.
Others can read it, but the buttons that change it are turned off, and pointing at one says why.
