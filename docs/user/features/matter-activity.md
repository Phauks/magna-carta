# Matter Activity

The Activity section is a matter's whole story in date order: every document added, every note
written, every hearing and deadline, time and costs booked, client money moved, items in the firm's
custody, conflicts checks, and the day the matter opened. It is the screen to open when you pick up
a file you have not touched in months, and the one to read from when you need to account for what
was done and when.

## Reading the Chronology

Open a matter and choose **Activity** from the **Record** menu on the section strip. The newest entry
is at the top. Each row shows:

- **Date** and **Time** — when the thing happened. A hearing shows the day it is held; a document
  shows the moment it was added; a note shows the day the thing it describes happened, where the
  writer recorded one.
- **Section** — which part of the matter the row belongs to: Files, Notes, Time, Costs, Calendar,
  Custodian, and so on.
- **Type** — what the thing is, in the words of the part of the matter that holds it. A document
  shows its document type where your firm has set one, otherwise what kind of file it is (a PDF
  document, a Word document, an archive). A hearing shows its event type. A note is a note.
- **Name** — what the thing is called: a file's name, a note's opening line, a time entry's
  narrative, a cost's description, an event's title.
- **Details** — the facts worth seeing beside it: a file's size, version and whether the scanner has
  held it back; a note's date and how many documents it points at; the hours on a time entry; the
  amount of a cost; where a hearing is held; whether a deadline is overdue.
- **Who** — the member who recorded it, where a person did.

## Finding Something

Sort by any column by pressing its heading. Right-click a heading to filter that column, hide it,
pin it so it stays put when the list scrolls sideways, or bring a hidden column back. The footer
says how many rows a filter is hiding.

- To see only one part of the matter, filter **Section**. Every section is offered whether or not
  the matter has anything in it yet.
- To see only one kind of thing, filter **Type**.
- To see only one person's work, filter **Who**.
- To see one period, filter **Date**.

The list shows the most recent entries first and loads more with **Load Older** at the foot of the
table. A filter narrows what has been loaded, so on a long matter load older entries first if what
you are looking for is far back.

## Opening a Row

Press a row, or select it and press Enter, to open the thing it describes in the section that holds
it. A row that cannot be opened yet says why when you hover over its name — the section that would
hold it is not built, or nothing on the matter holds that kind of record.

## Writing a Note From Here

**+ New Note** opens the same window the Notes section uses. Write the note, attach any of the
matter's documents, and save; the note takes its place in the chronology the moment it lands. See
[Matter Notes](matter-notes.md) for everything a note can carry.

## What You See, and What You Do Not

The Activity section shows you what you are allowed to read on the matter, and nothing else. If
your account cannot read the matter's notes, a note still appears as a row that says a note was
written, when, and by whom — but not what it said. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.

## What Activity Is Not

Activity keeps no record of its own. Every row is read from the part of the matter that holds it,
so what you see here can never disagree with the Files, Notes, Time or Calendar section. Deleting a
document or a note removes it from the chronology because the record itself is gone.

Nothing on the Activity section says a document was "filed". Filing means filing with a court, and
Magna Carta files nothing with a court on your behalf. A document is added to the matter, and an
email is saved to it.
