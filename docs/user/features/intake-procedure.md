# The Intake Procedure

An intake procedure is your firm's own list of what a kind of matter must clear before you consult.
Write one under **Settings** · **Intake Procedures**, and every intake of that kind shows the list
on its **Intake** section. The intake completes into a consultation once every required step is
answered, and the consultation opens on the firm's decision.

## What a Procedure Is

A procedure is bound to a kind of matter, or to every kind. A personal-injury consultation and an
estate consultation ask for different things, so a firm usually writes one procedure per kind and
perhaps one that applies to everything. A consultation is held to the procedures for its kind and
to every procedure bound to no kind.

Each procedure is a list of steps. A step has a title, optional instructions, and a kind. The kind
decides who answers the step:

- **Conflicts Check**: answered by the matter's conflicts record. The step is satisfied when a check
  has run at the firm's minimum, every surfaced hit has a reviewer's answer, and no re-check is owed.
  It cannot be marked done by hand; it can be waived.
- **Fee Agreement**: answered by the fee arrangement recorded from the matter's status window.
- **Engagement Letter** and **Document**: answered when a document is produced onto the matter from
  the template the step names, or by a member recording that the signed letter or the document came
  in. Leave the template blank for a document the client brings.
- **Retainer**: answered when client funds are held in trust for the matter, or by a member
  recording a retainer the trust ledger cannot see.
- **Questionnaire**: answered when the custom field the step names holds a value on the matter, or
  by a member recording that the questionnaire was completed.
- **Client Forms**: the forms the client fills in online have gone out and come back. Answered by
  a member recording it; the client portal will answer it when it exists.
- **Payment**: a payment sent or received before the firm acts, such as a consultation fee.
  Answered by a member recording it; payments will answer it when they are built.
- **Identity Verification** and **Acknowledgement**: answered by a member recording that they did
  it.

A step is **required** unless you say otherwise. A required step that is not answered stops the
intake completing; an optional step only shows.

## Writing a Procedure

1. Open **Settings** · **Intake Procedures** and press **New Procedure**.
2. Name it, say what it is for, and choose the kind of matter it binds.
3. Press **Steps**, then **Add Step**. Choose the kind, say what to do, and add instructions where
   the title is not enough. Untick **Required** for a step that should show but never hold the
   door.

Changing a procedure reaches every consultation of its kind at once. Anything already recorded on
a consultation stays as it was.

**Stop Requiring** takes a procedure out of force without removing it. Retiring a step does the
same for one step. Neither is deleted, because records on consultations still name them.

Only members whose role allows managing procedures can write them; everyone who can read a
consultation's intake can read the firm's procedures.

## Answering the Steps on an Intake

Open the intake and go to **Intake**. The line under the heading says how many required
steps are outstanding. Each step shows who answered it and what they found, in their own words.

- A step answered by the platform shows what it found. Where the fact is made somewhere else, the
  row offers the way there: **Conflicts** for the conflicts check, **Status** for the fee
  agreement, **Files** for a document, **Trust** for the retainer.
- **Record Done** records, under your name, that you did a step only a person can do. Add a note
  where it helps the next reader.
- **Waive** lets the matter open without a step. It asks for the reason, and the waiver stays on
  the record under your name. Only members whose role allows waiving see it.
- **Reopen** sets aside a recorded answer, so the step is answered again by the platform or by
  nobody. The earlier answer stays in the history.

Every answer ever recorded is kept. **Show the history** under the list reads them in order,
including the ones later set aside.

## Completing the Intake, and Opening the Matter

Press the status badge on an intake, then **Change Status**, and choose **Complete Intake** from
the steps on the left of the panel. On its right, **Requirements To Complete Intake** lists each
required step that is outstanding, in the words of
whoever should answer it, with a way through to the Intake section where they are answered; the
commit is available once the list is empty, and the same control stands on the Intake desk.
Pressing it while something is outstanding answers with the same list. Completing the intake draws
the consultation number.

A consultation opens the same way, with **Open Matter**, on the firm's decision to act; the
procedure does not gate it again. An intake of a kind no procedure binds has nothing standing
between it and the consultation.

## Tips and Edge Cases

- **The platform ships no procedures.** The list starts empty in every firm, because what a firm
  requires before it acts is the firm's own statement.
- **A waiver is never a completion.** It reads as a waiver on the record, with its reason, so it is
  never mistaken for the fact it stands in for.
- **Once the intake is complete**, its Intake section shows the record as it stood and offers no
  further changes, through the consultation and after the matter opens.
- **Two procedures on one kind** both apply; the section names which procedure each step belongs
  to.
