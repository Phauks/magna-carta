# Sharing files and folders

_Sharing is new. You can choose who outside the firm can see a file or folder, and what each person
can do with it, from a matter's **Files** page today. The place those people open what you shared,
the client portal, is still being built, so nobody outside the firm can sign in to see it yet._

## What it does

A matter's files are private to the firm until you share them. You can share one file, a folder, or
several things at once, with anyone in the firm's **Directory**: the client, opposing counsel, an
expert, co-counsel. Each share has its own role, so the same folder can be read-only for one person
and editable for another.

| Role       | What the person can do        |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Viewer** | Open and read                 |
| **Editor** | Change files and add new ones |

Downloading is a separate choice, on by default. **Commenter** joins the list once files have
comments.

## Seeing what is shared

- A shared file or folder carries a **people mark** after its name, in the file list and in the
  folder list on the left.
- The **Shared With** column lists the people who can see each row. Sort or filter by it like any
  other column. Turn it off from the column menu if you do not need it.
- Select a file and open the **Sharing** tab in the details panel on the right. Select a folder and
  its sharing is under its details.

## Sharing something

1. Right-click a file or folder (or a selection of several) and choose **Share**. The details panel
   opens on **Sharing** with the Directory search showing.
2. Find the person and pick them. They get what you selected as a **Viewer**.
3. Change their role with the menu beside their name. The **...** menu holds **Allow Download**,
   **Add End Date** and **Stop Sharing**.

A person must be in the Directory before you can share with them. That way every share names a
record the firm can run a conflicts check against.

## What a folder share covers

Sharing a folder shares the files directly inside it, including files added to it later. It does
not share the folders inside it: share those on their own when you mean to. Sharing **Home**, the top
of the matter, shares the files that are in no folder.

A file that someone can see because of its folder lists them under **Through** and the folder's
name. Change that on the folder, because it affects every file beside it.

## Reviewing everything at once

Choose **Sharing** in the list on the left, under the folders. It lists everyone who has anything
shared on the matter. Pick a person to see the matter's folders and files with what they can do
beside each one:

- a role menu where you shared the item with them (choose **No Access** to stop),
- **Viewer, through Pleadings** (for example) where a folder share reaches the file,
- **Share** where they cannot see it. Press it to share that item with them as a Viewer.

**Add Person** at the top picks someone from the Directory to start sharing with.

## When a share stops working

A share stops opening, without anyone deleting it, when:

- its end date passes (it shows **Ended**),
- the person who shared it leaves the firm or loses permission to share (it shows **Needs a new
  sharer**; change anything about the share and you become its sharer).

Stopping a share is immediate. The firm keeps a record of every share, change and stop.

## Who can share

Owners, attorneys and paralegals can share. Anyone else sees what is shared and the reason the
controls do not work for them.
