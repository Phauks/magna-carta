# Connecting your Outlook calendar

_Connecting an account is built. It is not yet in the settings navigation and nothing syncs yet: it
waits on your firm's workspace being registered with Microsoft, and on the sync rules that decide
which events are copied._

## What it does

You connect your own Microsoft 365 account to Magna Carta so that, once sync rules exist, your Magna
Carta events can also appear in your Outlook calendar. The connection is yours alone. Nobody else at
the firm can see it or use it, and nobody can connect an account for you.

Connecting copies nothing. No event moves in either direction until you choose which kinds of event
sync and to which of your Outlook calendars.

## Connecting

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connected Accounts**.
2. Press **Connect Outlook**. A window lists what you are allowing. Press **Allow**.
3. Microsoft asks you to choose an account and sign in. If you have more than one Microsoft account,
   choose the one whose calendar you use for work.
4. You are returned to Connected Accounts, which names the account you connected.

You have ten minutes to finish signing in, and you must finish in the same browser you started in.

## If it did not connect

- **Outlook was not connected.** Microsoft did not grant access to the calendar. If you did not
  cancel it yourself, your organization's Microsoft administrator may need to allow it.
- **The sign-in ran out of time.** Start again from Connected Accounts.
- **Outlook could not be connected.** Microsoft did not complete the sign-in, or the calendar could
  not be read afterwards. Try again; nothing was stored.

## Disconnecting

Press **Disconnect** on Connected Accounts. Magna Carta deletes its stored sign-in to the account.
Nothing in your Outlook calendar is changed. To connect again you sign in to Microsoft again.

You can also remove Magna Carta's access from the Microsoft side at any time, in your Microsoft
account's list of apps with access.

## Who can connect

Any member who can see the firm calendar.
