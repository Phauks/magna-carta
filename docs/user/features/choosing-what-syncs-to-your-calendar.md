# Choosing what syncs to your calendar

_Status: built, and switched off until your firm's Microsoft connection is set up. Events go from
Magna Carta to Outlook. A change you make in Outlook is not brought back yet, and repeating events
are not sent yet._

## What it does

After you [connect your Outlook account](connecting-your-outlook-calendar.md), you choose which
kinds of Magna Carta event are copied to your Outlook calendar, and which of your calendars each
kind goes to. Nothing is chosen for you. Until a row says otherwise, that kind of event is not
synced.

## Choosing

1. Open **Settings**, then **Calendar Sync**.
2. Every event type has its own row, and deadlines have one row. Press **Change** on a row.
3. In **Send To**, pick one of your Outlook calendars, or **Not Synced**.
4. In **Which Ones**, pick the events you take part in, or every one on a matter you are assigned
   to. For deadlines the first choice is the ones on matters you are responsible for.
5. Press **Save Changes**. The change applies at once.

Only calendars you can add events to are offered. A new event type your firm adds later appears as
a row that is not synced.

## What happens next

Your Outlook calendar is brought into line a few seconds after you save, and again after any
change to the calendar in Magna Carta.

- An event of a kind you chose appears in the calendar you picked. Editing it in Magna Carta
  updates the copy.
- Cancelling the event, setting the row back to **Not Synced**, or your firm stopping that kind
  from leaving removes the copy.
- A deadline appears as a whole-day entry on the day it is due, and is removed once it is no
  longer open.
- Nobody is invited. The copy is a plain entry in your own calendar, and Outlook emails no one.
- Events from a month back to two years ahead are kept in step.

## Repeating events

A repeating event goes to Outlook as one repeating event, not as a copy for each day. A day you
changed in Magna Carta appears as its own entry, and a day you called off is taken out of the series
in Outlook.

Outlook cannot repeat every rule on exactly the same days as Magna Carta. A monthly event on the
29th, 30th or 31st, a monthly event on a fifth weekday, a yearly event on 29 February, and a weekly
event whose first day is not one of its weekdays are not sent. Each appears on this page under
**Different in Outlook** as **Not sent**, with **Try Again** and **Dismiss**, so an event is never
quietly missing from your calendar.

## If you change a copy in Outlook

Magna Carta is the record, and an event there is shared by everyone on the matter. Moving, renaming
or deleting your copy in Outlook changes nothing in Magna Carta. Instead the copy appears on this
page under **Different in Outlook**, with what Magna Carta has beside what Outlook has, and two
answers:

- **Put Back** writes the event to Outlook again as Magna Carta has it. A deleted copy is made
  again.
- **Leave It** keeps Outlook as you made it. The copy is replaced only when the event next changes
  in Magna Carta, and a deleted copy stays deleted.

The section is absent when there is nothing to answer. It can take a few hours for a change you
made in Outlook to appear here.

A deadline is different: if you change a deadline's copy in Outlook, Magna Carta puts it back
without asking, so a deadline never reads a day off in your calendar. To change the event itself,
change it in Magna Carta.

If you disconnect your account, the copies already in Outlook stay there, and Magna Carta stops
updating them.

## What your firm decides

Your firm sets the outer limit. A row that reads **Not Allowed by the Firm** cannot be synced, and
a type your firm sends with the matter number and type only carries no title, location or notes
into Outlook. You can sync less than your firm allows and never more.

## If you cannot change a row

- **No account is connected.** Connect one in Connected Accounts first.
- **Outlook needs you to sign in again.** Connect again in Connected Accounts. Your choices are kept.
- **Your Outlook calendars could not be read.** Outlook did not answer. Your saved choices are
  shown and can be changed once it does.

## For administrators

Open **Settings**, then **Calendar Sync** under Practice. For each event type, and for deadlines,
press **Change** and choose whether it may leave Magna Carta and how much detail is sent. Stopping
a type from leaving removes every member's choice for it. If you allow it again later, each member
chooses again. Setting this needs the Owner/Admin role.
