# Your sidebar

The dark column down the left of the window is how you get everywhere. It is the same for everyone
at the firm to begin with, and then it is yours: you choose which rows appear, what order they sit
in, which groups stay open, and which pages you want at the very top.

## The groups

The rows are gathered under headings, and each heading answers one question.

- **Favorites** — pages you saved yourself. It only appears once you have saved one. See
  [Saving a page to Favorites](#saving-a-page-to-favorites).
- **My Desk** — what is on your plate: Home, Notifications, Tasks, Calendar and
  [Magnus](asking-magnus.md).
- **Workspace** — the firm's work: Matters, Intake, Conflicts Check, Directory, Documents,
  Forms, Discovery and Custodian.
- **Communications** — how the firm reaches people: [Messaging](calls-and-texts.md) and
  [Meetings](meetings.md).
- **Money** — Time, Fees, Costs, Billing and Trust.
- **Firm** — the firm about itself: Reports and Firm Usage.
- **Tools** — the utilities that are not about one matter. See [Tools](#tools).

**Click a heading to fold its group away**, and click it again to bring it back. The chevron beside
the heading points down while the group is open and sideways while it is folded. Folding is only
about the sidebar: everything in a folded group is still one search away.

Settings sits at the foot of the sidebar, alongside the controls for [Uploads](../uploads.md),
[Downloads](../downloads.md) and Customize.

## Tools

The tools are rows in the sidebar like anything else — there is no tools page to go to first. They
are gathered into four groups you can open and close:

- **Records** — Attorney License Lookup, Business Entity Lookup, County Property Records, Property
  Listing Links.
- **Internet** — Network Speed Test, Safe Website Viewer, WHOIS Lookup.
- **Mailing** — Shipping & Postage.
- **Files** — [File Workbench](file-workbench.md).

Each group lists its tools alphabetically. Click the group's name to open it; click a tool to go
straight to it, on the full width of the screen.

A tool marked **Soon** is shown rather than hidden, so you can see what is coming. It does not open
yet, and nothing about it pretends to work.

**To find a tool by what it does rather than what it is called**, use the search at the top of the
window (or press Ctrl+K, Cmd+K on a Mac). It searches everything in the product at once and knows
words the tools do not display: "bandwidth" reaches the Network Speed Test, "registrant" reaches the
WHOIS Lookup, and "ocr" reaches the File Workbench.

## Saving a page to Favorites

**Every screen has a star beside its title.** Click it and that page joins Favorites at the top of
your sidebar; click it again to remove it.

It works on any page, which is the point — a matter you are in every day, one tool you use and eight
you do not, a client's record, a section inside a matter. The name that appears is the name the page
had when you saved it, so if a matter is renamed later, open it and press the star again to update
what your sidebar calls it.

Favorites are kept in the browser you saved them in, so they do not follow you to another computer
yet.

## The page menu

**Beside the star is a menu (the three dots)** with what you can do with the page itself:

- **Copy Link** puts the page's address on your clipboard, ready to paste to a colleague.
- **Open in New Tab** opens the same page again in a second tab, so you can keep your place here.

Some pages add their own entries beneath those, such as **What You Hear About** on Notifications,
**Postage Account** on Shipping & Postage and **Clear History** on Uploads.

## Changing what you see

**Customize** is the sliders icon at the foot of the sidebar. While it is on, each group shows two
lists: the rows in your sidebar now, then the ones a click would bring back.

- **The eye** beside a row hides it or brings it back. A crossed-out eye means the row is not in your
  sidebar at the moment.
- **The star** beside a favorite removes it.
- **A padlock** means the row cannot be hidden — Home, Matters and Settings stay put.
- **Drag a row by the grip on its left** to move it within its group. You can also focus a row and
  press Alt with the up or down arrow.

Tools are not listed in Customize. Which tools you see is decided by what your firm has given you
permission to use, and that is not a preference. Fold the group away, or favorite the ones you want.

Hiding a row never removes what it does: the search at the top of the window still reaches every
page in the product, whether it is in your sidebar or not.

## Making the sidebar narrower

**Collapse** at the very foot of the sidebar shrinks it to a column of icons, giving the width back
to your work. Hovering an icon shows its name. In Settings you can choose **expand on hover**
instead, which keeps the sidebar narrow and floats it open when you point at it.
