# The community board

The community board, at `community.magnacarta.esq`, is where people at firms that use Magna Carta
ask for features, ask how to do something, and see what the Magna Carta team has decided. Anyone
can read it without signing in. Only signed-in members of a firm can post, vote, comment or report.

The board is about the software only. Nothing on it is ever copied from your matters, and nothing
you write there reaches your firm's data.

## Who you are on the board

You post under a **display name**, which can be any name you like, a pseudonym included. Your firm
is never shown, and nobody can see who voted for what. A vote count shows only once a request has
five votes, so an early vote cannot point to your firm.

To change your display name, open **New Post** and press **Change Name** beside the name shown.

## Signing in

1. On the board, press **Sign In**.
2. If you are not already signed in to Magna Carta, sign in there.
3. Magna Carta sends you back to the board, signed in.

The link that carries you across works once, for one minute. If you see "That sign-in link has
already been used or has run out", press **Sign in again**. **Sign Out** on the board signs you out
of the board only, not out of Magna Carta.

## Asking for a feature or asking a question

1. Press **New Post**.
2. Choose the **Topic**, the part of the software it is about.
3. Choose **Request** (something new, which others can vote on) or **Question** (how to do
   something today).
4. Write a title and the details, then post.

Your first post or comment is read by the Magna Carta team before anyone else sees it. Until then
only you can see it. After that, what you write goes up at once.

**Keep client detail out.** No client names, matter numbers, balances or screenshots. If the board
sees something that looks like client detail, it warns you before you post. If you post it anyway,
it is held until the team has read it.

## Voting, commenting and reporting

- **Vote** with the arrow beside a request. Press it again to take your vote back.
- **Comment** at the bottom of a post. Replies from the Magna Carta team are marked.
- **Mark as Answer**: on your own question, mark the reply that answered it.
- **Report** a post or comment that holds client detail, spam or abuse. A report of client detail
  hides it at once, until the team has looked.
- **Delete** your own post or comment. It is gone at once; there is no history of it kept.

## The roadmap

**Roadmap** shows the requests the team has taken on in five columns: Future, Planned, Building
now, Beta and Completed. There are no target dates, and a request can move back a column if plans
change. A request the team will not take on says why, under **Why Not**.

## Known issues

**Known Issues** shows what the Magna Carta status page says is wrong right now, with its latest
update. Check it before reporting a problem. A problem that involves your own firm's data belongs in a
report from inside Magna Carta, which reaches the team privately, never on the board.

## For the Magna Carta team

Members of the team see **Moderation** in the board's top bar. It lists every first contribution
waiting to be read and every post or comment hidden by reports, with the reports counted by reason.
**Publish** puts an item up; **Remove** blanks its text. On a request, **Team Triage** beside the
post sets its status; turning a request down needs a reason and a note, which everyone can read.
