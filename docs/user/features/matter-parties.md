# A matter's parties

Everyone connected to a matter is recorded on its **Parties** page: the client, the opposing party,
counsel, guardians, witnesses, experts. Each entry links a person or an organization from the firm's
Directory to the matter in a role, so the same record serves every matter that person appears on,
and a conflicts check can see who is on what.

## What you see

Open a matter and choose **Parties**. Each row shows the person or organization, the role they hold
on this matter, and the capacity they act in where one was recorded ("Administrator", "Trustee").
The name opens their Directory record.

One person can hold more than one role on the same matter. An executor who is also a beneficiary is
two rows, and each is removed on its own.

## Adding a party

1. Press **Add…**.
2. Start typing the name. After three characters the Directory is searched, and the matches appear
   in the firm's own order of relevance. Pick the one you mean. If the search found more than it can
   show, it says so; keep typing to narrow it.
3. Choose the role. The list holds the roles the firm uses; to record a role that is not in it,
   type it and choose the **Add** row.
4. Record the capacity if there is one. It is free text and is never filled in for you.
5. Press **Add**.

If the person or organization is not in the Directory yet, add them there first from the
**Directory** page, then return and pick them. Typing a name here never creates a record, which is
what keeps one client from ending up in the book twice under two spellings.

The page tells you when the person you picked is already on this matter and in which roles, and
it will not add the same person in the same role a second time.

## Removing a party

Press the unlink control at the end of the row and confirm. This removes the role from this matter
and nothing else: the person stays in the Directory with everything recorded about them, their other
roles on this matter are untouched, and a conflicts report that named them stays reproducible.

## Who can change the parties

Reading a matter's parties needs only access to the matter. Adding or removing one needs the
Directory edit permission. Without it the controls stay visible and say what would change the answer.
