# The trust compliance report policy

What your firm's trust compliance report lists is your firm's decision, made once, so that every
copy of the report reads the same way whoever produces it and whenever they do.

## Why it is the firm's setting

The trust compliance report is a document a firm hands to an examiner. Two reports for the same
period that list different things, because two people ticked different boxes, invite a question
about which one is the firm's account of its books. Deciding once, for everybody, means there is
one answer.

## The two settings

- **Ledgers at Zero All Period.** Whether a matter ledger that held nothing throughout the period
  is listed, at zero. Listed by default, so the report accounts for every ledger that existed.
  Omitting them makes a shorter document that lists only the ledgers with activity or a balance.
- **Who Authorized Each Payment.** Whether the ledgers and the journal name the member who
  authorized money leaving a client ledger. Named by default: the platform records it on every such
  payment for exactly this reason, and it is a strength in front of an examiner. It is also a
  personnel record going outside the firm, which is why a firm may choose to omit it.

## Changing them

Open **Settings**, and under **Practice** choose **Reports**. Choose the answers, then **Save** at
the bottom of the page. The change applies at once, to every report produced from then on. The
provenance section of each report states which way each setting was set when that report was
produced, so a report made before the change still says what it was made under.

Only a member who can manage the firm's report policy can change them. Everybody else sees the
firm's answers on the same page, stated as facts rather than as controls they cannot use. Every
change is written to the firm's audit trail, with who made it and when.
