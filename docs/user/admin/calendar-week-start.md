# The first day of the week

Every calendar in your firm begins its week on the same day. Which day that is, is your firm's
setting: it is not something each member chooses for themselves.

## Why it is the firm's setting

The week is the unit a firm plans in. When somebody says a deadline is "next week", or a partner
asks what is on the calendar this week, that has to mean the same seven days to everybody who
hears it. If one member's calendar began on Sunday and another's on Monday, the two would be
looking at different weeks while talking about the same one. So the firm decides once, and every
calendar, the firm's own and every matter's, draws its week the same way.

## What your firm starts with

**Sunday.** That is the week United States legal practice reads, and it is what every calendar
shows until an administrator changes it.

## Changing it

Open **Settings**, and under **Practice** choose **Calendar**. The page has one setting, **First
Day of the Week**, with two choices: Sunday or Monday. Choose the day, then **Save** at the bottom
of the page and confirm. The change applies at once, for everybody: the next time anybody opens a
calendar, or moves to another month, its week begins on the day you chose.

Only a member who can manage the calendar can change it. Everybody else sees the firm's answer on
the same page, stated as a fact rather than as a control they cannot use.

## What a change does and does not do

- **It moves the columns, not the dates.** A hearing on a Thursday is still on that Thursday; only
  which column Thursday sits in changes.
- **It is recorded.** Every change is written to the firm's audit trail, with who made it and when.
- **It does not change anybody's personal calendar outside the platform.** Your firm's Google or
  Outlook calendars keep their own settings.
