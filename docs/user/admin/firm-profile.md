# Firm Profile

Under **Settings › Firm › Firm Profile** an administrator keeps what the firm is: the name its
clients read, the marks its documents carry, and a view of the offices it practises from.

## The firm's name

The name is what a client sees on an invoice, in the portal, and on every generated document.
Change it in the box and press **Save**; new documents carry the new name from then on, and
documents already produced keep the name they were produced under.

## Brand images

The firm's marks: one **primary mark**, and any additional images the firm keeps beside it, such as
a seal or a wordmark. Every office's letterhead prints the primary mark unless that office has been
given a logo of its own, so a firm with one office sets its mark here once and never needs the
office's own.

Press **Add an Image**, choose a PNG or a JPEG of up to two megabytes, say whether it is the primary
mark or an additional image, and caption it if you like. Making an image the primary moves the mark
that was primary into the additional images rather than removing it; the star beside an additional
image does the same. Removing an image asks first. Documents already produced keep the mark they
were produced with; if the primary is removed, every office that printed it prints no mark until
another primary is set.

## Offices

The plate lists the firm's offices with how many members work at each and their standing. Each name
opens the office's record on the Offices screen, which is where offices are opened, corrected and
closed.

## Who can change it

A member whose role allows managing the firm's profile. Everyone else reads the page.
