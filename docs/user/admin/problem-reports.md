# Problem reports

When a member reports a problem from the app, the report comes to your firm's admins. Read and answer
them under **Settings › Data & Platform › Problem Reports**.

## Reading a report

The **Reports** list shows every report, newest first, with who sent it, when, and its status. Open
one to see the sender's words, the screenshot they sent (with anything they covered blacked out),
the page they were on, any Error ID, the app version, and the last steps they took in the app.

## Answering

Choose a **Status** and, if you like, write a **Note** to the sender, then press **Save and Notify**.

| Status          | Means                                                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Received        | The report arrived and nobody has answered it yet.                   |
| Looking Into It | Somebody is working on it.                                           |
| Fixed           | It is fixed. Say in the note what the sender should do, if anything. |
| Closed          | Done without a fix. Say why in the note.                             |

The sender gets a notification with the new status. The note is not in the notification; they read
it on their report under **My Reports**. The note is stored encrypted.

## Sending reports to Magna Carta

**Send Reports to Magna Carta** is on unless you turn it off. While it is on, Magna Carta also reads
the reports your members send, so it can fix what they find. Each time someone at Magna Carta opens
one of your reports, it is recorded in your firm's audit log.

Turning it off stops sending new reports and withdraws every report already sent: Magna Carta can no
longer open them. Turning it back on sends only reports made from then on.

## Who can do this

Reading and answering reports, and the sharing switch, need the `incidents:viewDescription`
permission, which the Owner and Admin roles hold. Every member can send a report and read their own.
