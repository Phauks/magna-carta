# File retention

When a matter closes, Magna Carta asks for the date the firm's paper file for it may be destroyed
after. Your firm can set the fewest years that date may be after the close. This page is that
setting.

## What your firm starts with

**No minimum (0 years).** Magna Carta never picks a retention period for you: how long a closed
file must be kept depends on the jurisdiction you practise in and on your firm's own policy, and a
period the platform chose would be a date somebody destroys a client's file on without anybody
having decided it.

## Changing it

Open **Settings**, and under **Practice** choose **File Retention**. The page has one setting,
**Minimum Retention**, a whole number of years from 0 to 100. Enter it, then **Save** at the bottom
of the page and confirm.

Only the Owner and Admin roles can change it: it carries the `matters:manageRetention` permission,
which no other role holds unless your firm grants it. Everybody else sees the firm's answer on the
same page.

## What the minimum does and does not do

- **It sets the earliest date a close will accept.** With a minimum of 7 years, a matter closed on
  9 November 2026 asks for a destruction date no earlier than 9 November 2033, and the close step
  opens on that date. A matter closed on 29 February counts forward to 1 March, so the period is
  never a day short.
- **A later date, or none, is always allowed.** The minimum is a floor, not a default anybody has
  to keep. **No Destruction Date** is always a choice when closing.
- **It does not reach back.** Dates already recorded stay as they are. The minimum applies the next
  time a date is stated: at a close, or when a closed matter's date is changed on its status window.
- **Nothing is destroyed or deleted when a date arrives.** The date is a reminder on the matter's
  status window and on the firm calendar under **File Destruction Dates**. Destroying the file is
  still a person's act.
- **It is recorded.** Every change to the setting is written to the firm's audit trail, with who
  made it and when.
