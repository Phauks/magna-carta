# Editing

What every document edited in the browser keeps a record of is your firm's decision, made once
under **Settings › Documents › Editing**. There is one setting today: whether every document
records its changes.

## Recording changes

**Record Changes in Every Document** is on for every firm until an administrator changes it. While
it is on:

- Every document opened in the browser editor records who changed what, from the first edit. The
  editor's own control for turning recording off is taken away, so nobody can open a document and
  quietly edit it unrecorded.
- The marks are hidden when a document opens. The page reads the way it always did, and typing
  looks the way it always did. A member who wants to see the record opens the editor's **Review**
  tab and turns **Show Track Changes** on; that choice is theirs and changes nothing for anybody
  else.
- The stored document keeps every mark for as long as it lives. Every copy that leaves the firm —
  a download, a bundle — is produced with the marks accepted, so nobody sends a redline they did
  not mean to. A document too large for that copy to be made downloads as stored, and the audit
  trail records that it did.

Recording is what the version history's attribution will be read from. A version records who was in
the document when it was saved; the marks record which words each of them wrote.

## Switching it off

Turn the switch off, then **Save** at the bottom of the page. The page asks you to confirm, because
what was not recorded cannot be recovered later. The change is written to your firm's audit log and
takes effect the next time anybody opens a document: a document already open keeps recording until
it is closed.

With recording off, the editor behaves as it always did. Each document's own setting decides
whether it records, and members can turn it on or off themselves.

## Who can change it

Only a member whose role carries the right to manage the editing policy, which the firm's Owner and
Administrator roles hold. Everybody else sees the firm's answer on the same page, stated as a fact
rather than as a control they cannot use.
