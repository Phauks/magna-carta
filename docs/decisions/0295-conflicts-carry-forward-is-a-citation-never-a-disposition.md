**A re-surfaced conflicts hit may cite an earlier conclusion; it may never inherit one. Nothing is recorded as concluded without a live human's name on the row.**

**Context:** The shipped engine has no re-surfacing problem because it only fires when a person asks. Event-driven and periodic re-checking creates one immediately: a weekly re-scan over an open matter re-surfaces every hit a reviewer worked through last month, and a reviewer facing a queue that is ninety percent already-answered stops reading the queue. That is the same failure as reporting nothing, reached by a longer route, and it is the failure the whole subsystem exists to prevent. The tempting fix is to let an evidence-identical re-surfaced hit inherit the prior disposition automatically. It is the wrong fix for a specific reason: the disposition table's value is that every conclusion carries the identity, rationale, and timestamp of the person who reached it, and an inherited row records a named reviewer as having concluded something on a day they did not, about a check they never saw.

**Decision:** (1) **Three derived states, nothing stored, nothing updated in place.** `undisposed` (no disposition row names this hit), `disposed` (the latest row by `created_at`, `rowid` — the shipped projection), and `answered-before` (undisposed, but a prior hit on the same matter with identical evidence carries a disposition). (2) **`answered-before` requires byte-identical evidence** — same entity, same tier, same matched-field set, same connecting path, same edge-expiry facts. **Any strengthening makes it a fresh question**: a stronger tier, a new matched field, a new or shorter path. Strengthening evidence is precisely the circumstance in which the earlier conclusion is worthless, so the grouping must not survive it. (3) **Bulk affirmation makes the human cheap, not optional.** A reviewer clears the whole `answered-before` group in one action, and it writes **one real `conflicts_disposition` row per hit**, each naming the **affirming** member as reviewer, each with a rationale, and each carrying a new nullable `carried_from_disposition_id` pointing at the conclusion it re-affirms. The reviewer's effort collapses; the record does not. (4) **No schema violence.** `carried_from_disposition_id` is added by `ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN`, which SQLite performs without a rebuild and which leaves the append-only `RAISE(ABORT)` triggers intact. **No `DISPOSITION_KINDS` value is added** and the `kind` CHECK is not widened — widening it would rebuild a growing append-only table for a vocabulary the design does not need, since a carry-forward is an ordinary conclusion that happens to cite an earlier one. (5) **Two structural layers stop the machine concluding anything.** A source-scan fitness function fails the build if either re-check consumer imports `disposition.ts`, and a `BEFORE INSERT` trigger refuses a `conflicts_disposition` row whose `reviewer_user_id` does not name a live member. Two layers, because "the automated consumer must not clear anything" is exactly the kind of rule that survives review and dies in a refactor. (6) **The conservative mode is built too.** `re-review` — no grouping, every re-surfaced hit fresh — is a legitimate firm posture; the mode in force is a firm setting recorded on the check.

**Considered & rejected:** _auto-inheriting the prior disposition onto an identical re-surfaced hit_ (records a conclusion its named reviewer did not make; the audit trail stops meaning what it says); _a `carried_forward` disposition kind_ (widens a CHECK on a growing append-only table, and misdescribes the act — a person really did conclude, they simply cited); _a mutable `disposition` column on the hit_ (already rejected by ADR-0192 for destroying the prior value, and re-surfacing makes it worse); _grouping on entity identity rather than on evidence identity_ (the merge that just made the hit tier 1 would be hidden under last month's tier-4 clearance — the single most dangerous behaviour available here); _no grouping at all as the only mode_ (correct in principle and, over a weekly re-scan, the mechanism by which a reviewer stops reading).

**Consequences:** Automatic re-checking becomes survivable without the platform ever concluding anything. Every row in the disposition table continues to answer "who decided this, when, and why", including the re-affirmations, and the `carried_from_disposition_id` chain shows how a long-running matter's conclusions were maintained. **F4** (which carry-forward mode is the default) and **F5** (whether tier-1 hits require a designated attorney rather than any `conflicts:dispose` holder) are owner decisions; both ship as settings with the policy in force recorded on the disposition row. Deepens **ADR-0192** (the dispositions-are-a-table-not-a-column decision, extended across re-surfacing); depends on the event-driven re-check ledger decision recorded alongside it.
