**A conflicts check reports; it does not certify. The platform may assert facts about its own search and never a fact about the world.**

**Context:** Slice 4·1 (ADR-0192) built an engine whose write side is already honest — no score, no threshold, the scope in force recorded verbatim on every run, and `unavailable` deliberately distinguished from `searched and clean`. Its read side has no shape of its own: a caller reads a check row and its hits and forms its own conclusion. The first surface that renders "0 hits" as a green tick will have made the platform say something no one designed it to say, and the sentence it says — that a matter is conflict-free — is one a firm cannot defend on a disqualification motion. What a firm can defend is a procedure: which channels ran, over what corpus, to what depth, with which sources unavailable and why. That is what Rule 1.10(e) asks a firm to maintain, and it is a description of the search rather than a claim about the matter.

**Decision:** (1) **One derived posture read** is the sanctioned way to ask where a matter stands: the latest check with its scope in force verbatim, the **shortfall** between that scope and the firm's current policy, open re-check causes, undisposed hit counts by evidence tier, disposition counts each naming their reviewer, every source that recorded `unavailable` with its reason, and a `neverChecked` state that is distinct from a check that surfaced nothing. (2) **There is no aggregate and no boolean.** No field is named `clear`, `clean`, `ok`, `passed`, `status`, `severity`, or `conflictFree`. A caller that wants one word must write it itself, and the platform never supplies it. (3) **Staleness is derived, never stored.** The scope shortfall and the pending re-checks are computed at read time against the live policy row and the live re-check ledger; nothing writes a judgement onto a record where it can rot. This is the posture `packages/calendar/src/overdue.ts` already holds for an overdue deadline and `readDormantTrustBalances` holds for a dormant trust balance. (4) **A zero-hit check renders as a statement of the search** — the channels that ran, the strictness and budgets in force, the entity counts reached, the sources that could not run and why, ending "Nothing surfaced." The last line is a fact about the machine. (5) **The discipline is a fitness function, not a convention.** `lint:conflicts-vocabulary`, modelled on the shipped `tooling/lint-custodian-vocabulary.mjs`, scans the package, the routes, the OpenAPI descriptions, the web surfaces and `docs/user/` for assertion vocabulary and for response fields whose names claim a verdict, and fails the build. `cleared` survives only as a `DISPOSITION_KINDS` value, where it names one human's conclusion about one hit and always carries that human's identity and rationale.

**Considered & rejected:** _a single severity or status field on the posture read_ (it is the verdict wearing a different name, and every consumer would render it as one); _storing staleness as a flag on the check row_ (the check is append-only for good reason, and a stored flag would need a sweep to maintain and would be wrong between runs); _leaving the vocabulary to a convention document and code review_ (the rule survives review and dies in a refactor — the custodian lint exists for exactly this reason); _forbidding the word `cleared` outright_ (a reviewer clearing a hit is a real and necessary act; what must never happen is the platform clearing a matter).

**Consequences:** Every later surface inherits a shape that cannot render a verdict, which is why this lands before the trigger and depth work rather than after it. A firm gains a per-matter answer it cannot get today without reading raw rows, including the two facts that matter most and are currently invisible: that an answer predates the firm's current policy, and that the corpus has moved since the answer was given. The cost is one read model, one lint, and the standing discipline that a conflicts response is a report. Deepens **ADR-0192**; serves **ADR-0013**.
