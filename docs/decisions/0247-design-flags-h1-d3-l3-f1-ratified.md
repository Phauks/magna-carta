**The four highest-gating design flags ratified as recommended; B1 ratified by acknowledged silence.**

**Context:** The five subsystem designs merged on 2026-08-05 (merge-field catalog, matter assets, case
calendar, auto-file rules, billing) carried fifty owner flags, each with a recommended default. Builds
the flags did not gate proceeded on those defaults; the four that gated the most work — H1, D3, L3,
F1 — were held for the owner.

**Decision:** On 2026-08-06 the owner ratified all four as recommended. H1 unlocks the catalog's
staffing/court/estate slices as platform schema. D3 is executed promptly because its freedom is
time-bound. L3 and F1 confirm shipped shapes and change no code.
