summary: Slice 3·3 lands a generalized recoverable-charge seam — a canonical `RecoverableChargeEvent`/`RecoverableChargeLine` a producer emits instead of reaching into the cost-entry tables. A line's `kind` is the closed distinction that drives recovery: `pass_through` (a third-party charge the firm fronted for the client) is ALWAYS recovered as a client disbursement; `platform_fee` (a Magna-Carta-absorbed fee such as e-signature, messaging, or shipping handling) is recovered only when a per-category firm policy `cost.recover_platform_fee.<category>` (default off) is on. **A neutral contract for any fee a producer fronts.** A subsystem that incurs a billable charge on a matter's behalf — a purchased shipment today, an e-signature send or an outbound message tomorrow — should not reach into the `cost_entry` tables to record a disbursement; that couples every fee source to the cost schema. **Decision:** producers emit a canonical **`RecoverableChargeEvent`** carrying one or more **`RecoverableChargeLine`** items (a `matterRef`, a `currency`, a `sourceRef`/`idempotencyKey`, and per line a `category`, integer-minor `amountMinor`, `kind`, and `description`); the mapping into cost captures lives downstream in `@magna-carta/costs`, not in the producer. `category` is an OPEN string (a new fee category needs no edit to the union); **`kind` is the CLOSED recovery distinction.** A **`pass_through`** line is a third-party cost the firm merely fronted for the client and is ALWAYS recovered as a client disbursement; a **`platform_fee`** line is a Magna-Carta-absorbed fee (e-signature, messaging, shipping handling) the firm MAY pass on, recovered only when its per-category firm policy `cost.recover_platform_fee.<category>` (default **off**, one setting per category) is on. The pure logic is **`shouldRecover(line, policyOn)`** (`pass_through ? true : policyOn`) and **`chargeToCaptureInputs(event, policy, incurredDate)`**, which skips non-recovered lines, maps recovered lines to `source: "recovery"` hard-cost captures, and derives a per-line composite `recoveryRef` of `${sourceRef}:${category}`; a `null` `matterRef` (a firm-administrative charge with nowhere to disburse) emits nothing. **THE BOUNDARY, stated explicitly:** 3·3 builds the firm→client recovery **CAPTURE only** — a WIP cost entry appears on the matter. The firm→client **BILLING** of that captured cost (cost → A/R → invoice → payment) is **3·4** and is not touched here; the ledger is never written in this slice. And the **MC→firm recoupment** of absorbed platform fees — how Magna Carta recovers its own e-signature/messaging/shipping costs back from firms — is a distinct, **not-yet-designed** future direction; the `platform_fee` kind names the absorbed cost but this slice does not bill the firm for it. **Considered & rejected:** *let each producer write cost rows directly* (rejected — couples every fee source to the cost schema and scatters the recovery decision); *a single boolean "recover?" flag instead of `pass_through`/`platform_fee`* (rejected — the always-recover third-party case and the policy-gated absorbed-fee case are genuinely different defaults; collapsing them loses the firm-favourable default on absorbed fees). **Consequences:** new fee sources plug in with their own producer adapter and need no change to the recovery decision; the recovery policy is one category-keyed firm setting; the firm→client billing path and the MC→firm recoupment path remain open, named seams.
