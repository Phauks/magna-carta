**Company email & account-of-record on Google Workspace (`magnacarta.esq`) — the firm's operational mailboxes and the identity that _owns_ third-party accounts run on Google Workspace: one paid human seat with role addresses (`ops@`/`billing@`/`support@`/`legal@`/`privacy@`/`security@`, plus `postmaster@`/`abuse@`) as free aliases forwarding into it. Two invariants bind: (1) recovery stays off the domain it depends on — the Cloudflare account and the `.esq` registrar keep an independent break-glass recovery mailbox _outside_ `magnacarta.esq` (their mail only resolves while Cloudflare DNS is up, so a same-domain recovery is a lockout loop), while accounts that don't serve this DNS (GitHub, EasyPost, Google Cloud, npm) move fully onto `ops@`; (2) infra/API logins use role addresses, never a personal `name@`. Distinct from the product's transactional sending (`no-reply@`, Cloudflare Email Sending, ADR-0074) — the two co-tenant the one DNS zone and are coordinated so neither clobbers the other's MX/SPF/DMARC.** **Context:** as the program acquires third-party services, the founder needs durable, recoverable, role-based logins plus a professional correspondence address; today those accounts sit on a personal mailbox — a bus-factor and account-recovery risk. `.esq` is a real Google-Registry TLD (legal-profession eligibility), already the canonical app domain; `magnacarta.esq` is owned. **Decision:** Google Workspace, over Microsoft 365 and over Cloudflare Email Routing + Gmail Send-As — chosen for compliance/audit maturity (enforced 2FA, audit log, retention, BAA — material to a legal-records product's future attestations, ADR-0090 perimeter) and a unified Google dev identity (the `.esq` registry, Google Cloud / OAuth verification, and the deferred Gmail/Calendar adapter of ADR-0092 all live in an owned Workspace org). **Considered & rejected:** Microsoft 365 (equal capability, no ecosystem alignment unless Microsoft Graph becomes the dominant integration); Cloudflare Email Routing + Gmail Send-As (free, but receive-only forwarding into one personal Gmail — weakest exactly for account-of-record: re-creates the single-human bus factor and risks the same-domain recovery loop); Migadu / Zoho / Fastmail (flat-cost unlimited real mailboxes, fully viable and product-neutral, but forgo the audit/BAA maturity and the Google dev-identity convenience — recorded as the rational budget alternative). **Consequences:** a small recurring per-seat cost (tracked in [`docs/operating-costs.md`](operating-costs.md)) buys a clean recovery/handoff story and an attestation-ready identity; the company mailbox choice is **decoupled from the product's email features** — the product sends via Cloudflare Email Sending (ADR-0074) and integrates with each firm's _own_ Gmail/Outlook (ADR-0092), never routing through the company Workspace; the only seam is the convenience of an owned Workspace org when building/testing the Google adapter's admin-consent path. Runbook + the two invariants + the §2 DNS-coexistence steps live in [`docs/conventions/operations.md`](conventions/operations.md) (_Company email & account-of-record_). The product email architecture ("what email means for users" — the per-user mailbox-connect adapter and a candidate inbound email-to-matter capture) is a separate, deferred design thread. Relates to **ADR-0074** (product email sending), **ADR-0092** (the per-user email/calendar adapter), **ADR-0090** (legal/attestation perimeter; trademark of `magnacarta.esq`), **ADR-0084** (secrets posture). **Status Accepted** (operational decision; setup is an owner runbook, not engineering work).
