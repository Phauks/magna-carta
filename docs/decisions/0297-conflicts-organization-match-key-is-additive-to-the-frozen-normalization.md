**Organization-name matching gains two additive derived keys; the frozen normalization is not touched, and no tier CHECK is widened.**

**Context:** The owner named the case directly: firms are `Smith & Jones LLP` on one record and `Smith and Jones, L.L.P.` on another. Run both through the shipped `normalize-name.ts` — NFC, case-fold, diacritic and punctuation strip, suffix and title parse — and they land as `smith jones llp` and `smith and jones llp`. No tier-2 match. The pair will probably surface at tier 4 on trigram overlap, buried under every other `smith` in the corpus, which is the "reports nothing useful" failure wearing a different costume. The obvious repair — teach `normalizeName` about ampersands and entity forms — is not available: `normalized_display` is computed at write time and stored across the whole corpus, and it is load-bearing for merge, for entry-time duplicate detection, and for the tier-2 exact-name probe, so changing it silently changes what tier 2 means and requires recomputing every name row before anything is consistent again.

**Decision:** (1) **Two derived columns beside `normalized_display`, never in place of it**, computed at write time in the same batch as the phonetic codes and by the same discipline, indexed, and backfilled once by a resumable budgeted sweep with a fitness probe mirroring `everyNameHasPhoneticCodes`. (2) **`normalized_match_key`** layers on the frozen output: `&` and `and` unified to one token; legal-entity forms canonicalized to a single token each (`l.l.p.`/`llp`/`limited liability partnership`; `inc`/`incorporated`; `corp`/`corporation`; `ltd`/`limited`; `plc`; `co`/`company`; `pc`; `pllc`; `lp`; `llc`); a leading `the` dropped; whitespace collapsed. The **legal form is preserved**, so only spelling is canonicalized — which is why a match here is **tier 2**. `Smith & Jones LLP` and `Smith and Jones, L.L.P.` are the same name written two ways and the engine should say so plainly. (3) **`normalized_match_key_bare`** is the same with the legal form **removed**, and a match is **tier 3**: `Smith & Jones LLP` and `Smith & Jones LLC` may be one firm re-formed or two unrelated legal persons, and surfacing the question at a weaker tier is the honest answer where equating them would not be. (4) **The tier CHECK is not widened.** `conflicts_hit.evidence_tier` carries `CHECK (evidence_tier BETWEEN 1 AND 4)`, and widening a CHECK on a growing append-only table is a rebuild the additive-only migration lineage forbids; the new signals map into the existing four and name themselves in `matched_fields_json`, which is where a reviewer reads *what* matched anyway. (5) **Both probes are strictness-scoped and additive**, applying the same merge-resolution `COALESCE`, the same deleted-entity exclusion, and the same name-role scoping the shipped exact-name probe applies, so `over_inclusive` remains a strict superset of `balanced` by construction.

**Considered & rejected:** _changing `normalize-name.ts`_ (corpus-wide recompute, silently redefines tier 2, and breaks the merge and duplicate-detection callers that share it); _one key that both canonicalizes and strips the entity form_ (collapses two genuinely different questions — "written differently" and "possibly re-formed" — into one tier, and the second is not tier-2 evidence); _widening the tier range to five or six_ (a rebuild of an append-only table for an ordering nicety, when `matched_fields_json` already carries the specific signal); _handling ampersands in the query rather than in a stored key_ (unindexed, so it becomes a scan per subject and cannot serve as a blocking channel); _a general string-similarity comparator over organization names_ (a threshold by another name, refused by the no-scorer decision).

**Consequences:** The owner's example matches at tier 2, and the re-formed-entity case surfaces at tier 3 rather than not at all. `normalized_display` and everything built on it are untouched. The costs are two columns, two indexes, one backfill sweep, two blocking probes, and a canonical-form table that grows by one line per legal form. Person-name breadth is a separate matter: nickname equivalence needs a curated corpus and ships **registered and empty**, so every check records `nickname_equivalence: unavailable` rather than omitting the dimension, and whether the `balanced` default should include former, maiden and alias name rows is **F1**, an owner decision shipped as a setting. Deepens **ADR-0192**; builds on **ADR-0085** (the frozen normalization and the write-time derivation discipline), **ADR-0086** (merge resolution in the match probes).
