**Post-quantum cryptographic posture — agility-first; the symmetric corpus is already quantum-safe; signatures deferred behind seams.** **Context:** the encrypted secret set (PII, plugin credentials, TOTP seeds) and the decades-long confidentiality lifetime of legal data (estate/sealed matters) raise the **harvest-now-decrypt-later (HNDL)** question. **Decision:** treat post-quantum readiness as primarily an _agility_ property, not a cipher swap. (1) **Keep AES-256 as the bulk cipher** — symmetric/hash crypto (AES-256-GCM, AES-KW, HKDF, HMAC) is not broken by Shor and only halved by Grover (AES-256 → 128-bit, infeasible; CNSA 2.0 approves AES-256), so the at-rest corpus needs **no migration** and there is no "more quantum-resistant" bulk cipher to adopt. (2) **HNDL is a transport threat**, met by Cloudflare's hybrid **X25519MLKEM768** TLS — on for inbound and, by default, Worker→origin egress (Phase-2 "PQC-only" downgrade protection targeted mid-2026); third-party plugin endpoints that won't yet negotiate PQC TLS are an inherited gap that is **logged**, not silently accepted. (3) **Public-key signatures** (passkeys/WebAuthn, the PAdES e-signature seal) are forge-able only once a quantum computer exists — a _migrate-before-then_, not retroactive, risk — so they are **seamed but deferred**, riding upstream standards (FIPS 204 ML-DSA / 205 SLH-DSA / 206 FN-DSA; COSE algorithm IDs for the WebAuthn path; ETSI/CA support for PAdES ML-DSA, est. 2026+). (4) **Crypto-agility is the mechanism**: every ciphertext, wrapped key, and signature carries an explicit **algorithm-version identifier**, making a future hybrid **ML-KEM** key-wrap or **ML-DSA/SLH-DSA** signature a versioned rotation behind the existing `protected()` DAL chokepoint, the envelope-rotation machinery (ADR-0026), and the e-signature port (ADR-0045) — never a rewrite. (5) **Prefer symmetric where there is a choice** — session signing stays HMAC (already quantum-safe); AES stays **256-bit everywhere** and KDF/HMAC hashing moves to **SHA-384** for CNSA-2.0 margin where free; the deferred external-KMS-rooted KEK (ADR-0026 _Honest limits_) gains "supports PQC-wrapped keys" as a selection criterion. **Considered & rejected:** swapping the bulk cipher to **ChaCha20-Poly1305** or **AES-256-GCM-SIV** (equally quantum-safe — a performance / nonce-misuse-robustness choice, not a quantum one, and not natively in Workers Web Crypto); adopting app-layer **ML-KEM now** (the app KEK is a symmetric Worker secret wrapped by AES-KW — there is no asymmetric key-establishment to harden until an asymmetric KEK is introduced); **doing nothing** until standards settle (forfeits the cheap agility seam and leaves transport HNDL unaddressed). **Consequences:** at-rest data is quantum-safe today; transport HNDL is mitigated by the platform; the only real migration work (signatures, an asymmetric KEK) is bounded, standards-gated, and reduced to adapter / field-version swaps; the cost is the discipline of carrying algorithm identifiers and the AES-256 / SHA-384 mandate. Extends **ADR-0026** (encryption & keys); touches **ADR-0004** (auth/passkeys) and **ADR-0045** (e-signature seal).
