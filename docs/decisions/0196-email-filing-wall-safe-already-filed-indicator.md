**Linking an email to a matter passes through a filing-time conflict/ethical-wall hook (placed now, no-op now), and the "already filed" and "also on" reads are wall-safe by construction — they return only matters the caller can access and never reveal a walled matter's existence.**

**Context:** The subsystem brainstorm ([`docs/specs/2026-06-16-email-filing-design.md`](../specs/2026-06-16-email-filing-design.md)) named two cross-cutting correctness properties for filing. First, a **filing-time conflict/ethical-wall check** is the subsystem's differentiator (no practice-management product does this) — but the conflicts engine is Phase 4 ([ADR-0013](0013-conflicts-conflicts-firm-wide-configurable-strictness-depth.md), realized in slice 4·1) and ethical-wall *enforcement* is still deferred. Second, cross-user dedup means a second person filing the same email must see it is **already filed** rather than double-file (the NetDocuments Global Filing Indicator, done right) — and that indicator must not leak a matter the viewer is walled from ([ADR-0052](0052-architecture-url-navigation-url-navigation-architecture.md), absence-not-existence). "Build when in the area" (the parent design's seam list) says place the cheap-now/expensive-to-retrofit call site now even where the machinery behind it is later.

**Decision:** (1) **A filing-time conflict/ethical-wall hook is called at the link site.** Every promotion of an `email_matter_link` (stage → filed) invokes a hook that today returns "no conflict" and is a no-op; in Phase 4 the conflicts engine drops in behind it, matching the matter's parties and the email's participants against the blind index without decrypting. Placing the call site now means the engine is a body swap, not a new call path through the capture code, and a fitness function can assert the dangerous act (filing) cannot happen without traversing the seam. (2) **The "already filed" read is wall-safe by construction.** For a message the firm has captured, `GET /api/v1/emails/already-filed?messageId=` (or by content hash) returns the matters it is filed to **filtered to the ones the caller can access** — the query is passed the matter ids the caller may see and never returns others. A caller with access to none of them receives a generic **"already captured by your firm"** with no matter identity, count, or name. The signal answers "should I file this again?" without ever becoming an oracle for a walled matter's existence. (3) **The same rule governs the multi-matter "also on ..." list.** Opening an email through one matter shows the other matters it is linked to **filtered to the caller's access**; a link to a walled matter is invisible to those off the wall — the email is seen through the lens of each matter you can access, never as one global object. (4) **The indicator ships in this slice, the enforcement machinery does not.** This is the read the add-in's "Already filed to {Matter}" badge will consume, built now because the dedup logic that backs it is built now. The conflict engine (Phase 4), the auto-file "DKIM-authenticated sender" guardrail (gated on the DKIM verdict seam *and* the add-in, since forwarding breaks the original DKIM), and ethical-wall enforcement are later; only their seams are placed.

**Considered & rejected:** returning the filed-to matter name whenever the message is captured, regardless of the caller's access (turns the anti-double-file signal into a walled-matter existence oracle — the exact leak [ADR-0052](0052-architecture-url-navigation-url-navigation-architecture.md) forbids); suppressing the "already captured" signal entirely for a caller with no matter access (then two people on the same email both file blind and the cross-user dedup value is lost — the generic wall-safe form preserves the anti-double-file signal without the leak); deferring the conflict-hook call site until Phase 4 (retrofitting a chokepoint into an existing capture path is the expensive-to-add case "build when in the area" exists to prevent, and it leaves a window where filing bypasses the check with no seam to fail on); building even a partial conflict check now (the engine is a Phase-4 subsystem with its own corpus and slice — a stub here would diverge).

**Consequences:** The add-in slice's "already filed" badge and the firm's cross-user double-file protection are available at launch, wall-safe. When the Phase-4 conflicts engine lands, filing-time checking is a body swap behind the placed hook with no capture-path change. The DKIM-verdict field stays `unverified` until its verifier is built; the auto-file guardrail that depends on it waits on both the verdict and the add-in. **Records the seam and the wall-safe rule** for the parent design's reserved conflict-check + already-filed ADR; the full filing-time conflict engine is deferred to Phase 4.
