**Slice 3·1 rate engine.** A `rate_card` holds named cards; `rate` rows are **append-only effective-dated versions** keyed by `(rate_card_id, scope_tier, scoped_id)` with `[valid_from, valid_to)`. `resolveRate` walks **per-entry override → matter → client → timekeeper → role → card default** and returns a `RateSource` — a superset of `rate.tier` (`"override" | "matter" | "client" | "timekeeper" | "role" | "card_default"`) where `override` and `card_default` are resolution outcomes, not stored tier rows. The resolved `rate_source` is captured alongside `rate_e4` and `rate_card_id` on the entry, making the resolution path auditable and re-derivable. `clientId` is derived from the matter and `roleId` from the timekeeper's member at capture. **Effective-dating:** the entry snapshots the resolved `rate_e4` on its `work_date` and keeps it forever — changing a card later never silently re-rates an existing entry (the white-paper "rate in force on its date" rule; the documented LEAP no-backfill bug we avoid). **Retroactive correction:** amending a past period inserts a new dated version; **billed entries never change** (corrections are new adjusting entries at billing); draft/submitted/approved entries keep their captured rate, with an opt-in **`reRateUnbilledDrafts`** action (gated by `time:manageRates`, audited, batched) so amounts never move silently. **Integrity:** closed-interval non-overlap is enforced in the service (appending closes the prior open row's `valid_to`; a `valid_from` inside an existing interval is refused) since SQLite cannot express a range-exclusion constraint — a fitness test asserts no overlap, so resolution always matches exactly one interval. **No-rate path:** `rate_e4`/`amount_minor` are nullable (null for non-billable internal time); time on a flat-fee/contingency matter still resolves and captures the *standard* rate for the realization report, even though 3·4 won't bill it hourly — so null means "non-billable," never "flat-fee." `currency` (ISO-4217) is stored on the entry and the rate so a `@magna-carta/money` Rate/Money is reconstructable (single-currency-per-entry today; multi-currency is a row-value change, not a migration). **Considered & rejected:** *auto-re-rate all unbilled drafts on a card amendment* (rejected — silently moves amounts, hard to audit); *forward-only corrections* (rejected — no clean past-fix path); *a single mutable current rate* (rejected — breaks effective-dating and history).
