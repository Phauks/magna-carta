**Time, dates & timezones — model time by kind on the Temporal API: instants in UTC, civil dates/deadlines as PlainDate + jurisdiction zone, IANA zones never fixed offsets; zone homes on Location + per-user display; enforced by one formatting layer + a coherence fitness function.** **Context:** legal work is deadline-driven and multi-jurisdiction; storing everything as one universal timestamp invites off-by-one-DST defects in docketing and inconsistent rendering across offices and timezones; this is the first entry in the new _Foundational data & runtime conventions_ section. **Decision:** adopt the **Temporal API** (TC39 Stage 4 / ES2026 — native in current Chromium/Firefox/Edge, polyfilled elsewhere and in the Workers runtime) and model time **by kind**: **instants** (audit, `created_at`, ledger posting, message sent, login) stored **UTC** (`Temporal.Instant`), never local; **civil dates / deadlines** (filing / statute-of-limitations / hearing dates, reconciliation periods) stored as **`Temporal.PlainDate` + an explicit jurisdiction zone**, never a UTC timestamp, with docketing (_Calendar and rules-based docketing_) computing in the **court's zone** against its business-day/holiday calendar; **wall-clock recurrences** as local-time + zone, materialized per occurrence; **durations** (time entries, ADR-0033) as quantities (minutes). **IANA zone identifiers, never fixed offsets** (offsets break across DST). **Zone homes:** each **Location** carries an IANA zone (ADR-0031; surfaced via settings, ADR-0040), each **user** a display-zone preference; a deadline keys off the court/jurisdiction zone, not the viewer's. **Enforced, not trusted:** a single UI formatting layer always renders in an explicit zone, and a **coherence fitness function** (ADR-0051) forbids a naive local datetime stored where an instant (UTC) or a civil date (`PlainDate`) belongs; rides the fixtures fixed-clock discipline (ADR-0034). **Considered & rejected:** **Luxon / date-fns-tz** (works today without a polyfill, but pre-Temporal idioms and the instant/PlainDate distinction is by convention, not type); **a single firm-level timezone** (wrong for multi-state firms and for court-zone deadlines); **user-timezone-only** (unsafe for docketing — a deadline must key off the court's zone). **Consequences:** DST and cross-zone-deadline bugs are prevented by type; cost is the Temporal polyfill where unsupported and the discipline of carrying zone context on Locations/users. Reuses **ADR-0031** (Locations), **ADR-0040** (settings), **ADR-0034** (fixtures), **ADR-0051** (fitness function); feeds **ADR-0033** (time tracking) and the Calendar/docketing subsystem.
