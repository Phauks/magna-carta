**UI design quality & the design process — distinctiveness and usability engineered into a foundation, not improvised per screen; a human-set design language + page archetypes that AI agents conform to, enforced by a design-critic + accessibility + visual-regression pipeline.** **Context:** the design-system enforcement of ADR-0028 prevents UI _drift_ (the "jumbled together" look) but not the _generic, AI-made_ look (default-styled primitives, flat hierarchy, toy-data layouts, vague microcopy) — a problem of design _judgment_, which AI performs poorly and which is a live risk for a platform built largely by AI. **Decision:** treat design quality as a **foundational step that precedes feature UI**, mirroring the prevent-by-construction-then-verify shape of ADR-0028 / ADR-0051. (1) **Judgment is set once, up front, by a human** (agent-assisted) and conformed to thereafter — AI applies an opinionated design well but invents one badly. (2) **Two foundation artifacts:** a _distinctive, restrained design language_ (deliberate type/scale, palette-with-intent, legal-power-user density, functional motion, signature elements, anchored to best-in-class references) and _page archetypes_ (List/Index, Record Detail, Editor, Form/Wizard, Dashboard — each fixing layout, hierarchy, and all states), **composed** rather than assembling raw primitives. (3) **A staged UI build pipeline:** interaction design (task/flow → archetype + states) → implementation (against the design system) → **design critique** (an anti-generic + usability rubric) → accessibility (keyboard-first + WCAG) → visual-regression — the critic being the judgment gate a linter cannot be. (4) **Built and reviewed on the load-profile fixtures** (ADR-0034), never toy data, with **domain-grounded microcopy** from the canonical glossary (ADR-0037). **Considered & rejected:** **consistency-enforcement alone** (ADR-0028 — necessary but yields a consistent-yet-generic UI); **per-screen customization of primitives for distinctiveness** (reintroduces the drift ADR-0028 prevents); **one-shot UI generation with no foundation or critic** (the direct cause of the generic AI look). **Consequences:** distinctiveness and usability become structural rather than per-screen luck; the cost is the up-front human design-language / archetype work and maintaining the critic rubric + pipeline. Extends **ADR-0028** (design system); reuses **ADR-0034** (fixtures) and **ADR-0037** (glossary/voice); shares the philosophy of **ADR-0051** (prevent by construction, then verify — applied to design).
