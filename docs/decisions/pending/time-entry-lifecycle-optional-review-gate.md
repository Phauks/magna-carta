**Slice 3·1 time-entry lifecycle.** Entries move **Draft → (Submitted → Approved) → Billed**, plus Void. A firm setting `time.approval_required` (default **false**) gates the Submitted/Approved states: with the gate off, entries go Draft → Billed at invoice finalize (3·4) exactly as the white paper's frictionless path; with it on, a timekeeper **submits** (locks the entry to the timekeeper, editable by a reviewer), a reviewer **approves** (billable) or **kicks back** to Draft with a reason stored in the dedicated `kick_back_reason` column on the entry (not in audit metadata — the audit row records only who/when; the free-text reason stays off the audit log to avoid privileged content in the shared log table) and cleared when the entry is resubmitted, and only Approved entries are billable. The submit/approve/kick-back transitions and a `time:approve` reviewer permission are built in slice 3·1; the UI rides 0·7. **Freeze contract:** `status` is a normal mutable column whose legal transitions are UPDATEs; on reaching `billed`/`void` the row's business columns freeze (the service refuses any further UPDATE except the 3·4 invoice-lock), proven by a fitness check (`no UPDATE … WHERE status IN ('billed','void')`). This is explicitly **not** the ledger's append-only invariant (ADR-0008) — a time entry is mutable until frozen. **Context:** the owner (2026-06-22 brainstorm) reconsidered §17.1's flat "no approval workflow"; competitor evidence (LEAP/Rocket Matter expose a submitted/review state before billing) and the deferred passive-capture draft-approve queue both want an intermediate state, and adding it now keeps that seam cheap. **Considered & rejected:** a *mandatory* approval step (rejected — adds friction to the daily driver, the white paper's whole point); *no intermediate state at all* (rejected — forgoes the passive-queue host and firms that genuinely want timekeeper review). **Consequences:** the default firm experience is unchanged; firms opt in per firm; **white-paper §17.1 is amended** (the "no approval workflow" line).
