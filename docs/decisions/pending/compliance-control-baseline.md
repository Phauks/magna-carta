**Compliance is engineered as a control baseline now and certified later: the cross-cutting technical controls that SOC 2, GDPR, PCI DSS, and ISO 27001 attest to are built up front (they are cheap as foundations, expensive to retrofit), mapped requirement-to-control in [`docs/conventions/compliance.md`](../conventions/compliance.md); the attestations, audits, and published legal/privacy documents themselves remain an owner/legal track — the [`legal-perimeter.md`](../research/legal-perimeter.md) inventory (ADR-0090 point 3) — produced "when distribution approaches," not an engineering deliverable on the build roadmap.** **Context:** the platform is pre-distribution with no live clients, target market is global/enterprise with US firms primary plus Canada and EU/UK, and SOC 2 Type II is table stakes for the firm/enterprise buyer (legal-perimeter.md). The controls already exist as decisions — tenancy isolation (ADR-0003), envelope encryption + `protected()` + crypto-shred (ADR-0026/0046/0084), trusted-insider RBAC (ADR-0068/0073), redact-by-default telemetry (ADR-0017/0038), backup/DR (ADR-0050), PCI SAQ A (ADR-0032) — but nothing mapped them to external frameworks or registered the gaps, and "we run on a SOC 2 provider" is not compliance: Cloudflare's attestations cover only the substrate it operates (a carve-out sub-service organization in our own audit), reachable on the Business plan under NDA, with a DPA on the self-serve agreement. **Decision:** adopt `docs/conventions/compliance.md` as the single home for the framework mapping and gap register; it links to each control's home and never restates it (one home per fact); every new subsystem is built against it in one pass (which framework requirements can this control satisfy?); each gap is tracked to a Proposed ADR or the owner/legal track; the two compliance domains — SaaS-vendor attestations vs. the attorney-ethics/trust-accounting regulatory catalog in [`../research/trust-rules/`](../research/trust-rules/) — are kept separate. GRC-platform adoption (Vanta/Drata/Secureframe, which only _collect_ evidence the controls already emit) is a later owner call pending pricing. **Considered & rejected:** bolt-on compliance near a sale (the retrofit — subject-attribution, audit history, residency cannot be added cheaply after the schema and corpus exist); a single mega-document restating every control (drifts against its real home — violates one-home-per-fact); buying a GRC platform before the controls exist (the platform collects evidence, it does not create controls; premature spend). **Consequences:** the map becomes a design checklist and a buyer-facing readiness artifact; the open gaps — subject-level erasure/DSAR, per-firm data residency, breach-notification workflow, audit viewer/retention, ROOT_KEK rotation, external-KMS anchor — are made explicit and each gets a decision; certification is cheaper later because the evidence is already produced. Builds on **ADR-0090** (the legal perimeter as owner track) and the control ADRs above; paired ADRs this cycle: **ADR-pci-saq-a-hosted-payment-modal**, **ADR-data-residency-firm-region-seam** (Proposed), **ADR-subject-level-erasure-dsar** (Proposed).
