**The disbursement WIP store.** Slice 3·3 adds one new per-firm table, **`cost_entry`** (migration **`0022_burly_piledriver`**) — the work-in-progress store for matter disbursements, never a ledger posting (costs converge onto an invoice in 3·4; this slice never writes the ledger). Money is **integer-minor** (`amount_minor`, validated a positive whole number at capture; no floats). The cost class column is **`disbursement_class`** (`hard|soft`) — renamed from the design's working `cost_class` to clear the money-naming conformance gate (the underscore-prefix gate is column-name-only; the dotted setting key `cost.recover_platform_fee.<category>` is unaffected). **The column is `disbursement_class`, never `cost_class`.** A `source` of `manual|grid|recovery` records how the row entered (a hand capture, the bulk grid, or the recoverable-charge consumer). The row carries the shared **billing-core lifecycle** (`draft → (submitted → approved) → billed`, plus `void`; `billed`/`void` freeze the business columns under a column-level freeze contract enforced by the service, with `status NOT IN ('billed','void')` WHERE-guards on the mutating UPDATEs as the backstop). The UTBMS **E-code is the cost type** — there is no separate firm cost-type catalog. `recovery_category`/`recovery_ref` are append-only; `recovery_ref` carries a **partial-unique index** (the consumer's per-line idempotency floor). **Permissions:** **`costs:track`** governs capture, reads, and receipt attach/detach; **`costs:approve`** governs the review-path transitions (approve / kick-back when the firm review gate is on). **THE E-BILLING RECOVERY EXEMPTION (a decision worth recording):** a billable cost on an electronic-billing matter normally REQUIRES a UTBMS expense code at capture — the LEDES exporter rejects an uncoded line, and at a manual or grid capture a person is present to choose the code, so refusing keeps an uncodeable entry out of the store. But a **system-recovered** cost (`source=recovery`) is **EXEMPT at capture**: no human stands at the consumer to pick a code, and dropping the line would silently lose a real fronted disbursement (the postage or fee the firm has already paid). Such a cost lands as **uncoded draft WIP** instead; the e-billing UTBMS requirement is **re-enforced later, at the 3·4 submit/bill gate**, where a person and the billing context exist to code it. **OPEN 3·4 QUESTION:** how recovered costs ultimately get coded for LEDES — auto-mapping a category at capture (e.g. postage → E108) versus a human coding at bill time. Capture-time auto-coding was rejected for now because the mapping is genuinely ambiguous (FedEx = E107 delivery vs USPS = E108 postage), so the question is deferred to the 3·4 billing gate rather than guessed at capture. **Considered & rejected:** *a separate firm cost-type catalog* (rejected — the UTBMS E-code already is the type); *drop an uncoded recovered cost on an e-billing matter* (rejected — loses a real fronted disbursement); *auto-code recovered costs at capture* (rejected for now — the postage-carrier ambiguity makes a capture-time guess unsafe). **Consequences:** recovered disbursements are never lost; an uncoded recovered draft is a known, intentional state the 3·4 gate must resolve; the ledger stays untouched in 3·3.
