**All seven custodian shape flags ratified as recommended; the will-on-death engine proceeds design-first with Nevada as the first jurisdiction.**

**Context:** The custodian design (docs/specs/2026-08-04-custodian-design.md, section 12) flagged eight
decisions for the owner. C1–C3 were answered at design time and are recorded; C4–C11 stayed open, and
the destruction/retention workflow build was gated on them because retention and destruction of client
property is exactly where the platform does not act on defaults.

**Decision:** On 2026-08-05 the owner reviewed each flag in full and ratified every recommendation:
depth cap of eight (C4), firm-configurable ownership labels (C5), advisory-only close notice (C6), no
platform retention defaults ever (C7), unencrypted declared values (C9), two permissions with no
separate destruction key (C10), and packet-level ownership across re-packeting (C11). For C8 the owner
chose the design-first path and named the first jurisdiction: build the jurisdiction-keyed catalog
shape now — the trust-rules pattern, where an unencoded jurisdiction refuses rather than guesses — and
encode nothing until Nevada's statutory basis for will custody and deposit-on-death obligations is
researched from primary sources and verified.

**Consequence:** The destruction workflow build (two-person by construction, hold-refused,
intrinsic-value refused with no override) and the C6 advisory notice are unblocked. The C8 catalog
shape lands with that work; the Nevada encoding lands only behind the verified research.
