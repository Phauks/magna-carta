# Third-party trademark & brand-asset usage

**Status:** Living register · **Last updated:** 2026-09-16 · **Owner:** Trevor Goldsmith (engineering maintains; counsel signs off before distribution)

The maintained register of every **third-party brand mark or logo Magna Carta displays** — today, the GitHub mark on the build panel's repository link (`@magna-carta/design-system/service-marks`) and the service buttons in `@magna-carta/property-links` (Zillow, Redfin, Google Maps, Realtor.com). It records each owner's brand guidelines, where to get official assets, the key usage constraints, and when we last checked — so logo use stays compliant and is cheap to maintain as brand pages move. Pairs with the IP-protection posture (ADR-0090) and the legal perimeter ([`docs/research/legal-perimeter.md`](../research/legal-perimeter.md)); brand-asset rendering details for the UI live with the design system (slice 0·7a).

## Policy

1. **Nominative use only.** We show a service's logo solely to link out to that service (identifying the real thing we link to). Display no more of the mark than needed, never imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement, and surface a "trademarks are the property of their respective owners" line where logos appear. This is the **nominative fair use** basis — it holds only while we also stay within each owner's published rules below.
2. **Self-host official assets, unaltered.** Bundle the owner's official logo asset; **never hotlink a favicon/asset service** (privacy/no-egress — ADR-0038/0090, and it leaks who is viewing what). Do not recolor, distort, re-proportion, or crowd the logo; respect required clear-space.
3. **Trademark-safe fallback is the default when in doubt.** Where an owner's terms are restrictive, permission-required, or unverified — or assets aren't yet cleared — render a **neutral Lucide icon (`external-link` / `map-pin`) + the service name as text** instead of the logo. A new provider ships with the fallback until its row here is completed.
4. **Government / public-records sources need no brand licensing** — we link to a county assessor/recorder or a Secretary of State portal **by name** (factual reference). But do **not** reproduce official **state seals / government emblems**, which often carry separate statutory use restrictions; use a neutral icon for these.
5. **Maintenance.** Before shipping a new provider's logo: complete its row below, set its _Last reviewed_, and have counsel confirm for distribution (ADR-0090 legal track). Re-review existing rows periodically — brand pages and asset URLs move.

## Adding an entry

When a new third-party service is added to a UI surface (a `property-links` provider, an integration, etc.):

1. Find the owner's official brand/trademark page and logo-asset source; read the constraints.
2. Add a row above with the URLs, the key constraints, and today's date as _Last reviewed_.
3. Bundle the official asset self-hosted (policy 2), or ship the trademark-safe fallback (policy 3) until assets/terms are cleared.
4. For anything restrictive or unverified, flag it for counsel (ADR-0090) before it ships to distribution.
