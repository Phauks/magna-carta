[Member Assistance Program (MAP)]
QUOTE: "All communication is confidential."

The Member Assistance Program, also called "MAP", offers a peer support network of volunteer
lawyers who have been trained to offer support to lawyers, judges and law students or their
immediate families. If you have questions, or want to be matched with a peer volunteer, call
602.340.7334. All communication is confidential; all calls to MAP and to MAP's volunteers are
confidential under Supreme Court rule ("This Rule does not require disclosure of information
otherwise protected by Rule 1.6 or information gained by a lawyer or judge while participating in an
approved lawyers assistance program."). Crisis referral given: Suicide Prevention Lifeline (free,
24/7). Also lists support groups and 12-Step programs (AA, Al-Anon, IDAA, NA) and a monthly
non-12-step Lawyer Support Group. NOTE: the canonical /member-assistance-program/ short URL returns
HTTP 404; live pages are under /member-assistance-program-wellness-resource/.
