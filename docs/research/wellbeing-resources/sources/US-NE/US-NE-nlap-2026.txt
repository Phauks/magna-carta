[Nebraska Lawyers Assistance Program (NLAP)]
QUOTE (hours): toll-free hotline (888) 584-6527 — "24HRS, 7 DAYS A WEEK"
QUOTE (confidentiality): "The relationship between a member of the Committee and/or the NLAP Director and a lawyer or judge who seeks or receives assistance shall be the same as that of attorney and client." (Neb. Rule of Prof. Conduct §3-501.6(c))

Funding: "NLAP is funded by the Nebraska Supreme Court through attorney mandatory dues payment. The
payment of NSBA voluntary dues is not required to access this service." Services: assessment and
referral; formal interventions; geographically matched peer-support network; education and
prevention for firms and courts. Operated under the Nebraska Supreme Court (Judicial Branch),
co-presented with the NSBA.
