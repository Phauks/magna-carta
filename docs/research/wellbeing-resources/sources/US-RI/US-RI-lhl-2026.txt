[Lawyers Helping Lawyers (Rhode Island Bar Association)] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "Simply pick up the telephone and call 866-482-8378 for confidential, round the clock support."

RI Bar members and dependents may receive free, confidential help, assessment, and referral through
the Lawyers Helping Lawyers Program. Services via the Association's contract with SupportLinc
(previously CorpCare, now under CuraLinc Healthcare) and via the volunteer Lawyers Helping Lawyers
Committee of peer attorneys. In 1991 the RI Supreme Court gave Committee members immunity from suit,
exception from the duty to disclose unprofessional conduct, and an obligation to confer in
confidence. Call 866-482-8378 (counselors answer 24/7). NOTE: ribar.com returned HTTP 403 this
session; 24/7 claim corroborated, not page-verified.
