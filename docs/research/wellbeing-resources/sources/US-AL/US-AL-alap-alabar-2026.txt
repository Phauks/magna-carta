[Alabama Lawyer Assistance Program (ALAP)]
QUOTE: "While ALAP is a program of the Alabama State Bar, we preserve the anonymity of participants and do not disclose to other departments within the state bar."

We provide free and confidential support to law students, lawyers, and judges. While ALAP is a
program of the Alabama State Bar, we preserve the anonymity of participants and do not disclose to
other departments within the state bar. Services include intervention, assessments, referrals to
vetted clinical professionals and treatment centers, peer support groups (in-person and virtual),
mentorship from volunteer attorneys and judges, monitoring, and advocacy for disciplinary and
Character and Fitness hearings. Crisis referrals given on-page: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
(available any time, day or night) and Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741). Contact: ALAP office
334-224-6920; director contact 334-517-2216; shannon.knight@alabar.org.
