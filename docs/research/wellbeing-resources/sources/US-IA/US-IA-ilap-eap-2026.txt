[Esquire Assistance Program (EAP)] (corroborated — iowacourts.gov 403)
A license benefit at no cost to all Iowa-licensed lawyers, judges, and court reporters and families:
three free in-person/telehealth sessions plus "unlimited access to master's-level counselors any
time of day or night by phone." Call 1-800-EAP-IOWA (1-800-327-4692); "available around the clock,
365 days a year." QUOTE: "all information you provide is confidential and will not be shared with the
