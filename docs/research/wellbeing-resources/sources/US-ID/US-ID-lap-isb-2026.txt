[Idaho Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)]
QUOTE: "All information is 100% confidential and will not be reported to the Idaho State Bar"
QUOTE (hours): "Call (866) 460-9014 for a 100% confidential, 24/7 hotline!"

The mission of the Idaho Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP) is to provide support for lawyers
experiencing problems associated with substance abuse and/or mental health issues in a safe manner.
Primary resources are provided by Southworth Associates International (800 S. Industry Way, Suite
300, Meridian, ID 83706). Program Coordinator: Ben Seymour, CADC — (208) 949-0363,
ben@southworthassociates.net. 24-Hour Hotline: (866) 460-9014. Services: alcohol/drug education,
mental-health treatment, interventions, monitoring, workplace re-entry, temporary attorney
replacement referrals, and family support. Idaho State Bar is a mandatory/unified bar.
