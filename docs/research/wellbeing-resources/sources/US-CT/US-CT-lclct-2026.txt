[Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers — Connecticut, Inc. (LCL-CT)]
QUOTE: "This is an anonymous, confidential program offered free of charge and free of judgment"

LCL-CT is a Connecticut non-profit created to assist Connecticut lawyers and judges experiencing
alcoholism, drug addiction, depression, anxiety, compulsive disorders (including gambling), stress
or other distress. Services are free and completely confidential: crisis intervention and referral;
free mental-health evaluation and assessment (no evaluations/documentation supplied to any
regulatory or state agency); biweekly peer-driven support groups with a professional facilitator.
"Our services are confidential and protected under Connecticut General Statutes §51-81d (a)." Not
affiliated with the State Judicial Branch, State Grievance Committee, Office of Disciplinary
Counsel, or Connecticut Bar Association (which support its mission). Contact: hotline 800-497-1422;
office 860-563-4900; info@lclct.org. Hours/24-7 not stated → availability unspecified. Connecticut
Bar is voluntary.
