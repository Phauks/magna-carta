[Kentucky Lawyer Assistance Program (KYLAP)]
QUOTE (hours): "Contact us 24 hours a day at (502) 226-9373 to get help for any Kentucky lawyer in need."
Confidentiality: "Pursuant to Supreme Court Rule 3.990, all communication with KYLAP is
confidential." Broad-brush program for addiction, substance abuse, and mental/emotional illness;
assessment and treatment referrals, peer support, monitoring/compliance contracts, recovery
meetings, and financial assistance. Director Yvette Hourigan, yhourigan@kylap.org. P.O. Box 1437,
Frankfort, KY.

[Kentucky Bar Association EAP] — kybar.org/.../Employee-Assistance-Program
QUOTE: "If you are unable to pay, you will receive up to four (4) clinical sessions at no charge to you."
"Someone will be available to you 24 hours a day, 7 days a week" via the KYLAP direct line
502-226-9373 (after hours routes to the EAP). Appointment within 24-48 hours from a statewide
provider network.

[KYLAP Foundation] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "additional assistance may be available through the KYLAP Foundation's forgivable loan program."
Nonprofit funding arm (www.kylap.org/foundation) — forgivable-loan aid for care beyond the free EAP
sessions. Own foundation page not loaded this session.
