[Lawyers Helping Lawyers Assistance Program (LHL)]
QUOTE: "By statute and OBA resolution, any information received by the program must remain completely confidential."
Cost: "The OBA offers all bar members up to six hours of free short-term, problem-focused or crisis
counseling." LHL hotline 800-364-7886 or oklalhl@gmail.com; the hotline is answered by a contracted
counseling/mental-health service (after-hours reaches licensed professionals) — no literal 24/7
statement → unspecified. Peer mentoring and monthly discussion groups (OKC and Tulsa).

[Lawyers Helping Lawyers Foundation] (nonprofit funding)
QUOTE: "Attorneys may qualify for a grant from the Lawyers Helping Lawyers Foundation to help pay for treatment or medication."
Grants applied for via the 800-364-7886 hotline / off-site coordinator.
