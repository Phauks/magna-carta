[Nevada Lawyer Assistance Program (NLAP)] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "The initial evaluation is conducted by a medical professional and is provided at no charge to the attorney."
Clinical program for attorneys/judges: assessment, treatment referral, and ongoing monitoring.
Voluntary self-report or Office of Bar Counsel referral. Member therapy benefit: "The first five
sessions are provided at no charge" (expanded from three), statewide and via secure video. Hotline
1-866-828-0022 (answered by a fellow lawyer).

[Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers (LCL) — Nevada] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "The first five sessions are provided at no charge. The inquiry and subsequent five sessions are completely confidential."
Peer-support program (est. mid-1980s) of attorneys in recovery: one-on-one help, statewide closed-
door AA meetings, therapist referrals. Same hotline 1-866-828-0022; leave only first name and phone.
