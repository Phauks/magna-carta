[North Carolina Lawyer Assistance Program (NC LAP)]
QUOTE: "All communications with LAP staff are strictly confidential."
Site tagline "Safe. Free. Confidential." Free, confidential, non-disciplinary help for lawyers,
judges, and law students; absorbed the historic PALS/FRIENDS peer-volunteer network. Two offices —
Charlotte (704-503-9695, Western) and Raleigh (919-719-9290, Eastern). "Office hours are M-F
(9:00-5:00) and in-person consultations are by appointment only." Crisis referrals: 911; 988.

[BarCARES of North Carolina] — barcares.org/about (HRC Behavioral Health & Psychiatry, P.A.)
QUOTE: "provided cost free to members of participating bar groups and law schools"
Confidential counseling/assessment/referral of the NC Bar Association via clinical vendor HRC
Behavioral Health; intake 1-800-640-0735; typically a three-session assessment/referral benefit.
NC LAP and BarCARES cross-refer. BarCARES line hours not stated → unspecified.
