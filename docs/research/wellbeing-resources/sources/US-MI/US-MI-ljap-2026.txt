[Lawyers and Judges Assistance Program (LJAP)]
QUOTE: "All services offered by LJAP are confidential as regulated by HIPAA."

Serves attorneys (including suspended/disbarred/reinstatement candidates), law students, bar
applicants, judges, family members, and colleagues. Services: free consultations for legal
professionals and families, clinical assessments, short-term counseling for law students, monitoring
programs, provider referrals, professional training, and free confidential virtual support groups.
Contact 800-996-5522; main office 517-346-6300; contactljap@michbar.org. LJAP's own helpline is not
advertised 24/7 → unspecified (LJAP's resources page lists national 24/7 lines: Crisis Text Line
741741, 988).
