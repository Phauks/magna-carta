[Kansas Lawyers Assistance Program (KALAP)]
QUOTE: "KALAP's confidential services, including counseling, support groups, mentoring and interventions, and referrals for assessments and treatment, are available to all Kansas lawyers at no cost."
Confidentiality: "By KS. Sup. Ct. R. 233, any information received by the KALAP director, board
members, and volunteers must remain completely confidential." Phone 785-368-8275;
klap@kscourts.gov; 515 S Kansas Ave, Topeka, KS 66603.

[KALAP 24/7 EAP Providers Helpline]
QUOTE: "KALAP has a 24/7 helpline that is staffed with trained mental health professionals."
"If you need support before KALAP staff return to the office, please call our confidential EAP
providers helpline at 1-800-466-8282 for personal support." A companion Kansas Judges Assistance
Committee serves judges via the same office.
