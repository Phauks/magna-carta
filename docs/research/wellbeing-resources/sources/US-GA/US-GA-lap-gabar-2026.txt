[Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)]
QUOTE: "Staffed by trained counselors 24 hours a day, 7 days a week."

Do you need immediate, confidential help? Call 800-327-9631. LAP is a confidential service provided
through SupportLinc, a program administered by CuraLinc Healthcare. Members are entitled to six
prepaid clinical sessions per calendar year. LAP provides a broad range of services for stress,
anxiety, depression, substance abuse, family problems, workplace conflicts, psychological and other
issues. Email gabar@curalinc.com; web portal galap.mysupportid.com.

QUOTE: "a confidential peer-to-peer program that provides attorneys with a fellow Bar member to be there, listen and help with various struggles."
Peer support via phone, text, meetings, or shared activities; disclosures held in an encrypted
