[Virginia Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program (VJLAP)] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "24-hour confidential help line" ; "Services offered without fee."

VJLAP provides confidential, non-disciplinary help to lawyers, judges, law students, bar applicants,
and other members of the legal community. 24-hour confidential help line: 1-877-545-4682;
info@vjlap.org. Services include substance-abuse and mental-health education, information and
referral, assessment and treatment referral, intervention, compliance monitoring, and family/staff
support. Formerly "Virginia Lawyers Helping Lawyers"; became an independent corporation in 2002.
