[Lawyers Helping Lawyers (LHL)]
QUOTE: "LHL falls under the protection of Rule 8.3 of the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct... That means what you say to LHL stays with LHL."
Phone (801) 900-3834; contact@lawyershelpinglawyers.org. Matches lawyers with one-on-one volunteer
peer mentors and conducts CLE. "LHL is a committee providing confidential peer-to-peer support. LHL
does not provide treatment or evaluation services." 24/7 not stated → unspecified.

[Tava Mental Health] (Utah State Bar member benefit; vendor) — utahbar.org/well-being/resources
QUOTE: "These services are now available to U.S. based active lawyers, Licensed Paralegal Practitioners (LPPS), and members of the Paralegal Division"
Free, confidential teletherapy via https://www.tavahealth.com/ (registration at care.tavahealth.com);
reported up to 6 free sessions/year (exact count not printed on the loaded page).
NOTE: Utah's dedicated program is LHL peer support; there is no separately branded "Utah Lawyers
Assistance Program." 988 (national) is surfaced on the Bar's well-being page as a crisis resource.
