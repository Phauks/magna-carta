[Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program (JLAP)]
QUOTE: "all contact with JLAP is both privileged and confidential and is not subject to subpoena or any other discovery mechanism."

Free, confidential assistance to lawyers, judges, law students, other legal professionals, and their
families for substance abuse, mental health, age, and other stressors. Confidential as a matter of
law (La. R.S. 37:221). Cost: "It does not cost a penny to call JLAP. Confidential, professional
mental health consultations with JLAP are available without limitation and at no cost whatsoever...
In cases where the person requires a formal Recovery Monitoring contract with JLAP, there is a $50
per month monitoring fee... In cases of severe financial hardship, JLAP has the flexibility to defer
or waive monitoring fees." 501(c)(3) owned/funded by the LSBA, operating independently from a
Mandeville office. Contact: toll-free 866-354-9334; confidential help line 985-778-0571;
jlap@louisianajlap.com.
