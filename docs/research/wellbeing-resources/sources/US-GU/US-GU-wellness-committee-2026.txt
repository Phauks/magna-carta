[Alternative Disciplinary and Attorney Wellness Committee]
QUOTE: "to promote attorney well-being by providing education, peer-to-peer support and resources to attorneys in the areas of mental health, emotional balance, stress management, and addiction"

Guam Bar By-Laws, Article X (standing committees), item 6: "Alternative Disciplinary and Attorney
Wellness Committee: Responsible for actively managing, determining and participating in all referrals
under Rule 11 of the GUAM RULES FOR LAWYER DISCIPLINARY ENFORCEMENT AND DISABILITY PROCEEDINGS ...,
and to promote attorney well-being by providing education, peer-to-peer support and resources to
attorneys in the areas of mental health, emotional balance, stress management, and addiction."
Committee added/amended March 21, 2024 (SPR23-001). No dedicated hotline published; access via the
bar. NOTE: web-search results describing a SupportLinc/CuraLinc LAP under "Guam Bar" were actually
the State Bar of GEORGIA (gabar.org), not Guam (guambar.org) — discarded.
