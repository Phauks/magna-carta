[Colorado Lawyer Assistance Program (COLAP)]
QUOTE: "All communications with COLAP are privileged and held in the strictest confidence."
Phone (303) 986-3345; info@coloradolap.org. Free and confidential; information cannot be used in
complaints, investigations, or disciplinary proceedings. Also the Judicial Assistance Program.
Services: individual/workplace consultations, vetted referrals, peer support, education, voluntary
monitoring agreements. Crisis resources it points to (24/7): Colorado Crisis Services
1-844-493-8255 / text TALK to 38255; 988.

[Colorado Lawyers Helping Lawyers (CLHL)] — colhl.org/about-us
QUOTE: "CLHL is a confidential resource for lawyers and law students"
Free, confidential court-approved nonprofit peer program: education, peer-support meetings, and
referrals. No dedicated CLHL phone/email published; routes urgent needs to Colorado Crisis Services
844-493-8255 and 988.

[The Waterman Fund] — denbar.org/Members/Waterman-Fund
QUOTE: "THE WATERMAN FUND is a charitable trust that provides financial assistance to Colorado lawyers."
Assists aged, infirm, or incapacitated Colorado bar members in good standing (practiced in CO >= 10
years). One-time to renewable six-month awards. Applicant/award info confidential. Contact (303)
824-5331.

[Colorado Crisis Services] (corroborated; own page not loaded)
Listed on the COLAP homepage under "Crisis Resources (24/7)": Colorado Crisis Services
1-844-493-8255 or text "TALK" to 38255. General statewide crisis line the lawyer programs route to.

NOTE: Colorado has no mandatory unified bar; regulation via the Colorado Supreme Court Office of
Attorney Regulation. CLHL domain spelling is colhl.org.
