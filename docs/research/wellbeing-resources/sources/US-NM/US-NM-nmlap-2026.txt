[New Mexico Lawyer Assistance Program (NM LAP)]
QUOTE (hours + confidentiality): "24 Hours Every Day ... Confidential Helpline/Hotline Assistance"; "communications made to a member, staff, or authorized agent of the Lawyers Assistance Program ... are privileged."

Free, confidential program for judges, attorneys, law students, and legal staff: confidential
helpline, substance-use assessments, peer support, referrals. "judges may access confidential,
judicial peer support and professional services at no cost." Helpline/peer contacts 505-420-8179,
505-797-6091; office 505-797-6003, 800-860-4914; nmlap@sbnm.org.

[The Solutions Group — NM LAP EAP] (vendor)
QUOTE: "Free Employee Assistance Program: www.solutionsbiz.com or 505-254-3555"
Outsourced EAP delivering free confidential counseling (up to four sessions/issue/year, corroborated
from search) for members and families.

[In This Together Fund] (corroborated)
QUOTE: "In This Together Fund & Donations"
NM LAP donation-supported fund (Bar Foundation) for treatment/assistance costs; detailed eligibility
text not extractable this session.
NOTE: NM LAP was formerly the Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program (renamed to distinguish it from
the new NM Judicial Wellness Program).
