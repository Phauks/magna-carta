[WSBA Member Wellness Program]
QUOTE: "All services are confidential" (references APR 19) ; "Our services are free of charge."

Phone 206-727-8268 / 206-727-8267; wellness@wsba.org. Free consultations for attorneys, LPOs, LLLTs,
law students, or those concerned about an attorney; mental-health support and referrals; peer
advising (~50 attorney volunteers statewide); addiction resources; job-search assistance;
presentations. Counseling delivered IN-HOUSE by WSBA-employed clinicians (Dan Crystal, Psy.D.; Adely
Ruiz, LICSW) rather than an outsourced EAP. A Judicial Assistance Service Program (JASP) track serves
judicial officers via the same program. No 24/7 crisis line advertised → unspecified.
