[Lawyer's Assistance Program (Committee)]
QUOTE: "This committee shall continue the process of creating and implementing a program to provide assistance to members of the Virgin Islands Bar Association who suffer from substance abuse and addiction."

VIRGIN ISLANDS BAR ASSOCIATION, PO Box 224108, Christiansted, VI 00822. Phone (340) 778-7497; email
info@usvibar.org. The "Lawyer's Assistance Program" is a standing committee; the official text scopes
it to substance abuse and addiction. Corroborating reports (not on the official page) indicate
partnerships with local providers (Insight Psychological Services, Island Therapy Solutions) for
expedited, same-day referrals. No LAP-specific hotline is published; contact is the general bar
line/email.
