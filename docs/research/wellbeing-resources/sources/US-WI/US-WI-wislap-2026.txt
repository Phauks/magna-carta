[Wisconsin Lawyers Assistance Program (WisLAP)]
QUOTE: "WisLAP services are free as a benefit of your State Bar membership"

WisLAP Line (800) 543-2625 or callwislap@wisbar.org; Program Manager (800) 444-9404 ext. 6159. "The
Lawyers Assistance Program offers confidential well-being support" and "WisLAP does not require
callers to disclose their identity"; exempt from reporting professional misconduct to the Office of
Lawyer Regulation and the Judicial Commission. Built on a "Lawyers Helping Lawyers / Judges Helping
Judges" model with 150+ trained volunteers. Services: initial evaluation, referrals, peer assistance,
consultation, education/CLE, mental-health trainings, well-being presentations. "We do not provide
legal advice or give lawyer referrals." Line hours not published → unspecified.
