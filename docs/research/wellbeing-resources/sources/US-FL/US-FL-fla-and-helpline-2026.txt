[Florida Lawyers Assistance, Inc. (FLA)] — fla-lap.org
QUOTE: "Confidentiality in voluntary cases is protected by a written contract with The Florida Bar which guarantees the confidentiality of FLA records, as well as by Bar Rule 3-7.1(j), Chap. 397.482-486, F.S., and other state and federal regulations."

Nonprofit established 1986 in response to the Florida Supreme Court's mandate to identify and assist
bar members with substance abuse, mental health, or other disorders. Independent of, but funded by,
The Florida Bar. Support groups are completely confidential; no record of attendance reported to FLA
or the Bar (unless required by probation/conditional admission). Toll-Free Hotline (800) 282-8981;
Judges' Hotline (888) 972-4040; office (954) 566-9040; mail@fla-lap.org. NOTE: the 24-hour claim for
the hotline appears only in secondary sources (ABA directory, which 403'd) — NOT confirmed on FLA's
own page → availability unspecified.

[Florida Lawyers Helpline] — floridabar.org/helpline (vendor: CuraLinc Health Care)
QUOTE: "Counseling sessions are completely anonymous and there is no cost to our members."
Offers 24/7/365 access to therapists and professional counselors (in-person or teletherapy); free,
up to five sessions annually. Call 1-833-351-9355. The 24/7/365 availability IS stated verbatim on
the primary floridabar.org page. The Florida Bar is a mandatory/integrated bar.
