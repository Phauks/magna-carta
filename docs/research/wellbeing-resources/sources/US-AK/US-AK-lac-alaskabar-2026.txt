[Lawyers' Assistance Committee (LAC)]
QUOTE: "The Lawyers' Assistance Committee promotes the well-being of the legal profession and protects the public by providing education, respectful confidential assistance and referrals for members of the Bar, their families, colleagues, and clients affected by a member's mental health and substance abuse issues."

Contact: Alaska Bar Association, 840 K Street, Suite 100, Anchorage, AK 99501-3353; phone
907-272-7469; info@alaskabar.org. Resources: a Provider List (PDF) of mental-health providers who
help attorneys with substance abuse and wellness issues, an FAQ document, and a link to the
Institute for Well-Being in Law (lawyerwellbeing.net). The committee comprises 15 members across
Alaska who can be contacted directly. Hours, cost, and an explicit confidentiality guarantee beyond
the mission statement are not stated on the page.
