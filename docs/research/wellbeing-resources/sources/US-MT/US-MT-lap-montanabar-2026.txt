[Montana Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)]
QUOTE (hours): "Confidential, 24-hours a day, every day"

Confidential helpline (406) 660-1181 (hotline counselor Mike Larson). "The Montana Lawyer Assistance
Program is independent from any disciplinary organization and does not police, report, discipline, or
otherwise threaten the career of an attorney or judge. By order of the Montana Supreme Court (Order
No. 85-96), the names of all persons seeking assistance under this program shall remain
confidential." Services: guidance and referral; treatment-program information; intervention;
family/friend support; education; weekly online support groups (Addiction Recovery Tue noon; Mental
Health Wed noon). Cost not stated on page.
