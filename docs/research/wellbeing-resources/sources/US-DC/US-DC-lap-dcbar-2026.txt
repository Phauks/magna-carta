[D.C. Bar Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)]
QUOTE: "All inquiries are strictly confidential."

The Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP) provides confidential support services to D.C. Bar members,
judges, and law students facing personal and professional challenges. Services include consultation,
evaluation, and short-term counseling (up to 12 sessions); consultations for family, employers, and
colleagues; support groups facilitated by licensed counselors; individual peer support connections;
outreach and education; and referrals with follow-up. To speak to an LAP counselor, call the
confidential line at 202-347-3131 or e-mail LAP@dcbar.org (901 4th Street, NW, Washington, DC
20001). NOTE: LAP is not an emergency response service — for a mental-health crisis or suicidal
thoughts, call or text 988. D.C. Bar is a mandatory/unified bar; LAP is in-house (not outsourced).
Line hours not published.
