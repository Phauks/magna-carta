[Tennessee Lawyers Assistance Program (TLAP)]
QUOTE: "our services are free, confidential and anonymous"

Administrative Office of the Courts (511 Union Street, Suite 600, Nashville, TN 37219). Professional
clinical staff provide crisis counseling, consultation, intervention, referral to
assessment/treatment providers, and TLAP Monitoring. Members of TLAP are relieved of the duty to
