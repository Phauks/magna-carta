[Missouri Lawyers' Assistance Program (MOLAP)]
QUOTE: "There are no fees for MOLAP services. MOLAP is funded from the general revenue of The Missouri Bar, with substantial support from The Bar Plan Insurance Company."

"Always Free and Always Confidential." Confidential services to lawyers, judges, law students, and
families. Staffed by a licensed mental health professional. MOLAP does NOT report to the Disciplinary
Counsel's Office, the Board of Law Examiners, or other Bar staff. Toll-free 800-688-7859. "If
concerns are urgent, the MOLAP Director is available and accessible after normal business hours by
cell phone." Email MOLAP@mobar.org / achambers@mobar.org. Phone availability recorded as
businessHours (after-hours via director cell, not a 24/7 line).
