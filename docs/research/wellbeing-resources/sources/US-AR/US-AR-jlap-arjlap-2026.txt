[Arkansas Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program (JLAP)]
QUOTE: "their involvement with JLAP and their conversations with JLAP staff, committee, agents (referrals), or volunteers are confidential"

Phone: 501-907-2529. Email: confidential@arjlap.org. Address: 101 E Capitol Ave, Little Rock, AR
72201. JLAP offers a free and confidential assessment. Confidentiality is protected by Arkansas
Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program Rule 10. Serves Arkansas judges, lawyers, law
students/applicants, and immediate adult family members. Services: mental-health counseling,
substance-abuse assistance, stress/burnout support, anxiety and depression, anger management,
process addiction, grief and loss, work transition, peer support, and family-member services.
Established 2000 as a committee of the Arkansas Supreme Court (the Arkansas Bar Association is
voluntary and runs no separate program). Hours not stated → availability unspecified.
