New Jersey Lawyers Assistance Program (NJLAP). Helpline: 800-246-5527. Serves "all NJ Lawyers,
Judges, Law Students, and Law Graduates." Described as "free and confidential," a resource to help
members "achieve and maintain personal and professional well-being." Established under NJ Court Rule
R. 1:28B and funded by a mandatory annual assessment on every licensed NJ attorney. Runs sub-programs
(Senior Attorneys, Small/Solo Firm Support). Verbatim block captured from the NJ Judiciary official
