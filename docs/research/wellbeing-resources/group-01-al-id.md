## Alabama (US-AL)

- ✅ **Alabama Lawyer Assistance Program (ALAP)** — Alabama State Bar (mandatory bar). Free,
  confidential; anonymity preserved from other bar departments. Peer support, mentorship,
  referrals, monitoring. Office 334-224-6920 / 334-517-2216; shannon.knight@alabar.org. Hours not
  stated → `unspecified`.
- ⚠️ **Alabama Lawyer Assistance Foundation** — affiliated nonprofit funding arm; only corroborated
  from the ALAP page (no standalone page loaded).

## Alaska (US-AK)

- ✅ **Lawyers' Assistance Committee (LAC)** — Alaska Bar Association (mandatory). Education,
  confidential assistance, and referrals (vetted provider PDF). 907-272-7469 (bar office line);
  info@alaskabar.org. Cost/hours not stated. Sole dedicated body.

## Arizona (US-AZ)

- ✅ **Member Assistance Program (MAP)** — State Bar of Arizona (mandatory). Peer-support model
  (volunteer lawyers), confidential under AZ Supreme Court rule. 602-340-7334. NOT an outsourced
  EAP. Canonical /member-assistance-program/ URL 404s; live under
  /member-assistance-program-wellness-resource/.

## Arkansas (US-AR)

- ✅ **Judges and Lawyers Assistance Program (JLAP)** — Arkansas Supreme Court committee (`court`;
  the AR Bar is voluntary). Free, confidential (JLAP Rule 10). 501-907-2529;
  confidential@arjlap.org.

## Colorado (US-CO) — four-body ecosystem (no mandatory unified bar)

- ✅ **Colorado Lawyer Assistance Program (COLAP)** — Colorado Supreme Court (`court`). Free,
  privileged. 303-986-3345; info@coloradolap.org.
- ✅ **Colorado Lawyers Helping Lawyers (CLHL)** — court-approved nonprofit peer program
  (colhl.org). No own phone; routes to crisis lines.
- ✅ **The Waterman Fund** — Denver Bar charitable trust; financial aid for aged/infirm CO lawyers
  (10+ yrs practice). 303-824-5331.
- ⚠️ **Colorado Crisis Services** — the 24/7 line COLAP/CLHL refer to (1-844-493-8255); own page not
  loaded → corroborated.

## Connecticut (US-CT)

- ✅ **Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers — Connecticut, Inc. (LCL-CT)** — independent nonprofit
  (lclct.org), NOT affiliated with the bar/disciplinary bodies. Free, anonymous; protected under
  Conn. Gen. Stat. §51-81d(a). Hotline 800-497-1422; office 860-563-4900. 24/7 not stated.

## Delaware (US-DE)

- ✅ **Delaware Lawyers Assistance Program (DE-LAP)** — Delaware State Bar Association (voluntary).
  Confidential, free, non-disciplinary; committee relationship is attorney-client privileged.
  302-777-0124; delap@de-lap.org. Verified via DSBA-hosted page (de-lap.org served a mismatched TLS
  cert). Toll-free 877-243-3527 seen in search, unconfirmed.

## District of Columbia (US-DC)

- ✅ **D.C. Bar Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)** — D.C. Bar (mandatory), in-house. Short-term
  counseling (up to 12 sessions), peer mentors, monitoring. 202-347-3131; LAP@dcbar.org. Not an
  emergency service (→ 988).

## Florida (US-FL)

- ⚠️/✅ **Florida Lawyers Assistance, Inc. (FLA)** — independent nonprofit contracted by The Florida
  Bar (fla-lap.org). Confidential by contract + Bar Rule 3-7.1(j) / Fla. Stat. 397.482-486. Hotline
  800-282-8981; judges' 888-972-4040. Resource is primary-official ✅, but the hotline's 24/7 claim
  is only in secondary sources → phone availability `unspecified` (⚠️ on hours).
- ✅ **Florida Lawyers Helpline** — Florida Bar member benefit via vendor CuraLinc
  (floridabar.org/helpline). 24/7/365 stated verbatim; free, up to 5 sessions/yr. 1-833-351-9355.

## Georgia (US-GA)

- ✅ **Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)** — State Bar of Georgia (mandatory), via SupportLinc/CuraLinc.
  24/7 hotline 800-327-9631 (stated verbatim); six prepaid sessions/yr; teletherapy portal.
  **Currency:** CuraLinc is the current administrator, NOT the older CorpCare.
- ⚠️ **Lawyers Helping Lawyers (GA peer program)** — georgialhl.org site did not render →
  corroborated from the gabar.org page.

## Hawaii (US-HI)

- ✅ **Attorneys and Judges Assistance Program (AAP)** — Hawaii Supreme Court Rule 16 (`court`).
  Confidential/privileged (Rule 16.6). 808-531-2880; Neighbor Islands 1-800-273-8775. **Hours:**
  search snippets claim 24/7 but the loaded pages do not state it → `unspecified`.

## Idaho (US-ID)

- ✅ **Idaho Lawyer Assistance Program (LAP)** — Idaho State Bar (mandatory), clinical services by
  Southworth Associates. 24/7 confidential hotline 866-460-9014 (stated verbatim); coordinator Ben
  Seymour 208-949-0363. 100% confidential, not reported to the Bar.
