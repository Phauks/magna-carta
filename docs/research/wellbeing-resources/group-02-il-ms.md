## Illinois (US-IL)
- ✅ **Illinois Lawyers' Assistance Program (LAP)** — nonprofit (illinoislap.org). Confidential per
  IL Sup. Ct. Rule 1.6(d); free. 312-726-6607. Hours unspecified.

## Indiana (US-IN)
- ✅ **JLAP** — Indiana Supreme Court (`court`). Confidential under Adm. & Disc. Rule 31 §9. Free
  (external referrals at own cost). 317-833-0370 / 866-428-JLAP.

## Iowa (US-IA) — two resources; official sites blocked
- ⚠️ **Iowa LAP (ILAP)** — nonprofit; cert mismatch on iowalap.org → corroborated.
- ⚠️ **Esquire Assistance Program (EAP)** — court-provided license benefit via a third-party EAP
  vendor; 3 free sessions + 24/7 phone (1-800-EAP-IOWA). iowacourts.gov 403'd → corroborated.

## Kansas (US-KS) — two resources
- ✅ **KALAP** — Kansas Supreme Court (`court`), Rule 233. Free. Office 785-368-8275 (business hrs).
- ✅ **KALAP 24/7 EAP helpline** — outsourced vendor, 1-800-466-8282, verbatim 24/7.

## Kentucky (US-KY) — three resources
- ✅ **KYLAP** — Kentucky Bar Association. 24/7 direct line 502-226-9373 (verbatim). SCR 3.990.
- ✅ **KBA EAP** — partner vendor; up to 4 free clinical sessions, 24/7 via the KYLAP line.
- ⚠️ **KYLAP Foundation** — nonprofit forgivable-loan aid; own page not loaded → corroborated.

## Louisiana (US-LA)
- ✅ **JLAP** — nonprofit owned/funded by the LSBA (louisianajlap.com). Privileged under La. R.S.
  37:221. Free to call/consult; $50/mo only for formal monitoring, waivable. 866-354-9334.

## Maine (US-ME)
- ✅ **Maine Assistance Program (MAP)** — nonprofit (me-lap.org), est. 2002. Confidential under Me.
  Rev. Stat. Title 14 §164-A(3). Free. 207-266-5951.

## Maryland (US-MD)
- ✅ **MSBA Lawyer Assistance Program** — state bar. Free, confidential; financial assistance for
  treatment. 1-888-388-5459. Multi-state counselor network.

## Massachusetts (US-MA)
- ✅ **Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers (LCL)** — nonprofit (lclma.org), est. 1978; sole state LAP.
  Free; MA RPC 1.6(d). 617-482-9600. After-hours → 988.

## Michigan (US-MI)
- ✅ **Lawyers and Judges Assistance Program (LJAP)** — State Bar of Michigan. Confidential
  (HIPAA); free consultations + virtual support groups. 800-996-5522. (Michigan's page lists
  national 24/7 lines — those live in the national safety net, not as MI resources.)

## Minnesota (US-MN) — two resources
- ✅ **Lawyers Concerned for Lawyers (LCL)** — nonprofit (mnlcl.org), est. 1976; business-hours
  office line, partly Supreme Court-funded.
- ✅ **AllOne Health** — LCL's outsourced counseling vendor; the actual 24/7 line (800-451-1834,
  verbatim), up to 4 free sessions.

## Mississippi (US-MS)
- ⚠️ **Mississippi LJAP** — The Mississippi Bar; MS Supreme Court-authorized. 24-hour hotline
  800-593-9777. msbar.org 403'd + courts.ms.gov 500'd → corroborated.
