`@magna-carta/intake`: what a kind of matter must clear before it opens, as a firm-authored procedure bound to a matter type (or every type) holding ordered steps of eight kinds. A step's kind decides who answers it: the conflicts posture, the billing arrangement in force, an assembly of a named template, the matter's trust position, a custom field's value, or a member's own record. The checklist is derived on every read and stored nowhere, so a step cannot say done after the fact it stood on has changed and editing a procedure reaches every consultation of its kind at once. The readers are a registry the composition root supplies to both the checklist route and the opening gate, which is what lets a kind exist ahead of its subsystem: identity verification and acknowledgement are a member's word today. The matter shell gained an open-precondition hook mirroring its close hook; the gate refuses while a required step is outstanding and concludes nothing about the client; the override is a waiver under its own permission with a mandatory rationale, append-and-flip like every record here. Four permissions, twelve routes, an open-readiness read, Settings · Intake Procedures, the matter's Intake section, and Requirements To Open in the status window. The platform seeds no procedure.
